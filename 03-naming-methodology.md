# Naming Methodology & Current Decision

## Goal
Choose a **blank-slate brand name** for the product.

We intentionally avoided names that already carry strong existing meaning (e.g. Python, Tutor, Guru, Baat, Seekho, etc.).  
The name should act as an empty vessel so that *we* get to define what it means through product experience and marketing — similar to how “Google” or “Stripe” started with little prior association.

---

## Naming Principles We Followed

We applied standard professional naming criteria used by strong tech brands:

1. **Blank slate** – The name should not already trigger strong expectations, emotions, or category assumptions.
2. **Pronounceable** – Easy to say correctly on the first attempt.
3. **Spellable** – Someone should be able to type it after hearing it once.
4. **Short** – Preferably 1–2 syllables, maximum 3.
5. **Memorable** – Distinctive enough to stick.
6. **Ownable** – Reasonable chance of available domain + clean trademark path.
7. **Expandable** – Should not lock us into one subject (Python, Physics, etc.). The product may later cover many skills and domains.

---

## Process

1. Rejected descriptive and semi-descriptive names (CodeBaat, PyVaani, BolPy, GuruPy, etc.) because they already tell the user what the product is.
2. Generated a list of invented / highly abstract names.
3. Filtered for sound, length, and lack of strong prior meaning.
4. Shortlisted several candidates.
5. **Current primary choice: Zivon**
6. Final decision will also depend on actual domain availability and price.

---

## Current Primary Choice

### **Zivon**

**Why it was selected as the leading candidate:**
- Fully invented — almost no existing meaning for most people
- Short and easy to pronounce
- Distinctive sound
- Feels modern and brandable
- Leaves complete freedom for future expansion into any subject or skill domain

## Decision Rule Going Forward

We will check domain availability and pricing for the shortlisted names.

Priority order:
1. `.com` availability and reasonable price
2. Strong alternatives: `.ai`, `.app`, `.io`
3. Clean social handle availability (especially X and Instagram)

The final name will be the one that best balances:
- Brand quality
- Domain cost
- Long-term ownability
