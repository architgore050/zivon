Voice tutor: before we start

Before using voice: when you press the mic, your speech is sent to Gnani AI (Prisma STT & Timbre TTS) to transcribe and speak the tutor's physics explanation. Raw voice audio is never kept; short text transcripts expire after 7 days. Reading, simulations, and checks work completely without voice.

Explain-back adds: your transcript and the AI feedback are saved in your account until you delete them. The recording itself is never kept.

How it is judged

The app gives you the prompt and a count of its key ideas. After an attempt, the tutor model marks which ideas you covered from your transcript.
