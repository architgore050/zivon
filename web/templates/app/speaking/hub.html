How explain-back works
  • You speak for up to 45 seconds. The browser sends one short recording to Zivon.
  • Gnani Prisma transcribes it. The recording is dropped after transcription and is never stored as audio.
  • The tutor model marks which authored key ideas you covered. The app turns that into a band; it does not show a percentage or an exam mark.
  • Your transcript, the feedback, and your own note are kept in your account until you delete them. Deleting an attempt deletes the text.
  • Voice needs consent on this device. The first recording asks for it; reading and the rest of the app never require a microphone.

The AI feedback is practice guidance, not a teacher's mark. Privacy summary
