How it works

The rules before you start

  • No negative marking. A correct answer earns its marks; a wrong or missing answer earns zero.
  • Server-side marking. The key is never sent to the browser before you submit.
  • The clock is real. Time comes from when the paper started, not from the browser tab. Leaving and returning resumes the same attempt and clock.
  • Autosave. Answers save when you press Save, and again in the background when JavaScript is on. A failed save is shown, never hidden.
  • Mark for review. A marked question is flagged in the palette; it is not graded differently.
  • Review after submit. Score, accuracy, chapter and concept breakdown, every answer, and the explanation.
