How the placement works
  • Relative placement — chapters are compared with your own other chapters by two medians: performance (weighted accuracy) and coverage. Nobody else's data is involved.
  • Rated chapters — a chapter needs at least {{ swot.rules.rated_min_items }} scored items (practice answers, chapter tests, explain-backs) and a measurable coverage.
  • Recency — every answer is weighted with a {{ "%.0f"|format(swot.rules.half_life_days) }}-day half-life; recent work counts more.
  • Trend — the last {{ swot.rules.trend_window_days }} days against your own older answers, when there are at least {{ swot.rules.trend_min_recent }} recent items.
  • Exam weight (IMP) — a board-heavy chapter is ordered first inside its box and marked IMP. It never adds a fifth box.
  • Placement mode — {{ swot.mode }} (relative is the default; it compares your chapters with each other).

Rules version {{ swot.rules.version }}, formula {{ swot.rules.formula_version }}. Weakness means effort without results — not "you are bad". Threat is a watch-out — not a prediction. The full method is on the How this works page.
