How this works

Honest notes

  • The question bank is imported from the NCERT-backed assessment packs; every answer ships with an explanation and a concept tag.
  • Hints are not authored yet. The nudge button says so and offers a general study prompt, never the answer.
  • Retry is always allowed. Your progress record uses the first attempt on each question, so guess-until-right does not inflate it.
  • There is no timer and no public score. The summary is for you.
  • “Ask the tutor” opens the chapter's lesson page, where the real voice dock lives. Practice does not fake a second tutor.
  • Questions are in your saved language when the bank has it; otherwise this page says English is being used.
