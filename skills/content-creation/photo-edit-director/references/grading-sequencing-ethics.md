# Selection, sequencing, grading, ethics, review

## Selection (three passes)
Pass 1 technical rejects. Pass 2 narrative selects (unique job per image). Pass 3 final edit: hero image first,
then supporting images, then alternates. "No two images doing the same job."

## Sequencing a carousel or set
Opener (earns the swipe: a number, a result, a strong visual) > Context (one slide) > Proof (the real evidence) >
Decision (the tradeoff) > Honest gap (what is not done) > Close (one clear ask or next step).
Pairing: use juxtaposition (before/after, plan/reality, then/now) where it adds meaning. 5-8 slides is a
reasonable range for most posts; stop earlier if the story is done. Each slide must make sense alone at
thumbnail size, and the last slide should work as a standalone share.

## Grading
- Corrective first (white balance, exposure, straighten, crop), creative second (mood), and only if the image is
  not a proof/documentary image.
- One grade per set. Test it across the whole set before locking; a grade that fails on 10 other images is a filter.
- Work from the original (RAW if it exists); export sRGB JPEG or PNG for screens.
- Social feeds tolerate slightly higher contrast and saturation than print; do not clip highlights or crush shadows.
- Simple recipe language: white balance (warm/cool shift), exposure (+/- stops), contrast (S-curve mild/moderate),
  saturation/vibrance (small), sharpening (small). Avoid heavy filters and HDR looks.

## Retouching limits
- Documentary/proof images: crop, straighten, exposure, white balance, redact. Do not remove, add, or move content.
- Designed graphics: full creative freedom, but never imply a result that did not happen.
- People: remove temporary blemishes at most; preserve skin texture; the person should recognise themselves.
- Products: match the real colour and surface; do not hide defects that matter to the claim.
- AI edits (background removal, upscaling, generative fill) need a human check for edge artefacts, warped text,
  and invented detail. Do not use generative fill on proof images.

## Review protocol (every image or set)
1. Thumbnail test (about 300 px wide): subject and key text readable?
2. Squint test: does one thing dominate?
3. Grayscale test: does the hierarchy survive without colour?
4. Phone test: view on a phone at arm's length.
5. Secrets sweep on the EXPORTED file: keys, emails, URLs, faces/minors, metadata.
6. Critique in two parts, Composition then Edit. Lead each with what works, then what does not, naming actual
   regions, lines, colours, or tonal zones. Be honest; no filler praise. Finish with the single change that would
   improve the image most.
7. Verdict: SHIP / FIX (numbered, specific) / RESHOOT.

## Delivery
Name files dayNN_A1_vN.ext. Provide: final exports, the one-line alt text per image (describe what is shown,
including key numbers), and the caption suggestion if asked. Keep originals untouched.
