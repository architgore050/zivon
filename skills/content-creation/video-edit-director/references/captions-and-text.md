# Captions and on-screen text

- Always burn captions in. About 80-85 percent of feed video is watched muted (third-party figure, treat as
  directional). Auto-captions from the platform are unreliable and cannot be styled.
- Timing: word-level accuracy within about 100 ms; check at 0.5x speed in the first 5 s of the export. Words must
  not appear before they are spoken.
- Phrase size: 3-6 words per line, 2 lines max, one idea per caption block.
- Emphasis: highlight ONE keyword per phrase (a noun or verb carrying meaning). Candidates: numbers, outcomes,
  product names, contrast words ("but", "instead"), stakes words ("losing", "never"). Never highlight "the", "and",
  "like", "just", "really". Highlighting everything highlights nothing.
- Style: high contrast (light text with dark outline or shadow), one accent colour, one typeface family. No
  emojis unless it is the user's voice.
- Text cards: one line, under 7 words, readable in 1 s. Hook text appears in frame 1.
- Safe zones for vertical video: keep text and faces inside the central area; avoid roughly the top 14 percent and
  the bottom 20-35 percent, where platform UI overlays. Side margin at least 60 px on a 1080 px canvas. Verify
  against the platform's current UI.
- Proofread captions against the transcript. Product names and numbers must be exact.
- Language: if the audio switches language, bridge it with a text card or subtitle; do not leave a silent non-sequitur.
- Deliver the .srt (or .vtt) alongside the video.
