# PL judge calibration corpus (2026-11-21)

Seeded-defect corpus for the three personalized-learning judges
(EV-PL-001 pedagogy, EV-PL-002 faithfulness, EV-PL-004 adversarial).
17 samples: 10 positive defects (synthetic, planted mutations documented in
`planted_mutation`) + 7 clean samples (3 of them reference the recorded clean
golden transcripts by path; no golden is duplicated or edited). Student text
is synthetic; no answer key beyond the recorded `check_keys` shape.

Expected outcomes live in each sample's `expected` block. Generated by
`python scripts/gen_pl_judge_corpus.py` (deterministic bytes).
