Six voice docks, one conversation

Every frame runs the identical six-turn Hinglish doubt — “Sir, impulse aur change in momentum same kyun hote hain?” — over the real generated Laws of Motion page, with block b-13 (the batsman worked example) ringed in pen-blue. Hold the mic (or press space) to speak; Esc stops the tutor; the little MOCK · state button forces any of the 18 client states.

Inert fakes: no WebSocket, no microphone, no network, no fonts from a CDN. Judge the shape and the streamed caption, not the content.
