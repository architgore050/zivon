Laws of Motion

Newton's second law says the rate of change of momentum is proportional to the applied force. Push a wall and it pushes back — that is the third law.

When the force acts for a short time, the product of force and time is called impulse. A cricket bat stays in contact with the ball for a tiny fraction of a second, but the force is large.

WORKED EXAMPLE · B-13

A bat applies 400 N for 0.15 s. Impulse = 400 × 0.15 = 60 N·s. That is also the change in the ball's momentum.
