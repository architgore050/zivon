About · Build in public

A Physics page that answers where you are reading.

Zivon exists because the gap is rarely “there is no explanation”. It is that the explanation is not for the exact sentence the student is stuck on.

Started 2026 · Built by Archit · Physics, Class 9–12

Why Physics, and why a page.

Physics punishes a half-understood sentence. A student can nod through a paragraph on net force, then meet a numerical and discover the idea never landed. A video can explain the general case; it cannot see which sentence the student is actually on.

A page can. It has a position, a paragraph, a diagram and a check. If the tutor knows that position, it can answer the student's version of the question and point back at the evidence. That is the whole bet.

What is built.

Live lessons are generated from NCERT chapters, reviewed against their source packs, translated into English, Hindi and Hinglish, and published as static pages. The catalogue lists every published chapter. Class pages exist for all four classes; a class with no published chapter says so.

Reading, diagrams, simulations and checks work without an account. The voice tutor is the add-on: speech in, a page-anchored answer, speech out. It is being built; the status page names exactly what works today.

The stack, plainly.

  • Voice: Gnani Prisma for speech-to-text and Timbre for text-to-speech, chosen for Indian languages and code-mixed speech.
  • Tutor: Gemma 4 via the Gemini API, anchored to the lesson page.
  • Content factory: an internal generation pipeline with separate review gates for physics, code, accessibility and performance.
  • Site: static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript. No framework, no CDN, no third-party fonts, no trackers.

The stack is a current choice, not a permanent identity. If it changes, the change gets recorded.

Where it came from.

Zivon began as a build for the Great Indian AI Internship Challenge by Gnani AI in 2026. The challenge deadline is gone; the product is now on a startup track, measured by whether a student can open a page, speak a doubt, and hear a useful answer on a low-end phone.

The build-in-public record is part of the product, not marketing: what shipped, what broke, and what is still missing. The source is on every lesson page for the same reason.

What is still missing.

  • Accounts and cross-device progress.
  • Five-question practice, chapter tests and test series.
  • A consented student recording to replace the sample transcript on the landing page.
  • Class 10 and Class 12 lessons.
  • A monitored public support mailbox. Until it exists, the repository issue tracker is the contact channel.
