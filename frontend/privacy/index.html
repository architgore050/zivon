Privacy & data summary

Private by default. No accounts yet, no raw audio kept, and you can erase everything from this device anytime.

Consent, in one line.

Consent status: no consent record on this device.

The voice tutor is not part of the public lesson page yet. When it is, the microphone stays off until you grant consent on this device; declining leaves reading, simulations and checks usable.

Accounts do not exist yet. When they arrive, they will be optional, private by default, and this table will name what changes before anyone signs up.

Clear this device's data.

This clears local preferences, consent and the device pseudonym, and asks the server to delete any transcript tied to that pseudonym. If the server cannot be reached, the page says so and keeps the local data so you can retry; it never claims a deletion that did not happen.

Erase all Zivon data for this device?

This clears your local preferences, consent record, and requests immediate server-side deletion of any session or transcript records tied to this device pseudonym.

Questions or a correction.

Use the contact page. Do not send a child's name, phone number, school, or a screenshot with personal details. A monitored privacy mailbox is not published yet; the public issue tracker is the contact channel for now.
