Contact

Tell us what broke.

A wrong equation, a broken link, a simulation that will not move, a check that marks a correct answer wrong. Those reports are how the pages get better.

There is no contact form yet. This page will not show a form that cannot send.

Report a bug or a content error.

Open an issue on the public repository at github.com/architgore050/zivon/issues. The tracker is the monitored channel until a support mailbox is published.

Include:

  • the page URL and the block, diagram or check number if you can see one;
  • what you expected and what happened;
  • the phone or browser, and whether JavaScript was on;
  • a screenshot only if it contains no personal information.

Please do not include a student's name, phone number, school, or home address. The report does not need any of them.

Privacy or data request.

The privacy page clears local preferences, consent and the device pseudonym, and asks the server to delete any transcript tied to that pseudonym. Use it first; it is the fastest path and the one that does not require sending anyone your data.

A monitored privacy mailbox is not published yet. Until it is, use the issue tracker and say only “privacy request”; do not paste personal data into an issue.

Rights holders and takedowns.

No third-party exam-paper content is published on Zivon today. Before any exam-pattern content ships, this page will carry the approved takedown notice and a monitored email address. If you believe a lesson reproduces your work, open an issue with the page URL and your relationship to the work; we will review it.

For schools and teachers.

A schools page and a schools waitlist are planned, not open. If you want to use a live chapter with a class, open an issue with the class and chapter; there is no teacher dashboard yet.

What we will not ask for.

  • Your name, phone number, school, or address.
  • A password or a one-time code.
  • Payment details. There is no payment integration.
  • Voice recordings. The product does not keep raw audio.

This page is static. It does not send anything by itself; it sends you to a channel that is actually monitored.
