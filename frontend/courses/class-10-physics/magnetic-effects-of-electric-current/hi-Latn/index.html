Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap magnet ke magnetic field ko describe kar sakte hain aur magnetic field lines ke properties list kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap right-hand thumb rule use karke straight wire, circular loop aur solenoid mein current se produce hone wale magnetic field ki direction find kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap Fleming's left-hand rule use karke magnetic field mein rakhe current-carrying conductor par lagne wale force ki direction find kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap domestic circuit ke live, neutral aur earth wires explain kar sakte hain, aur kyun electric fuse circuit ko short-circuiting aur overloading se protect karta hai.

Aap electric current ka ek effect already jaante hain: yeh jis wire se flow karta hai use heat karta hai. Ek doosra effect bhi hai. Current carry karne wali wire bhi magnet ki tarah behave karti hai, aur aap ise compass se dekh sakte hain.

Ek thick straight copper wire lein aur use electric circuit ke do points X aur Y ke beech connect karein. Wire ko paper ke plane ke perpendicular rakhein. Wire ke paas ek chhota compass horizontally rakhein aur uski needle ki position note karein.

Ab key insert karein taaki current flow ho, aur needle ko phir dekhein. Needle deflected hai. Copper wire ke through electric current ne magnetic effect produce kiya hai. Electricity aur magnetism ek doosre se linked hain.

Yeh link accidentally mila tha. 1820 mein, Hans Christian Oersted ne discover kiya ki jab ek metallic wire ke paas se electric current pass hota hai to compass needle deflect ho jaati hai. Unki observation ne dikhaya ki electricity aur magnetism related phenomena the.

Jab compass needle ko bar magnet ke paas laaya jaata hai, to woh deflect kyun ho jaati hai?

Kyon?

Compass needle ek chhota bar magnet hota hai. Bar magnet apne aas-paas magnetic field banata hai, aur us field ke andar doosre magnet par force act karta hai, isliye needle turn (ghoom) jaati hai.

Compass needle ek chhota bar magnet hota hai. Iske do sire lagbhag north aur south directions ki taraf point karte hain. Jo end north ki taraf point karta hai use north-seeking ya north pole kehte hain; doosra end, jo south ki taraf point karta hai, use south-seeking ya south pole kehte hain. Like poles ek doosre ko repel karte hain, aur unlike poles ek doosre ko attract karte hain.

Drawing board par white paper ki ek sheet fix karo. Centre mein ek bar magnet rakho aur uske chaaron taraf iron filings uniformly sprinkle karo. Board ko halke se tap karo. Iron filings ek pattern mein arrange ho jaati hain.

Ye pattern mein kyun arrange hoti hain? Magnet apne aas-paas ke region mein influence daalta hai, aur filings force (bal) experience karti hain. Magnet ke aas-paas ka woh region jismein magnet ki force detect ki ja sakti hai, usme magnetic field hota hai. Jin lines ke along iron filings align hoti hain, woh magnetic field lines represent karti hain.

Ab chaar properties follow hoti hain. Pehli, kisi point par magnetic field ki direction wahi hoti hai jis direction mein compass needle ka north pole wahan move karta. Convention ke hisaab se, field lines north pole se bahar nikalti hain aur south pole par milti hain, aur magnet ke andar unki direction south pole se north pole ki taraf hoti hai, isliye field lines closed curves hoti hain.

Doosri, field ki relative strength lines ke closeness ki degree se dikhti hai: jahan lines crowded hoti hain wahan field stronger hota hai. Teesri, koi bhi do field lines ek doosre ko cross nahi karti, kyunki intersection point par compass needle do directions ki taraf point karegi, jo possible nahi hai.

Chauthi, needle ka deflection badhta hai jab use field line ke along poles ki taraf move kiya jata hai.

Compass se ek magnetic field line kaise draw karein

Setup karein

Drawing board par white paper ki ek sheet fix karein, us par bar magnet rakhein aur magnet ki boundary mark karein.

Pehli position

Compass ko magnet ke north pole ke paas rakhein. Needle ka south pole magnet ke north pole ki taraf point karta hai, aur compass ka north pole magnet ke north pole se door ki direction mein hota hai. Needle ke dono ends ki positions mark karein.

Aage badhein

Needle ko nayi position par move karein aise ki uska south pole wahi position le le jo pehle uske north pole ne li thi.

South pole tak continue karein

Isi tarah step by step aage badhein jab tak magnet ke south pole tak na pahunch jayein.

Marks ko join karein

Paper par mark kiye gaye points ko ek smooth curve se join karein. Yeh curve ek field line represent karti hai.

Repeat karein

Poora procedure repeat karein aur jitni lines draw kar sakte hain utni lines draw karein. Saath milkar yeh magnet ke around magnetic field represent karti hain.

Do magnetic field lines ek dusre ko intersect kyun nahi karti?

Kyon?

Kisi point par field ki direction wahi hoti hai jis direction mein compass needle ka north pole wahan move karega. Ek single point do directions nahi de sakta, isliye do field lines cross nahi kar sakti.

Is chapter mein magnetic field produce karne ke kaun se do methods dikhaye gaye hain?

Kyon?

Magnetic field magnet ke aas-paas ke region mein hota hai, aur electric current carry karne wale metallic wire ke saath bhi ek magnetic field associated hota hai.

Current ke through conductor mein generate hone wale magnetic field ka pattern kya decide karta hai? Pattern conductor ki shape par depend karta hai. Long straight wire se start karte hain.

Ek long straight copper wire, 1.5 V ke do ya teen cells aur ek plug key lein, aur unhe series mein connect karein. Straight wire ko compass needle ke parallel aur uske upar rakhein, phir key plug karein. Agar current north se south ki taraf flow karta hai, to compass needle ka north pole east ki taraf move karta hai. Ab cell connections ko replace karein taki current south se north ki taraf flow kare; needle west ki taraf move karta hai. Current reverse hone par magnetic field ki direction reverse ho jati hai.

Same experiment se do aur facts milte hain. Agar aap wire mein current badhate hain, to needle ka deflection badh jata hai; iska matlab hai ki kisi diye gaye point par magnetic field ka magnitude badh jata hai jab wire mein current badhta hai.

Agar aap current same rakhte hain lekin compass ko wire se door le jaate hain, to deflection kam ho jata hai; iska matlab hai ki diye gaye current se produce hone wala field kam ho jata hai jab wire se distance (doori) badhta hai. Field ko represent karne wale concentric circles aur bade hote jaate hain jab hum wire se door jaate hain.

Nimnlikhit mein se kaun sa sahi tarah se ek lambi seedhi current-carrying wire ke paas magnetic field (chumbakiya kshetra) ka varnan karta hai?

Kyon?

Ek seedhi current-carrying wire ke aas-paas chhidke hue iron filings wire ke chaaron taraf concentric circles mein vyavasthit ho jate hain, aur yeh circles field lines ko darshate hain.

Yahan ek aasan tarika hai yaad rakhne ka ki ek seedhi current-carrying conductor ke around field ki direction kya hoti hai. Socho ki aap conductor ko apne right hand mein is tarah pakde hue hain ki aapka thumb current ki direction ki taraf point karta hai. Phir aapki fingers conductor ke around field lines ki direction mein lapet jaati hain. Yahi right-hand thumb rule hai.

Isi rule ko Maxwell's corkscrew rule bhi kaha jata hai: agar aap maan lein ki aap current ki direction mein ek corkscrew drive kar rahe hain, to corkscrew ke rotation ki direction hi magnetic field (chumbakiya kshetra) ki direction hoti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek horizontal power line mein current east se west direction mein flow karta hai. Iske exactly neeche ek point aur exactly upar ek point par magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

  1. Thumb ko point karo Power line ko apne right hand mein is tarah pakdo ki aapka thumb current ke along point kare, yani east se west.
  2. Fingers ko curl karo Aapki fingers wire ke around wrap ho jati hain. Ye wire ke perpendicular plane mein field lines ki direction batati hain.
  3. Ek end se dekho East end se dekhein to ye curling clockwise hai; west end se dekhein to anti-clockwise.
  4. Upar aur neeche same Wire ke exactly neeche ya exactly upar kisi point par kuch nahi badalta: east end se dekhein to field abhi bhi wire ke perpendicular plane mein clockwise turn karta hai, aur west end se dekhein to anti-clockwise.

Uttar (Answer) Wire ke exactly neeche ya exactly upar kisi bhi point par, east end se dekhein to magnetic field wire ke perpendicular plane mein clockwise turn karta hai, aur west end se dekhein to anti-clockwise.

Kaun sa rule straight current-carrying conductor ke around produce hone wale magnetic field ki direction batata hai?

Kyon?

Current-carrying straight conductor ko apne right hand mein is tarah pakdo ki thumb current ki direction mein point kare. Phir aapki fingers conductor ke around magnetic field lines ki direction mein wrap hoti hain.

Ab us straight wire ko circular loop mein bend karo aur usme current pass karo. Ek straight wire ka magnetic field usse distance (doori) par inversely depend karta hai; isi tarah, current-carrying circular loop ke har point par field ko represent karne wale concentric circles wire se door jane par aur bade hote jaate hain.

Jab hum loop ke centre tak pahunchte hain, in bade circles ke arcs straight lines jaise dikhte hain. Current-carrying wire ka har point aisa magnetic field banata hai jo loop ke centre par straight line jaise dikhta hai. Right-hand rule apply karne se pata chalta hai ki wire ka har section loop ke andar magnetic field lines ko same direction mein contribute karta hai.

Kisi given point par current-carrying wire se produce hone wala magnetic field usme pass hone wale current par directly depend karta hai. Isliye agar ek circular coil mein n turns hain, to produce hone wala field single turn se produce hone wale field ka n times hota hai: har circular turn mein current ki direction same hoti hai, aur turns ke due to fields simply add up ho jaate hain.

Solenoid ek coil hoti hai jisme cylinder ke shape mein wound kai circular turns hote hain. Current-carrying solenoid ke through aur uske around magnetic field lines ka pattern bar magnet jaisa hota hai, aur solenoid ka ek end magnetic north pole ki tarah act karta hai. Current carry karte hue ek long straight solenoid ke andar, magnetic field sabhi points par same hota hai.

Ek current-carrying solenoid coil ka use uske andar rakhi steel rod ko magnetise karne ke liye hota hai. Yeh arrangement ek electromagnet hota hai. Electromagnet mein soft iron ka core hota hai jiske around insulated copper wire ki coil wrapped hoti hai.

Sahi option chuniye. Current carry karte hue ek lambe straight solenoid ke andar magnetic field

Kyon?

Current carry karte hue ek lambe straight solenoid ke andar field lines parallel hoti hain aur field uniform hota hai, isliye iske andar sab points par field same hota hai.

Bataiye ki nimnlikhit statement true hai ya false: Ek lambi circular coil jisme current flow ho raha hai, uske centre par field parallel straight lines hogi.

Kyon?

Current-carrying circular coil ke centre par bade circles ke arcs straight lines jaise dikhte hain, aur wire ka har section wahan same direction me contribute karta hai, isliye centre par field lines parallel straight lines hoti hain.

Ek circular loop of wire table ke plane mein rakha hai aur usme current clockwise flow ho raha hai, jaise upar se dekha gaya ho. Right-hand rule apply karte hue, loop ke centre par magnetic field kis direction mein hoga?

Kyon?

Apne right hand ki fingers ko current ki direction mein curl karein, yaani clockwise; phir aapka thumb table ke andar point karega. Loop ke andar wire ka har section apna field usi same direction mein contribute karta hai.

Ek current-carrying conductor magnetic field paida karta hai, aur ye field conductor ke paas rakhe magnet par force (bal) lagata hai. French scientist Andre Marie Ampere ne suggest kiya ki magnet bhi current-carrying conductor par barabar aur opposite force lagata hoga.

Ek chhota aluminium rod AB lagbhag 5 cm ka lein aur use stand se do connecting wires ki madad se horizontally latka dein. Ek strong horse-shoe magnet aise rakhein ki rod uske do poles ke beech mein ho aur magnetic field upwards directed ho: north pole vertically rod ke neeche aur south pole vertically rod ke upar. Rod ko battery, key aur rheostat ke saath series mein connect karein, aur rod mein current end B se end A ki taraf pass karein. Rod left ki taraf displace hota hai. Current ki direction reverse karein to rod right ki taraf displace hota hai.

Ab magnet ke do poles interchange karke field ki direction vertically downwards kar dein. Current-carrying rod par lagne wale force ki direction phir se reverse ho jati hai. Isliye force ki direction current ki direction aur magnetic field ki direction par depend karti hai.

Rod ka displacement (visthapan) sabse zyada hota hai, yani force (bal) ka magnitude sabse high hota hai, jab current ki direction Magnetic field ki direction ke right angles par hoti hai. Aisi condition mein ek simple rule force ki direction deta hai.

Fleming's left-hand rule: apne left hand ke thumb, forefinger aur middle finger ko aise stretch karo ki wo mutually perpendicular hon. Agar first finger Magnetic field ki direction mein point karta hai aur second finger current ki direction mein, to thumb motion (gati) ki direction ya conductor par lagne wale force ki direction mein point karta hai.

Yaad rakho ki current ki direction electrons ki motion (gati) ki direction ke opposite li jati hai. Jin devices mein current-carrying conductors aur Magnetic fields use hote hain unme electric motor, electric generator, loudspeakers, microphones aur measuring instruments shamil hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek electron horizontally east ki taraf move karta hai aur ek Magnetic field mein enter karta hai jo vertically upwards point karta hai, uski motion (gati) ke right angles par. Electron par act karne wala force (bal) kis direction mein point karta hai?

  1. Field ki direction fix karein Magnetic field vertically upwards point karta hai, isliye left hand ki forefinger upar ki taraf point karti hai.
  2. Current ki direction dhoondein Electron east ki taraf move karta hai. Current ki direction electrons ki motion ki direction ke opposite li jaati hai, isliye current west ki taraf hai. Second finger west ki taraf point karti hai.
  3. Thumb se direction padhein Forefinger upar aur second finger west ki taraf point karti hui ho, to left hand ka thumb north ki taraf point karta hai.
  4. Result bataayein Force field aur current dono ke perpendicular act karta hai, isliye ye horizontally north ki taraf directed hai.

Uttar (Answer) Electron par lagne wala force north ki taraf act karta hai.

Ek positively charged alpha-particle west ki taraf project kiya gaya hai aur magnetic field ke karan north ki taraf deflect ho jata hai. Magnetic field ki direction hai:

Kyon?

Alpha-particle positively charged hai, isliye current west ki taraf hai. Fleming's left-hand rule ka use karte hue, thumb north (force (bal)) ki taraf point karega aur second finger west (current) ki taraf; forefinger phir upward point karega, jo field ki direction hai.

Ek electron horizontally east ki taraf move karta hai. Electric current kis direction mein liya jata hai?

Kyon?

Current ki direction electrons ke motion (gati) ki direction ke opposite li jati hai. Kyunki electron east ki taraf move karta hai, current west ki taraf liya jata hai.

Aap ek chamber mein baithhe hain aur aapki peeth ek deewar ki taraf hai. Ek electron beam jo back wall se front wall ki taraf horizontally move kar raha hai, ek strong magnetic field ke karan aapke right side ki taraf deflect ho jata hai. Magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Electrons front wall ki taraf move karte hain, isliye current ko opposite direction mein liya jata hai, yaani back wall ki taraf. Force aapke right side ki taraf hai. Fleming's left-hand rule ke saath, thumb right ki taraf point karta hai aur second finger back wall ki taraf point karta hai; phir forefinger vertically neeche ki taraf point karta hai.

Jab current-carrying conductor ko magnetic field mein rakha jata hai, to us par lagne wala force (bal) kab sabse zyada hota hai?

Kyon?

Activity mein rod ka displacement (visthapan), aur isliye force ka magnitude, sabse zyada tab hota hai jab current ki direction magnetic field ki direction ke right angles par hoti hai.

Activity 12.7 mein, current-carrying rod par lagne wala force (bal) inmein se kis par depend karta hai?

Kyon?

Current reverse karne se displacement (visthapan) ki direction reverse ho jati hai, aur poles interchange karke magnetic field ki direction badalne se bhi force reverse ho jata hai. Isliye force ki direction current ki direction aur field ki direction dono par depend karti hai.

Electric power ghar tak main supply ke through pahunchti hai, chahe woh overhead electric poles se support ho ya underground cables se. Is supply ka ek wire, aam taur par red insulation ke saath, live wire (ya positive) kahlata hai. Dusra wire, black insulation ke saath, neutral wire (ya negative) kahlata hai. Hamare desh mein dono ke beech ka potential difference 220 V hai.

Ghar ke meter-board par yeh wires main fuse ke through electricity meter mein jaate hain, aur main switch ke through ghar ki line wires se connect hote hain. Aksar do alag circuits use hote hain: ek 15 A current rating wala higher power rating wale appliances jaise geysers aur air coolers ke liye, aur dusra 5 A current rating wala bulbs aur fans ke liye.

Earth wire ka insulation green colour ka hota hai aur ise aam taur par ghar ke paas zameen mein gehri metal plate se connect kiya jata hai. Yeh ek safety measure hai, khaas kar un appliances ke liye jinke metallic body hote hain, jaise electric press, toaster, table fan ya refrigerator. Metallic body ko earth wire se connect kiya jata hai, jo current ke liye low-resistance conducting path pradan karta hai. Phir metallic body mein current ka koi bhi leakage uska potential zameen ke barabar rakhta hai, aur user ko severe electric shock nahi lag sakta.

Har alag circuit mein, different appliances ko live aur neutral wires ke across connect kiya ja sakta hai, aur har appliance ka apna separate switch hota hai jo current ko on aur off karta hai. Isliye ki har appliance ko equal potential difference mile, appliances ko ek dusre ke parallel connect kiya jata hai.

Fuse sabhi domestic circuits ka ek important component hai. Yeh overloading ke karan appliances aur circuit ko damage hone se rokta hai. Overloading tab ho sakta hai jab live wire aur neutral wire direct contact mein aa jayein; aisa tab hota hai jab wires ki insulation damaged ho ya appliance mein fault ho. Aisi situation mein circuit ka current abruptly badh jata hai, aur ise short-circuiting kehte hain. Fuse mein hone wali Joule heating use melt kar deti hai, jisse electric circuit toot jata hai aur unduly high current ka flow ruk jata hai.

Overloading supply voltage mein accidental hike ke karan bhi ho sakta hai, aur kabhi-kabhi ek hi socket se bahut zyada appliances connect karne se hota hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek domestic circuit mein, ek wire ki insulation damage ho jaati hai aur live wire seedha neutral wire ke contact mein aa jaati hai. Explain kijiye ki circuit mein kya hota hai aur fuse kya karta hai.

  1. Direct contact Live wire aur neutral wire seedha contact mein aa jaate hain. Isse short-circuiting kehte hain.
  2. Current increases Is direct contact ki wajah se circuit mein current achaanak badh jaata hai.
  3. Fuse heats Bahut zyada high current fuse mein Joule heating paida karta hai.
  4. Circuit breaks Joule heating se fuse melt ho jaata hai, jisse electric circuit toot jaata hai aur bahut zyada high current ka flow ruk jaata hai; isse appliances aur circuit ko damage hone se bachaaya jaata hai.

Uttar (Answer) Damaged insulation short-circuiting ka kaaran banta hai: live aur neutral wires ek dusre ko touch karte hain, current achaanak badh jaata hai, aur Joule heating fuse ko melt kar deta hai, jisse circuit toot jaata hai aur damage se bachaav hota hai.

Short circuit ke time, circuit mein current

Kyon?

Jab live wire aur neutral wire seedha contact mein aa jaate hain, to circuit mein current achanak badh jaata hai; ise short-circuiting kehte hain.

Electric short circuit kab hota hai?

Kyon?

Yeh tab hota hai jab wires ki insulation damaged ho jati hai ya appliance mein fault hota hai, aur phir circuit mein current achanak badh jata hai.

Earth wire ka function kya hai, aur metallic appliances ko earth karna kyon zaroori hai?

Kyon?

Earth wire ko earth ke andar gehri metal plate se connect kiya jata hai. Jab appliance ki metallic body isse jod di jati hai, to leakage current user ke through jaane ke bajaye safely earth mein chala jata hai.

Bataiye ki nimnlikhit kathan sahi hai ya galat: green insulation wala wire aam taur par electric supply ka live wire hota hai.

Kyon?

Green insulation wala wire Earth wire hota hai. Live wire par aam taur par red insulation hoti hai aur neutral wire par black insulation hoti hai.

Electric circuits aur appliances mein aam taur par use hone wale do safety measures ke naam bataiye.

Kyon?

Jab current bahut zyada high hota hai to fuse pighal kar circuit tod deta hai, aur earth wire leakage current ko safely earth tak le jaata hai.

Domestic electric circuits ke overloading se bachne ke liye kya precaution lena chahiye?

Kyon?

Overloading tab ho sakti hai jab ek hi socket par bahut zyada appliances connect kiye jaate hain, aur supply voltage mein accidental increase se bhi; agar aisa hota hai to circuit break karne ke liye fuse hota hai.

Electric current hamesha magnetic field produce karta hai. Hamare body ki nerve cells ke saath travel karne wale weak ion currents bhi magnetic field produce karte hain. Jab aap kisi cheez ko touch karte hain, to aapki nerves aapko use karne wale muscles tak electric impulse pahunchati hain, aur ye impulse ek temporary magnetic field produce karta hai. Aise fields bahut weak hote hain, lagbhag earth ke magnetic field ka one-billionth.

Human body ke andar jin do main organs mein produce hone wala magnetic field significant hota hai, wo heart aur brain hain. Body ke andar ka magnetic field Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) naam ki technique se different body parts ke images obtain karne ka basis banata hai, aur in images ka analysis medical diagnosis mein madad karta hai.

Kaunsi technique human body ke andar ke magnetic field ka use karke different body parts ki images obtain karti hai?

Kyon?

Body ke andar ka magnetic field, jo heart aur brain mein significant hota hai, MRI dwara body parts ki images obtain karne ka basis hai, aur in images ka analysis medical diagnosis mein help karta hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

Magnetic field
Magnet ke around ka woh region jahan magnet ka force detect kiya ja sakta hai.
Magnetic field line
Woh line jiske along iron filings magnet ke around line up hote hain; us par rakhi compass needle uski direction ke along point karti hai.
Right-hand thumb rule
Straight current-carrying wire ke around field ki direction ke liye rule: thumb current ke along, curled fingers field ke along.
Fleming's left-hand rule
Current-carrying conductor par force ki direction ke liye rule: forefinger field ke along, second finger current ke along, thumb force ke along.
Solenoid
Cylinder ke shape mein wound many circular turns ki coil, jiska field bar magnet jaisa hota hai.
Electromagnet
Soft iron core jiske around insulated copper wire ki coil wrapped hoti hai, aur coil se current pass karke use magnetic banaya jaata hai.
Electric fuse
Ek safety device jo bahut zyada current flow hone par melt ho jaata hai aur circuit break kar deta hai, appliances ko damage se bachata hai.
Short-circuiting
Ek fault jisme live wire aur neutral wire directly touch ho jaate hain, isliye circuit ka current abruptly badh jaata hai.
Earth wire
Green-insulated safety wire jo earth mein metal plate se connected hoti hai, jo metallic appliance body ko earth ke potential par rakhti hai.
