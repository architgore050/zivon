Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap magnet ke magnetic field aur uski field lines describe kar sakte hain, aur field lines ke properties list kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap right-hand thumb rule ka use karke straight wire, circular loop aur solenoid mein current se produce hone wale magnetic field ki direction find kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap Fleming's left-hand rule ka use karke magnetic field mein rakhe current-carrying conductor par force ki direction find kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap domestic electric circuit ke wires, safety devices aur common faults describe kar sakte hain.

Wire mein current wire ko garam kar sakta hai, lekin yah kuch aur bhi kar sakta hai. Electric current le jane wala wire magnet ki tarah behave karta hai.

Ek circuit ke do points X aur Y ke beech ek seedha mota copper wire connect karo, aur wire ko paper ke plane ke perpendicular rakho. Wire ke paas ek chhota compass horizontally rakho, uski needle ki position note karo, phir key insert karo aur current pass karo. Needle deflect ho jati hai.

Deflection ka matlab hai ki copper wire se guzarne wale electric current ne magnetic effect produce kiya hai. Isliye electricity aur magnetism ek dusre se linked hain.

19th century ke leading scientists mein se ek, Hans Christian Oersted ne yah 1820 mein find kiya. Unhone accidentally discover kiya ki jab paas rakhe metallic wire se electric current pass hota tha to compass needle deflect ho jati thi, aur is observation ke through unhone dikhaya ki electricity aur magnetism related phenomena the. Magnetic field (chumbakiya kshetra) strength ki unit ko unke samman mein oersted naam diya gaya hai.

Ek seedhi copper wire ke paas rakhi compass needle tab deflect hoti hai jab key insert ki jati hai aur current flow karta hai. Yah kya dikhata hai?

Kyon?

Needle sirf tabhi deflect hoti hai jab current flow karta hai, isliye current khud wire ke around magnetic field paida kar raha hai. Isi liye electricity aur magnetism ko linked kaha jata hai.

Jab compass needle ko bar magnet ke paas laya jata hai to woh deflect ho jati hai. Compass needle asal mein ek chhota bar magnet hi hoti hai: iske ends lagbhag north aur south ki taraf point karte hain. North ki taraf point karne wala end north-seeking ya north pole kehlata hai, aur doosra end, jo south ki taraf point karta hai, south-seeking ya south pole kehlata hai.

Ek drawing board par white paper ki sheet fix karo aur centre mein ek bar magnet rakho. Magnet ke chaaron taraf iron filings ko uniformly sprinkle karo, chahe to salt-sprinkler use karo, aur board ko halke se tap karo. Filings ek pattern mein arrange ho jati hain, kyunki magnet apne aas-paas ke region mein apna influence dalta hai aur filings force (bal) ka anubhav karte hain. Magnet ke aas-paas ka woh region, jismein magnet ki force detect ki ja sakti hai, usme magnetic field hota hai. Jin lines ke along iron filings align hoti hain, woh magnetic field lines ko represent karti hain.

Aap chhote compass aur bar magnet ki madad se field lines khud draw kar sakte hain. Magnet ko drawing board par lagi paper sheet par rakhiye aur magnet ki boundary mark kijiye. Compass ko north pole ke paas rakhiye: needle ka south pole magnet ke north pole ki taraf point karta hai, aur compass ka north pole us se door ki taraf directed hota hai.

Needle ke dono ends ki position mark kijiye. Ab compass ko nayi position par move kijiye aise ki uska south pole pehle uske north pole se occupied position par aa jaye. Step by step aage badhiye jab tak magnet ke south pole tak na pahunch jaiye. Paper par mark kiye gaye points ko ek smooth curve se join kijiye: yeh curve ek field line ko represent karta hai. Procedure repeat kijiye aur jitni lines draw kar sakte hain kijiye; jo pattern aapko milta hai woh magnet ke around ka magnetic field hai. Jab aap needle ko field line ke along poles ki taraf move karte hain, to deflection badh jata hai.

Magnetic field ek aisi quantity hai jisme direction aur magnitude dono hote hain. Kisi point par magnetic field ki direction woh hoti hai jis direction mein compass needle ka north pole us field ke andar move karta hai. Isliye convention se, field lines north pole se emerge hoti hain aur south pole par merge hoti hain. Magnet ke andar field lines ki direction south pole se north pole ki taraf hoti hai. Is tarah magnetic field lines closed curves hoti hain.

Magnetic field ki relative strength field lines ke paas-paas hone se dikhti hai. Jahan field lines zyada crowded hoti hain, wahan field stronger hota hai, yani wahan rakhe dusre magnet ke pole par lagne wala force (bal) zyada hota hai.

Do field lines kabhi ek dusre ko cross nahi karti. Agar ve cross karti, to iska matlab hota ki intersection point par compass needle do directions mein point karegi, jo possible nahi hai.

Humare bodies mein bhi weak magnetic fields hoti hain. Nerve cells ke along ion currents temporary fields produce karte hain jo earth ke magnetic field ka lagbhag one-billionth hote hain. Heart aur brain significant fields produce karte hain, aur body ke andar ka magnetic field body parts ki images obtain karne ka basis hai, jise Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) technique kehte hain.

Jab compass needle ko bar magnet ke paas laate hain to woh kyun deflected hoti hai?

Kyon?

Compass needle khud ek chhota bar magnet hoti hai. Bar magnet ke magnetic field ke andar uspar force lagta hai, isliye woh ghoom jaati hai aur uske ends field lines ke along point karte hain.

Nimnlikhit mein se kaun si magnetic field lines ki property NAHI hai?

Kyon?

Koi bhi do field lines ek doosre ko cross karti hui nahi milti. Agar aisa hota, to intersection point par compass needle ek hi samay mein do directions ki taraf point karti, jo possible nahi hai.

Compass ki madad se magnetic field line kaise draw karein

Paper aur magnet ko fix karein

Drawing board par fixed white paper ki sheet par ek bar magnet rakhein, aur magnet ki boundary mark karein.

North pole ke paas se shuru karein

Compass ko magnet ke north pole ke paas rakhein. Needle ka south pole magnet ke north pole ki taraf point karta hai, aur needle ka north pole us se away directed hota hai.

Needle ko mark karein

Paper par needle ke dono ends ki position mark karein.

Needle ko aage badhayein

Compass ko nayi position par move karein aise ki uska south pole wahi position le le jo pehle uske north pole ne li thi.

South pole tak chalein

Isi tarah step by step aage badhein jab tak magnet ke south pole tak na pahunch jayein.

Marks ko join karein

Paper par marked points ko ek smooth curve se join karein. Yeh curve ek field line represent karta hai. Poori procedure repeat karein aur jitni zyada lines ho sake draw karein.

Ek lambi seedhi moti copper wire ko vertically pakad kar usme current pass karein, aur ek cardboard par jiske through wire pass hoti hai, iron filings uniformly sprinkle karein. Cardboard ko halke se tap karne par filings copper wire ke around concentric circles ke pattern mein arrange ho jaati hain. Ye concentric circles magnetic field lines ko represent karti hain.

Ek circle par kisi point par compass rakhein. Compass needle ke north pole ki direction us point par field line ki direction batati hai, aur aap is direction ko arrow se show kar sakte hain. Agar straight wire se jaane wale current ki direction reverse kar di jaaye, to magnetic field lines ki direction bhi reverse ho jaati hai.

Seedhi wire ka field: current badlo, distance (doori) badlo

Ek seedhi wire ko end-on dekha jaata hai, aur usse ek chuni hui distance par ek compass rakha hai. Wire ke around concentric field circles banaaye gaye hain. Current badhane par needle aur zyada ghumti hai; compass ko aur door le jaane par ghumav chhota ho jaata hai. Needle ka ghumav sirf shabdon mein dikhaya gaya hai (chhota, medium, bada), kyunki chapter sirf ye batata hai ki deflection current ke saath badhta hai aur distance ke saath ghatta hai, ye nahi ki kitna.

Kisi given point par rakhi compass needle ka deflection kya hota hai agar copper wire mein current badla jaye? Current ko vary karo aur dekho. Agar current increase kiya jaye, to deflection bhi increase hota hai. Iska matlab hai ki kisi given point par produce hone wale magnetic field ka magnitude badhta hai jab wire ke through current badhta hai.

Agar compass ko copper wire se door le jaya jaye jabki current same rahe to kya hota hai? Compass ko zyada door point par rakho. Needle mein deflection kam ho jata hai. Is tarah, conductor mein kisi given current se produce hone wala magnetic field kam ho jata hai jab usse distance (doori) badhta hai, aur current-carrying straight wire ke around field ko represent karne wale concentric circles aur bade hote jate hain jab hum usse door move karte hain.

Nimnalikhit mein se kaun sa long straight wire ke paas magnetic field ko sahi describe karta hai?

Kyon?

Jis cardboard se wire guzar raha hai us par bikhri hui iron filings wire ke around concentric circles ke along settle ho jati hain; isliye long straight current-carrying wire ke paas field lines concentric circles hoti hain jo wire par centred hoti hain.

Straight copper wire jo compass needle ke parallel aur uske upar rakha hai, us activity mein current north se south ki taraf flow karta hai aur needle ka north pole east ki taraf move karta hai. Ab current ko south se north ki taraf flow karaya jata hai. Needle ka north pole kis taraf move karega?

Kyon?

Wire mein current ki direction reverse karne se uske dwara produce hone wale magnetic field ki direction reverse ho jati hai, isliye needle opposite direction mein deflect hoti hai — west ki taraf.

Yahan current-carrying conductor se associated magnetic field ki direction find karne ka ek convenient tarika hai. Imagine kijiye ki aap ek current-carrying straight conductor ko apne right hand mein aise hold kar rahe hain ki thumb current ki direction ki taraf point karta hai. Phir aapki fingers conductor ke around magnetic field ki field lines ki direction mein wrap hongi. Isse right-hand thumb rule kehte hain.

Is rule ko Maxwell's corkscrew rule bhi kaha jata hai. Agar hum maan lein ki hum corkscrew ko current ki direction mein drive kar rahe hain, to corkscrew ke rotation ki direction hi magnetic field ki direction hoti hai.

Ise aise use kijiye: pehle apne thumb se current ki direction fix kijiye, phir apni fingers ke curl karne ke tareeke se field ki direction read kijiye. Field circles wire ke perpendicular planes mein hote hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek horizontal power line mein current east se west direction mein flow karta hai. Iske theek neeche ek point par aur theek upar ek point par magnetic field (chumbakiya kshetra) ki direction kya hai?

  1. Current ki direction note kijiye Power line horizontal hai aur current east se west ki taraf flow karta hai.
  2. Right-hand thumb rule apply kijiye Wire ko apne right hand mein aise hold kijiye ki thumb current ki direction — east se west — ki taraf point kare. Phir aapki fingers magnetic field ki direction mein wire ke around curl karti hain.
  3. Field circles ko imagine kijiye Field lines circles hain jo wire ke perpendicular plane mein lie karti hain, yani north-south chalne wale vertical plane mein. Wire ke theek neeche wale point par aur theek upar wale point par field dono inhi circles par lie karta hai.
  4. Turning ka sense read kijiye East end se wire ke along dekhein to fingers ka curl — aur isliye field — clockwise turn karta hai; west end se dekhein to wahi field anti-clockwise turn karta hai.

Uttar (Answer) Wire ke neeche ya upar kisi bhi point par magnetic field wire ke perpendicular plane mein clockwise turn karta hai jab east end se dekha jaye, aur anti-clockwise jab west end se dekha jaye.

Ek lambi seedhi wire mein current vertically upwards flow karta hai. Right-hand thumb rule ka use karte hue, wire ke aas-paas magnetic field lines hain

Kyon?

Apne right hand ka thumb current ke along upwards point karein; aapki fingers wire ke chaaron taraf curl karti hain. Upar se dekhein to yeh curl anticlockwise hai.

Maan lijiye ki seedhi wire ko circular loop ke roop mein mod diya gaya hai aur usme current pass kiya jata hai. Ek current-carrying seedhi wire dwara produce kiya gaya magnetic field uske distance (doori) par inversely depend karta hai, isliye loop ke har point par field ko represent karne wale circles waise-waise bade hote jate hain jaise-jaise hum wire se door jate hain.

Jab hum circular loop ke centre tak pahunchte hain, tab in bade circles ke arcs straight lines ke roop mein dikhte hain. Current carry karne wali wire ke har point par ek magnetic field utpann hota hai jo loop ke centre par straight line ke roop mein dikhta hai. Right-hand rule apply karne se, yeh check karna aasan hai ki wire ka har section loop ke andar same direction mein magnetic field lines mein contribute karta hai.

Ek circular loop of wire table ke plane mein hai aur current clockwise flow karta hai jab upar se dekha jaye. Loop ke centre par magnetic field hai

Kyon?

Loop par right-hand rule apply karo: apni fingers ko current ki direction mein curl karo, jo ki upar se dekhne par clockwise hai, aur thumb aap se door point karta hai — neeche ki taraf table ke andar. Isliye loop ke andar field table ke perpendicular hai, neeche ki taraf point karta hua, aur loop ke bahar yeh doosri direction mein point karta hai.

Current-carrying wire dwara kisi given point par produce kiya gaya magnetic field usme pass hone wale current par directly depend karta hai. Isliye, agar ek circular coil hai jisme n turns hain, to produce kiya gaya field single turn dwara produce kiye gaye field se n times bada hota hai. Yeh isliye kyunki har circular turn mein current ki direction same hoti hai, aur har turn ke due field bas add up ho jate hain.

Solenoid ek coil hai jisme insulated copper wire ke many circular turns cylinder ke shape mein wound hote hain. Iski magnetic field lines bar magnet jaisi hoti hain: solenoid ka ek end magnetic north pole ki tarah act karta hai. Solenoid ke andar field lines parallel straight lines hoti hain, aur magnetic field uniform hota hai — sabhi points par same — ek long solenoid ke andar jo current carry kar raha ho.

Current-carrying solenoid ke andar ek strong magnetic field ka use uske andar rakhe gaye steel ke piece ko magnetise karne ke liye kiya jata hai. Ek electromagnet mein soft iron ka core hota hai jiske around insulated copper wire ki coil wrapped hoti hai.

Sahi option chuniye. Current-carrying long straight solenoid ke andar magnetic field

Kyon?

Long current-carrying solenoid ke andar field lines parallel straight lines hoti hain, barabar spacing ke saath, isliye field uniform hai — iske andar sabhi points par same hai.

Kisi conductor se flow hone wala electric current ek magnetic field produce karta hai, aur is tarah produce hua field conductor ke aas-paas rakhe magnet par force (bal) lagata hai. French scientist Andre Marie Ampere ne suggest kiya ki magnet bhi current-carrying conductor par barabar aur opposite force lagata hoga.

Ek chhoti aluminium rod AB, lagbhag 5 cm lambi, ko do connecting wires se stand se horizontally latkayein. Ek strong horse-shoe magnet ko aise rakhein ki rod do poles ke beech ho aur magnetic field upwards directed ho: north pole rod ke vertically neeche aur south pole rod ke vertically upar. Rod ko battery, key aur rheostat ke saath series mein connect karein, aur rod mein current end B se end A tak pass karein. Rod displace ho jaati hai. Current ki direction reverse karein to rod opposite direction mein displace hoti hai.

Ab magnet ke do poles interchange karein taaki field vertically downwards ho. Phir se current-carrying rod par lagne wale force ki direction reverse ho jaati hai. Isse pata chalta hai ki conductor par force ki direction current ki direction aur magnetic field ki direction par depend karti hai.

Prayoghon ne dikhaya hai ki rod ka displacement (visthapan) sabse zyada hota hai, yaani, force (bal) ka magnitude sabse zyada hota hai, jab current ki direction magnetic field ki direction ke right angles (90 degree) par hoti hai. Aisi sthiti mein hum conductor par lagne wale force ki direction dhundhne ke liye ek simple rule use kar sakte hain.

Yeh rule hai Fleming's left-hand rule. Apne left hand ke thumb, forefinger aur middle finger ko aise stretch karein ki ve mutually perpendicular ho. Agar first finger magnetic field ki direction mein point kare aur second finger current ki direction mein, to thumb motion (gati) ki direction mein point karega, ya conductor par lagne wale force ki direction mein.

Jin devices mein current-carrying conductors aur magnetic fields ka use hota hai unme electric motor, electric generator, loudspeakers, microphones aur measuring instruments shamil hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek electron magnetic field mein right angles par enter karta hai. Electron par lagne wale force (bal) ki direction hogi (a) right ki taraf. (b) left ki taraf. (c) page se bahar. (d) page ke andar.

  1. Current ki direction fix karein Current ki direction electron ki motion (gati) ki direction ke opposite li jaati hai. Isliye pehle electron ki motion ki direction ko half turn se ulta karke current ki direction nikaalte hain.
  2. Current aur field ke beech ka angle note karein Electron field ke right angles par enter karta hai, isliye current bhi field ke right angles par hai — yahi woh case hai jahan force sabse zyada hota hai aur Fleming's left-hand rule seedha apply hota hai.
  3. Fleming's left-hand rule apply karein First finger ko magnetic field ke along point karein aur second finger ko current ke along (electron ki motion ke opposite). Phir thumb force ki direction deta hai, jo field aur current dono ke perpendicular hai.
  4. Figure se answer padhein Dikhayi gayi directions ke liye ise apply karne par force page ke andar ki direction mein milta hai.

Uttar (Answer) Option (d): force page ke andar ki direction mein hai.

Ek positively-charged particle (alpha-particle) jab west ki taraf project kiya jata hai, to magnetic field usse north ki taraf deflect karta hai. Magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Particle positive hai, isliye current west ki taraf hai. Force (bal) north ki taraf hai, current ke right angles par. In do directions par Fleming's left-hand rule apply karne se, first finger (field) upward point karni chahiye.

Horse-shoe magnet ke poles ke beech latki hui aluminium rod AB wale experiment mein, jab rod mein current end B se end A tak pass kiya jata hai to rod left ki taraf displace hoti hai. Agar rod mein current ki direction reverse kar di jaye to kya hoga?

Kyon?

Conductor mein current ki direction reverse karne par rod par lagne wale force (bal) ki direction reverse ho jati hai. Isliye rod opposite direction mein, right ki taraf displace hoti hai.

Aap ek chamber mein baithe hain aur aapki peeth ek wall ki taraf hai. Ek electron beam, jo back wall se front wall ki taraf horizontally move kar rahi hai, ek strong magnetic field se aapki right side ki taraf deflect ho jati hai. Magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Electrons back wall se front wall ki taraf move karte hain, isliye current ki direction unke motion (gati) ke opposite li jati hai — front wall se back wall ki taraf. Beam aapki right side ki taraf deflect hoti hai, isliye force (bal) right ki taraf hai. In do directions par Fleming's left-hand rule apply karne se, first finger vertically downward point karni chahiye.

Jab ek current-carrying conductor magnetic field mein rakha jata hai, to us par lagne wala force (bal) kab sabse zyada hota hai?

Kyon?

Rod ka displacement (visthapan) sabse zyada hota hai, yani force sabse zyada hota hai, jab current ki direction magnetic field ki direction ke right angles par hoti hai.

Kaun sa rule current-carrying straight conductor par lagne wale force (bal) ki direction deta hai jab woh magnetic field ke perpendicular rakha ho?

Kyon?

Fleming's left-hand rule current-carrying conductor par lagne wale force ki direction deta hai: pehli finger field ke along, doosri finger current ke along, thumb force ke along. Right-hand thumb rule (Maxwell's corkscrew rule) straight current-carrying conductor ke around magnetic field ki direction ke liye hota hai.

Apne gharon mein hum electric power ki supply main supply ke through prapt karte hain, jo ya to overhead electric poles ke through hoti hai ya underground cables ke through. Is supply mein ek wire, jis par aksar red insulation cover hota hai, live wire ya positive wire kaha jata hai. Doosri wire, jis par black insulation hota hai, neutral wire ya negative wire kaha jata hai. Hamare desh mein in dono ke beech potential difference 220 V hota hai, aur hum 50 Hz frequency wali 220 V AC electric power prapt karte hain.

Ghar ke meter-board par ye wires main fuse ke through electricity meter mein jaati hain. Main switch ke through ye ghar ki line wires se connect hoti hain. Ye wires ghar ke andar alag-alag circuits ko electricity supply karti hain. Aksar do alag circuits use hote hain: ek 15 A current rating wala un appliances ke liye jinki power rating zyada hoti hai, jaise geysers aur air coolers, aur doosra 5 A current rating wala bulbs, fans, etc. ke liye.

Earth wire, jis par green colour ka insulation hota hai, aksar ghar ke paas zameen ke andar gehri metal plate se connect kiya jata hai. Ise safety measure ke roop mein use kiya jata hai, khaas kar un appliances ke liye jinpar metallic body hoti hai, jaise electric press, toaster, table fan ya refrigerator. Metallic body ko earth wire se connect kiya jata hai, jo current ke liye low-resistance conducting path pradan karta hai. Is tarah yeh ensure karta hai ki appliance ki metallic body mein current ka koi bhi leakage uske potential ko earth ke potential ke barabar rakhta hai, aur user ko severe electric shock nahi lag sakta.

Har alag circuit mein, different appliances ko live wire aur neutral wire ke across connect kiya ja sakta hai, aur har appliance ka apna separate switch hota hai jisse uske through current ko on aur off kiya ja sake. Taaki har appliance ke across equal potential difference ho, unhe ek doosre ke parallel mein connect kiya jata hai.

Electric fuse sabhi domestic circuits ka ek important component hota hai. Circuit mein fuse overloading ke karan appliances aur circuit ko damage hone se rokta hai. Overloading tab ho sakti hai jab live wire aur neutral wire direct contact mein aa jate hain. Aisa tab hota hai jab wires ki insulation damaged ho jati hai ya appliance mein fault ho jata hai. Aisi situation mein circuit ka current abruptly badh jata hai. Ise short-circuiting kehte hain.

Electric fuse ka use electric circuit aur appliance ko possible damage se bachata hai, kyunki yah unduly high electric current ke flow ko rok deta hai. Fuse mein hone wala Joule heating use melt kar deta hai aur electric circuit ko break kar deta hai. Overloading supply voltage mein accidental hike ke karan bhi ho sakti hai, aur kabhi-kabhi ek hi socket se bahut zyada appliances connect karne se bhi ho sakti hai.

Electric circuits aur appliances mein commonly use hone wale do safety measures ke naam bataiye.

Kyon?

Fuse circuit ko break kar deta hai jab current bahut zyada ho jata hai, aur earth wire low-resistance path deta hai taaki metallic body tak leakage current severe shock na de.

Domestic electric circuits mein overloading se bachne ke liye kya precaution lena chahiye?

Kyon?

Overloading tab ho sakti hai jab ek single socket par bahut zyada appliances connect kiye jayein, isliye ek socket par appliances ki number kam rakhni chahiye. Supply voltage mein accidental hike bhi circuit ko overload kar sakti hai.

Short circuit ke samay, circuit mein current

Kyon?

Short-circuiting mein live wire aur neutral wire seedhe contact mein aa jate hain, isliye circuit mein current achank badh jata hai.

Bataiye ki nimnlikhit kathan true hain ya false: (a) Current carrying ek lambi circular coil ke centre par field parallel straight lines hogi. (b) Green insulation wala wire aam taur par electric supply ka live wire hota hai.

Kyon?

Ek lambi circular coil ke centre par field circles ke arcs straight lines ki tarah dikhte hain, isliye (a) true hai. Green insulation wala wire earth wire hota hai; live wire par red insulation hota hai, isliye (b) false hai.

Earth wire ka function kya hai, aur metallic appliances ko earth karna kyun zaroori hai?

Kyon?

Metallic appliances ko earth isliye karte hain kyunki unke bodies mein leaking current aa sakta hai. Earth wire us current ko earth tak easy path deta hai, isliye body earth ke potential par rehti hai aur user protected rehta hai.

In mein se kaun se do magnetic field produce karne ke methods hain?

Kyon?

Magnetic field magnet ke aas-paas ke region mein hota hai, aur electric current carry karne wale metallic wire ke aas-paas bhi magnetic field hota hai. Is chapter mein magnetic field produce karne ke yahi do tareeke bataye gaye hain.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

magnetic field
Magnet ke aas-paas ka region jisme magnet ki force detect ki ja sakti hai; iski direction aur magnitude dono hoti hain.
magnetic field line
Iron filings ya compass needle ki help se draw ki gayi smooth curve jo magnetic field ki direction dikhati hai; lines north pole se emerge hoti hain, south pole par merge hoti hain aur kabhi cross nahi karti.
right-hand thumb rule
Apne right hand ka thumb straight conductor mein current ke along point karein; phir aapki curled fingers magnetic field lines ki direction dikhati hain.
solenoid
Insulated copper wire ke many circular turns ka coil jo cylinder ke shape mein wound hota hai; uska field bar magnet jaisa hota hai aur andar uniform hota hai.
electromagnet
Insulated copper wire ke coil se wrapped soft iron core, jo coil se current flow hone par strong magnet ban jata hai.
Fleming's left-hand rule
First finger magnetic field ke along, second finger current ke along, thumb conductor par force ke along.
live wire
Red insulation wala domestic supply wire, jise positive wire bhi kehte hain.
neutral wire
Black insulation wala domestic supply wire, jise negative wire bhi kehte hain; live aur neutral ke beech potential difference 220 V hota hai.
earth wire
Green-insulated wire jo earth mein deep metal plate se connected hoti hai, low-resistance path deti hai taki leakage current se severe shock na lage.
short-circuiting
Live aur neutral wires ke beech direct contact (damaged insulation ya faulty appliance) jisse current abruptly increase ho jata hai.
overloading
Aisi condition jisme circuit ka current bahut zyada ho jata hai, short-circuiting, supply voltage mein hike, ya ek socket par bahut zyada appliances ke karan.
fuse
Safety device jo current bahut zyada hone par Joule heating se melt hota hai aur circuit break kar deta hai.
