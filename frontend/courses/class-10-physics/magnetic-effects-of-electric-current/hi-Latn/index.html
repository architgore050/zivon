Magnetic Effects of Electric Current

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap explain kar sakte hain ki electric current kaise magnetic field produce karta hai aur compass needle kaise dikhati hai.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap magnetic field lines describe kar sakte hain aur straight current-carrying conductor ke liye right-hand thumb rule use kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap circular loop, solenoid aur electromagnet ke fields explain kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap current-carrying conductor par force ke liye Fleming's left-hand rule use kar sakte hain aur domestic circuit ke safety parts explain kar sakte hain.

Ek chhota compass ek seedhi copper wire ke paas rakho. Current flow hone se pehle, needle ek hi direction mein rest karti hai. Jab aap wire se current pass karte hain, toh needle deflect hoti hai. Yeh simple observation is chapter ka darwaza hai.

Yeh deflection ka matlab hai ki copper wire se guzarne wala electric current ne magnetic effect produce kiya hai. Electricity aur magnetism linked hain. Ek current-carrying wire magnet ki tarah behave karti hai.

1820 mein Hans Christian Oersted ne accidentally paya ki jab ek metallic wire ke paas se electric current pass hota hai toh compass needle deflect ho jati hai. Isne dikhaya ki electricity aur magnetism related phenomena the.

Oersted ka naam magnetic field strength ki unit ke liye use hota hai, the oersted.

Ek seedhi copper wire ke paas rakhi compass needle deflect hoti hai jab wire se current pass kiya jata hai. Yeh kya dikhata hai?

Kyon?

Compass needle ka deflect hona dikhata hai ki copper wire se guzarne wale current ne magnetic effect paida kiya hai.

Compass needle ek chhota bar magnet hota hai. Iska north-seeking end lagbhag north ki taraf point karta hai aur north pole kahlata hai; iska south-seeking end lagbhag south ki taraf point karta hai aur south pole kahlata hai. Like poles repel karte hain, jabki unlike poles attract karte hain.

Iron filings ke paas magnet lao to filings ek pattern mein arrange ho jate hain. Magnet apne aas-paas ke region mein force (bal) exert karta hai. Us region mein magnetic field hota hai.

Jin lines ke along iron filings align hote hain unhe magnetic field lines kehte hain. Kisi point par magnetic field ki direction woh direction hoti hai jisme compass needle ka north pole wahan move karta hai.

Field lines north pole se emerge hoti hain aur south pole par merge hoti hain. Magnet ke andar, yeh south pole se north pole tak run karti hain, isliye field lines closed curves hoti hain.

Field lines ki closeness strength dikhati hai: crowded lines ka matlab stronger field. Koi bhi do field lines cross nahi karti, kyunki crossing par compass needle ko ek hi samay do directions mein point karna padega, jo impossible hai.

Jab compass needle ko bar magnet ke paas laate hain to wo deflected kyun hoti hai?

Kyon?

Compass needle ek chhota bar magnet hota hai, isliye paas ke magnet ka field us par force lagakar use ghumata hai.

Bar magnet ke around magnetic field lines kaise draw karein

Paper aur magnet ko fix karein

White paper ki ek sheet drawing board par fix karein aur centre mein ek bar magnet rakhein. Magnet ki boundary mark karein.

North pole ke paas se shuru karein

Magnet ke north pole ke paas ek chhota compass rakhein. Needle ka south pole magnet ke north pole ki taraf point karta hai, aur compass ka north pole magnet ke north pole se away direction mein hota hai.

Mark karein aur aage badhein

Needle ke dono ends ki position mark karein. Compass ko nayi position par move karein taaki uska south pole wahi position le le jo pehle uske north pole ne li thi.

South pole tak continue karein

Step by step aage badhein jab tak magnet ke south pole tak na pahunch jayein. Paper par marked points ko ek smooth curve se join karein. Yah curve ek field line represent karta hai.

Aur lines draw karein

Procedure repeat karein aur jitni lines draw kar sakte hain karein. Aapko magnet ke around magnetic field lines ka pattern milta hai.

Nimnalikhit mein se kaun si magnetic field lines ki property hai?

Kyon?

Field lines north pole se emerge hoti hain aur south pole par merge hoti hain; magnet ke andar yeh south se north ki taraf jaati hain, isliye yeh closed curves banati hain. Koi bhi do field lines cross nahi karti.

Do magnetic field lines ek doosre ko intersect kyun nahi karti hain?

Kyon?

Crossing par field ke ek hi point par do directions hote, isliye compass needle ko ek hi samay par do directions mein point karna padta.

Magnetic field produce karne ke kaun se do methods hain?

Kyon?

Magnet ke around magnetic field hota hai, aur current-carrying conductor bhi magnetic field produce karta hai.

Ek lambi seedhi copper wire se current pass karo. Jis cardboard se wire guzarti hai us par iron filings chhidko. Ye filings wire ke chaaron taraf concentric circles banati hain. Ye circles magnetic field lines hain.

Agar tum current reverse karte ho, to field lines ki direction reverse ho jaati hai. Agar tum current badhate ho, to compass ka deflection badh jaata hai, isliye field stronger hota hai. Agar tum compass ko wire se door le jaate ho, to deflection kam ho jaata hai, isliye field weaker hota hai.

Ek seedhi current-carrying wire ke chaaron taraf field lines wire par kendrit concentric circles hoti hain.

Nimn mein se kaun sa long straight wire ke paas magnetic field ko sahi describe karta hai?

Kyon?

Ek straight current-carrying wire ke aas-paas iron filings wire par centred concentric circles mein arrange ho jaati hain.

Right-hand thumb rule ka use karna

Conductor ko pakdo

Kalpana karo ki tum current-carrying straight conductor ko apne right hand mein pakde hue ho.

Thumb ko point karo

Apne thumb ko current ki direction mein point karo.

Fingers ko curl karo

Tumhari fingers conductor ke around magnetic field ki field lines ki direction mein wrap ho jaati hain.

Doosra naam yaad rakho

Is rule ko Maxwell's corkscrew rule bhi kehte hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek horizontal power line mein current east se west direction mein flow karta hai. Iske theek neeche ek point par aur theek upar ek point par magnetic field ki direction kya hogi?

  1. Current direction identify karo Current east-west direction mein hai.
  2. Right-hand thumb rule apply karo Right hand ka thumb current ke along point karo; fingers wire ke around magnetic field ki direction mein curl karte hain.
  3. East end se dekho East end se dekha jaye to wire ke neeche ya upar kisi bhi point par magnetic field wire ke perpendicular plane mein clockwise turn karta hai.
  4. West end se dekho West end se dekha jaye to wahi field anti-clockwise turn karta hai.

Uttar (Answer) East end se dekhne par wire ke neeche ya upar kisi bhi point par magnetic field clockwise turn karta hai, aur west end se dekhne par anti-clockwise turn karta hai.

Agar ek seedhi wire mein current reverse kar diya jaye, to uske around magnetic field lines ke saath kya hota hai?

Kyon?

Current reverse karne se current dwara produce magnetic field ki direction reverse ho jati hai.

Straight wire ko bend karke circular loop bana do. Loop ke har point par field lines wire ke around circles hoti hain. Jaise-jaise aap wire se door jaate hain, ye circles bade hote jaate hain. Loop ke centre par, in bade circles ke arcs straight lines jaise dikhte hain. Wire ka har section loop ke andar apna field same direction mein add karta hai.

Agar coil mein n turns hain, to field single turn ke field se n times bada hota hai, kyunki har turn current ko same direction mein carry karta hai aur fields add up ho jaate hain.

Ek circular wire loop table ke plane mein rakha hai. Jab upar se dekha jaye to loop mein current clockwise hai. Right-hand rule ka use karke, loop ke andar magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Right hand ki fingers ko current ki clockwise direction mein curl karo; thumb table ke andar ki taraf point karega. Loop ke bahar field lines opposite direction mein wapas aati hain.

Solenoid ek coil hai jisme cylinder ke shape mein wound kai circular turns hote hain. Iski field lines bar magnet ki field lines jaisi hoti hain. Solenoid ke andar field lines straight hoti hain aur field uniform hota hai. Ek end north pole ki tarah kaam karta hai aur doosra south pole ki tarah.

Electromagnet ek soft iron core se banta hai jiske around insulated copper wire ki coil wrapped hoti hai. Current-carrying solenoid andar rakhi steel rod ko magnetise kar sakta hai.

Ek lambi straight solenoid jisme current flow ho raha hai, uske andar magnetic field:

Kyon?

Ek lambi solenoid jisme current flow ho raha hai, uske andar field lines straight hoti hain aur field uniform hota hai, isliye andar har point par field same hota hai.

Kisi diye gaye region mein magnetic field uniform hai. Yeh field lines dwara kaise represent kiya jata hai?

Kyon?

Ek uniform field ki har jagah same strength aur direction hoti hai, isliye iski field lines parallel aur barabar doori wali seedhi lines hoti hain.

Magnetic field mein rakha hua current-carrying conductor ek force (bal) experience karta hai. Ek strong horse-shoe magnet ke poles ke beech ek chhoti aluminium rod ko horizontally suspend karo aur usmein current pass karo. Rod displace ho jati hai. Current reverse karo to displacement (visthapan) reverse ho jata hai. Magnetic field reverse karo to force phir se reverse ho jata hai.

Force sabse zyada hota hai jab current magnetic field ke right angles par ho. Current-carrying conductors aur magnetic fields ka use karne wale devices mein electric motors, electric generators, loudspeakers, microphones aur measuring instruments shamil hain.

Fleming's left-hand rule ka upyog

Ungliyon ko stretch karein

Apni left hand ke thumb, forefinger aur middle finger ko stretch karein taaki ve ek dusre ke perpendicular hon.

First finger ko point karein

First finger ko magnetic field ki direction mein point karein.

Second finger ko point karein

Second finger ko current ki direction mein point karein.

Thumb ki direction dekhein

Thumb phir motion (gati) ya conductor par lagne wale force (bal) ki direction mein point karta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Activity 12.7 mein, ek chhoti aluminium rod AB ko stand se horizontally latkaya gaya hai. Ek strong horse-shoe magnet aise rakha gaya hai ki uska north pole rod ke vertically neeche aur south pole vertically upar ho. Rod mein current end B se end A tak pass kiya jata hai, aur rod left ki taraf displaced ho jati hai. Explain kijiye ki rod kyon displaced hoti hai, aur bataiye ki agar rod mein current ki direction reverse kar di jaye to kya observe hoga.

  1. Magnetic field (chumbakiya kshetra) ki direction note kijiye Magnet ka north pole rod ke vertically neeche hai aur south pole vertically upar hai, isliye poles ke beech magnetic field upwards directed hai.
  2. Current ki direction note kijiye Current rod mein end B se end A tak pass kiya jata hai.
  3. Force rule apply kijiye Magnetic field mein rakha current-carrying conductor force (bal) experience karta hai. Yeh force current ki direction aur magnetic field ki direction dono ke perpendicular hota hai, isliye hum iski direction nikalne ke liye Fleming's left-hand rule use karte hain.
  4. Fleming's left-hand rule se direction read kijiye First finger ko magnetic field ke along (upwards) point kijiye aur second finger ko current ke along (B se A) point kijiye. Thumb phir rod par force ki direction deta hai, isi wajah se rod left ki taraf displaced hoti hai.
  5. Current reverse kijiye Conductor mein current ki direction reverse karne par force ki direction reverse ho jati hai. Isliye displacement ab right ki taraf hota hai. (Force sabse zyada hota hai jab current magnetic field ke right angles par ho, jaise yahan hai.)

Uttar (Answer) Rod left ki taraf displaced hoti hai kyunki current-carrying rod magnetic field mein force experience karti hai, jiski direction Fleming's left-hand rule se milti hai. Current reverse karne par force ki direction reverse ho jati hai, isliye rod ab right ki taraf displaced hoti hai.

Activity 12.7 mein, ek chhoti aluminium rod AB ko ek strong horse-shoe magnet ke poles ke beech latkaya gaya hai aur usme se current pass kiya jata hai. Rod displaced ho jati hai. Yeh kya dikhata hai?

Kyon?

Rod ka displacement (visthapan) yeh darshata hai ki current-carrying aluminium rod par force lagta hai jab use magnetic field mein rakha jata hai.

Activity 12.7 mein, rod AB ke through current ki direction reverse kar di jati hai. Kya observe hota hai?

Kyon?

Force (bal) ki direction reverse ho jati hai jab conductor ke through current ki direction reverse hoti hai. Magnetic field ki direction ko poles interchange karke change karne se bhi force reverse ho jata hai.

Ek positively-charged particle (alpha-particle) jab west ki taraf project kiya jata hai, to magnetic field usse north ki taraf deflect karta hai. Magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Fleming's left-hand rule use karein. Current west ki taraf hai aur force (bal) north ki taraf hai; isliye magnetic field upward hona chahiye.

Jab ek current-carrying conductor ko magnetic field mein rakha jata hai, to us par lagne wala force (bal) kab sabse zyada hota hai?

Kyon?

Rod ka displacement (visthapan) sabse zyada hota hai jab current ki direction magnetic field ki direction ke right angles par hoti hai.

Ek electron beam horizontally chamber ki back wall se front wall ki taraf move karti hai. Yeh ek strong magnetic field ke karan aapke right side ki taraf deflect hoti hai. Magnetic field ki direction kya hai?

Kyon?

Electron current electron motion ke opposite hota hai. Fleming's left-hand rule ka use karte hue, force right side ki taraf hone se magnetic field downward milta hai.

Kaunsa rule straight current-carrying conductor ke aas-paas magnetic field ki direction deta hai, aur kaunsa rule magnetic field mein current-carrying conductor par force ki direction deta hai?

Kyon?

Right-hand thumb rule straight conductor ke aas-paas field ki direction deta hai, aur Fleming's left-hand rule current-carrying conductor par force ki direction deta hai. Chapter rotation se induced current ke liye koi rule nahi batata; yeh sirf electric generator ko ek device ke roop mein naam deta hai.

Gharo mein electric power mains ke through aati hai, chahe overhead poles se ho ya underground cables se. Live wire par aksar red insulation hoti hai aur ye positive hota hai. Neutral wire par black insulation hoti hai aur ye negative hota hai. Hamare desh mein inke beech potential difference 220 V hai. Supply AC hai, 220 V par, frequency 50 Hz ke saath.

Meter-board par wires electricity meter aur main fuse se guzarti hain. Main switch ke through ye line wires se connect hoti hain. Aksar do circuits use hote hain: high-power appliances jaise geysers aur air coolers ke liye 15 A circuit, aur bulbs aur fans ke liye 5 A circuit.

Earth wire par green insulation hoti hai aur ye zameen ke andar gehri metal plate se connected hoti hai. Ye metallic bodies wale appliances ke liye safety measure hai. Ye low-resistance path deta hai taaki metallic body mein current ka koi leakage user ko severe shock na de.

Appliances live aur neutral ke across parallel mein connected hote hain taaki har ek ko same potential difference mile. Har appliance ka apna switch hota hai. Fuse overloading se damage rokta hai. Fuse mein Joule heating use melt karti hai aur circuit break karti hai.

Short-circuiting tab hota hai jab live aur neutral wires seedhe touch karti hain; current abruptly badh jata hai. Overloading supply voltage mein accidental hike se ya ek socket se bahut zyada appliances connect karne se bhi ho sakti hai.

Do safety measures ke naam bataiye jo electric circuits aur appliances mein commonly use hote hain.

Kyon?

Fuse overloading se protection deta hai kyunki overload hone par ye melt ho jata hai, aur earth wire leakage current ke liye safety path provide karta hai.

Ek electric fuse domestic circuit ko kaise protect karta hai?

Kyon?

Fuse overloading ke karan appliances aur circuit ko hone wale damage se bachata hai, kyunki yeh had se zyada high electric current ka flow rok deta hai; fuse mein Joule heating hoti hai jisse woh melt ho jata hai aur circuit break ho jata hai.

Domestic electric circuits ke overloading se bachne ke liye kya precaution lena chahiye?

Kyon?

Overloading tab ho sakti hai jab ek hi socket mein bahut saare appliances connect kiye jaate hain; agar current bahut zyada ho jaye to fuse circuit ko protect karta hai.

Short circuit ke samay, circuit mein current

Kyon?

Jab live aur neutral wires directly touch karte hain, to circuit mein current achanak se badh jata hai.

Electric short circuit kab hota hai?

Kyon?

Live aur neutral wires ke beech direct contact short-circuiting hai; yeh tab ho sakta hai jab insulation damaged ho ya kisi appliance mein fault ho.

Earth wire ka function kya hai, aur metallic appliances ko earth karna kyun zaroori hai?

Kyon?

Metallic body earth wire se connected hoti hai. Agar current body mein leak hota hai, to earth wire uska potential earth ke potential ke barabar rakhta hai aur user ko protect karta hai.

Kaun sa statement true hai?

Kyon?

Current-carrying circular coil ke centre par field lines parallel straight lines dikhti hain. Green-insulated wire earth wire hota hai, live wire nahi.

Body mein electric currents, yahan tak ki nerve cells ke saath weak ion currents bhi, magnetic fields paida karte hain. Ye fields bahut weak hain, earth ke magnetic field ka lagbhag one-billionth. Heart aur brain do main organs hain jahan produce hone wala magnetic field significant hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

magnetic field
Magnet ke aas-paas ka region jahan uska force detect kiya ja sakta hai.
magnetic field line
Ek line jo dikhati hai ki ek hypothetical free north pole kis path par move karega; ye magnetic field ko represent karti hai.
right-hand thumb rule
Straight current-carrying wire ke around field ki direction ke liye ek rule: thumb current ke along, curled fingers field lines ke along.
Fleming's left-hand rule
Current-carrying conductor par force ke liye ek rule: forefinger field ke along, middle finger current ke along, thumb force ke along.
solenoid
Ek cylindrical coil jisme wire ke many turns hote hain; iska field bar magnet jaisa hota hai.
electromagnet
Ek soft iron core jiske around insulated copper wire ki coil wrapped hoti hai, jo current flow hone par magnetic ho jata hai.
live wire
Red-insulated domestic supply wire, jise positive kaha jata hai.
neutral wire
Black-insulated domestic supply wire, jise negative kaha jata hai.
earth wire
Green-insulated safety wire jo earth mein deep metal plate se connected hoti hai.
fuse
Ek safety device jo current bahut zyada hone par melt ho jata hai aur circuit break kar deta hai.
short circuit
Live aur neutral wires ke beech direct contact jo current ko abruptly increase kar deta hai.
MRI
Magnetic Resonance Imaging, ek technique jo diagnosis ke liye images banane ke liye body ke magnetic field ko use karti hai.
