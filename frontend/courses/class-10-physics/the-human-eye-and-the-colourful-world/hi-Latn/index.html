The Human Eye and the Colourful World

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap human eye ke parts describe kar sakte hain aur bata sakte hain ki light andar jaate waqt har part kya karta hai.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap accommodation explain kar sakte hain aur normal eye ka near point aur far point bata sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap myopia, hypermetropia aur presbyopia identify kar sakte hain aur bata sakte hain ki kaunsa lens kaunsa defect correct karta hai.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap prism se refraction, dispersion aur rainbow, stars kyun twinkle karte hain, aur sky blue kyun hota hai, yeh explain kar sakte hain.

Hamare sabhi sense organs mein, eye hi ek aisa organ hai jo humein duniya aur uske colours dekhne deta hai. Aankhein band karke aap smell, taste, sound ya touch se kaafi cheezein pehchan sakte hain, lekin kisi bhi cheez ka colour nahi bata sakte.

Human eye lagbhag camera ki tarah kaam karta hai. Iska lens system eye ke andar ek light-sensitive screen par image banata hai, aur woh screen retina (aankh ke andar ki light-sensitive membrane) hoti hai.

Eyeball lagbhag spherical hota hai, jiska diameter about 2.3 cm hai. Light isme front side ki ek thin membrane se enter karti hai jise cornea (aankh ke front ki transparent membrane) kehte hain, jo eyeball ki front surface par ek transparent bulge banati hai.

Aankh mein pravesh karne wali light ka zyadatar refraction (apvartan) cornea ke bahari surface par hota hai. Aankh ke andar ka crystalline lens sirf focal length ka finer adjustment karta hai jo different distances par objects ko retina par focus karne ke liye zaroori hai.

Cornea ke peeche iris hota hai, ek dark muscular diaphragm. Yeh pupil ke size ko control karta hai, jo uske centre par opening hai, aur pupil aankh mein pravesh karne wali light ki matra ko regulate aur control karta hai.

Aankh ke peeche, retina ek delicate membrane hai jismein bahut zyada number mein light-sensitive cells hote hain. Jab light un par padti hai to woh activate ho jate hain aur electrical signals generate karte hain.

Yeh signals optic nerves ke saath brain tak travel karte hain. Brain unko interpret karta hai aur information ko process karta hai, taaki hum objects ko waise perceive karein jaise woh hain.

Eye lens object ka inverted real image retina par banata hai. Brain hi hai jo is image ko humare liye samajhne layak banata hai.

Manav aankh kisi object ka image apne kis part par banati hai?

Kyon?

Aankh camera jaisi hoti hai: iska lens system aankh ke peeche wali light-sensitive screen par image banata hai, aur woh screen retina hoti hai.

Human eye mein entering light ka zyada tar refraction (apvartan) kahan hota hai?

Kyon?

Zyada tar refraction cornea ki outer surface par hota hai. Crystalline lens sirf focal length ka finer adjustment karta hai jo alag-alag distances par objects ko retina par focus karne ke liye zaroori hai.

Eye lens ek fibrous, jelly-like material se bana hota hai. Iski curvature ko ciliary muscles kuch had tak change kar sakti hain, aur curvature badalne se lens ki focal length badal jaati hai.

Jab ciliary muscles relaxed hoti hain, to lens patla ho jaata hai. Iski focal length badh jaati hai, aur isse hum door ke objects ko clearly dekh paate hain.

Jab aap eye ke closer kisi object ko dekhte hain, to ciliary muscles contract karti hain. Eye lens ki curvature badh jaati hai, lens mota ho jaata hai, aur iski focal length kam ho jaati hai. Isse hum paas ke objects ko clearly dekh paate hain.

Eye lens ki apni focal length adjust karne ki ability ko Accommodation (samayojan) kaha jaata hai.

Accommodation ki ek limit hoti hai. Eye lens ki focal length ko ek certain minimum se neeche nahi kam kiya ja sakta. Koi printed page aapki aankhon ke bahut paas rakh kar padhne ki koshish kijiye: image blur ho jaati hai aur aankh par strain mehsoos hota hai.

Kisi object ko comfortably aur distinctly dekhne ke liye use aankhon se lagbhag 25 cm door hold kijiye. Minimum distance (doori) jis par objects bina strain ke sabse distinctly dekhe ja sakte hain, use least distance of distinct vision, ya eye ka near point kehte hain. Normal vision wale young adult ke liye near point lagbhag 25 cm hota hai.

Sabse door point jahan tak eye objects ko clearly dekh sakti hai, use eye ka far point kehte hain. Normal eye ke liye far point infinity hota hai. Isliye ek normal eye 25 cm aur infinity ke beech kahin bhi rakhe objects ko clearly dekh sakti hai.

Kabhi-kabhi old age mein logon ka crystalline lens milky aur cloudy ho jaata hai. Is condition ko cataract kehte hain. Isse partial ya complete loss of vision ho sakta hai, aur cataract surgery ke through vision restore kiya ja sakta hai.

Aankh ki power of accommodation ka kya matlab hai?

Kyon?

Accommodation eye lens ki focal length adjust karne ki ability hai, taaki different distances par rakhe objects retina par focus ho jaayein. Ciliary muscles lens ki curvature badal kar ye adjustment karte hain.

Manav aankh eye lens ki focal length ko adjust karke different distances (dooriyon) par sthit objects par focus kar sakti hai. Yeh kis karan se hota hai?

Kyon?

Eye lens ki focal length ko adjust karna hi accommodation ka matlab hai. Presbyopia, myopia aur hypermetropia defects hain, normal ability nahi.

Aankh ke lens ki focal length mein change kiski action se hota hai?

Kyon?

Ciliary muscles eye lens ki curvature ko change karti hain. Zyada curvature ka matlab thicker lens aur shorter focal length; relaxed muscles ka matlab thin lens aur longer focal length.

Normal vision wale young adult ke liye least distance of distinct vision lagbhag kitni hoti hai?

Kyon?

Near point, ya least distance of distinct vision, normal vision wale young adult ke liye lagbhag 25 cm hota hai.

Normal vision wali human eye ka far point aur near point kya hote hain?

Kyon?

Normal eye ke liye far point infinity hota hai aur near point lagbhag 25 cm hota hai, isliye eye 25 cm aur infinity ke beech ki har cheez saaf dekh sakti hai.

Normal eye 25 cm se paas rakhe gaye objects ko clearly kyun nahi dekh pati?

Kyon?

Ciliary muscles eye lens ki curvature sirf ek had tak badha sakti hain, isliye focal length minimum se neeche nahi kam ho sakti. Isliye lagbhag 25 cm se paas ke objects blurred image dete hain aur eye par strain daalte hain.

Jab hum object ki eye se distance (doori) badhate hain, to eye mein image distance ka kya hota hai?

Kyon?

Retina eye ke back par fixed hoti hai, isliye image ko hamesha usi par fall karna chahiye. Jab object aur door jata hai, eye image ko move nahi karti; balki ciliary muscles eye lens ki curvature badalti hain aur isse focal length badalta hai, taki image phir bhi retina par hi banti rahe.

Kabhi-kabhi aankh dheere-dheere apni power of accommodation kho deti hai. Uske baad vyakti objects ko distinctly aur comfortably nahi dekh pata, aur eye ke refractive defects ki wajah se vision blurred ho jata hai.

Vision ke mainly teen common refractive defects hote hain: myopia ya near-sightedness, hypermetropia ya far-sightedness, aur presbyopia. Inhe suitable spherical lenses ka use karke correct kiya ja sakta hai.

Myopia ko near-sightedness bhi kaha jata hai. Myopia wala vyakti paas ke objects ko clearly dekh sakta hai, lekin door ke objects ko distinctly nahi dekh pata. Aise vyakti ka far point infinity se nazdeek hota hai; vyakti kuch metres ki distance (doori) tak hi clearly dekh sakta hai.

Myopic eye mein, door ke object ka image retina ke saamne banta hai, retina par nahi. Ye defect eye lens ki excessive curvature ki wajah se ho sakta hai, ya kyunki eyeball elongated ho gaya hai.

Myopia ko suitable power ke concave lens se correct kiya jata hai. Concave lens image ko wapas retina par le aata hai.

Hypermetropia ko far-sightedness bhi kaha jata hai. Hypermetropia wale vyakti door ke objects ko saaf dekh sakte hain, lekin paas ke objects unhe clearly nahi dikhte. Aise vyakti ka near point (paas ka point) normal 25 cm se zyada door hota hai, isliye vyakti ko reading material eye se 25 cm se kaafi zyada door rakhna padta hai.

Yeh isliye hota hai kyunki paas ke object se aane wali light rays retina ke peeche ek point par focus hoti hain. Yeh defect ya to eye lens ki focal length bahut zyada hone se hota hai, ya eyeball bahut chhota ho jane se.

Hypermetropia ko appropriate power ke convex lens ka use karke correct kiya jata hai. Converging lenses wale eye-glasses wo additional focussing power dete hain jo image ko retina par banane ke liye zaroori hoti hai.

Eye ki power of accommodation usually ageing ke saath kam ho jaati hai, aur near point dheere-dheere door chala jaata hai. Phir log bina corrective eye-glasses ke paas ke objects ko comfortably aur distinctly dekhna mushkil paate hain. Is defect ko presbyopia kaha jaata hai. Yeh ciliary muscles ke gradually weak hone aur eye lens ki flexibility kam hone ki wajah se hota hai.

Kabhi-kabhi ek person ko myopia aur hypermetropia dono ho sakte hain. Aise logon ko often bi-focal lenses chahiye hote hain, jo concave aur convex lenses dono se milke bante hain: upper portion distant vision ke liye concave lens hota hai, aur lower part near vision ke liye convex lens hota hai.

Aaj-kal refractive defects ko contact lenses se ya surgical interventions ke through bhi correct kiya ja sakta hai.

Myopic eye (myopia) wale person ko 1.2 m se door ke objects distinctly nahi dikhte. Proper vision restore karne ke liye kis type ka corrective lens use karna chahiye?

Kyon?

Myopia ko suitable power ke concave lens se correct kiya jaata hai. Is person ka Far point sirf 1.2 m hai, isliye concave lens ko distant object ka image retina ke aage se wapas retina par laana hoga.

Ek student ko last row mein baithkar blackboard padhne mein difficulty hoti hai. Bachche ko kaun sa defect ho sakta hai, aur usse kaise correct kiya ja sakta hai?

Kyon?

Blackboard door hota hai, aur jo bachcha last row se use padh nahi sakta, wo distant objects ko distinctly nahi dekh sakta. Yah myopia hai, aur ise suitable power ke concave lens se correct kiya jata hai.

Lens power aur focal length

Power dioptres mein measure hoti hai

Lens power dioptres mein quote ki jaati hai, jise D likha jaata hai. Is chapter ke numerical problems mein distant vision ke liye -5.5 D aur near vision ke liye +1.5 D jaise values use hote hain.

Power aur focal length

Lens ki power P aur uski focal length f (metres mein) ke beech sambandh P = 1/f hai, isliye f = 1/P. f ko metres mein rakho to power dioptres mein aati hai.

Power ka sign batata hai kaun sa lens

Myopia correct karne ke liye jo lens use hota hai, jo ki concave lens hai, wahi hai jise chapter minus sign (-5.5 D) ke saath quote karta hai. Hypermetropia correct karne ke liye jo lens use hota hai, jo ki convex lens hai, wahi hai jise plus sign (+1.5 D) ke saath quote kiya jaata hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek person ko apni distant vision correct karne ke liye -5.5 dioptres power ka lens chahiye. Near vision correct karne ke liye usko +1.5 dioptre power ka lens chahiye. (i) distant vision aur (ii) near vision correct karne ke liye required lens ki focal length kya hai?

  1. Jo diya gaya hai use likhiye Distant vision ke liye lens ki power P = -5.5 D. Near vision ke liye lens ki power P = +1.5 D.
  2. Relation choose kijiye Power aur focal length ka relation P = 1/f hai, jahan f metres mein hai, isliye f = 1/P. Answer metres mein aata hai.
  3. Distant vision f = 1/(-5.5) = -0.18 m, yani lagbhag -18 cm. Negative sign batata hai ki ye concave lens hai.
  4. Near vision f = 1/(+1.5) = +0.67 m, yani lagbhag +67 cm. Positive sign batata hai ki ye convex lens hai.

Uttar (Answer) Distant vision: focal length lagbhag -0.18 m (lagbhag -18 cm), ek concave lens. Near vision: focal length lagbhag +0.67 m (lagbhag +67 cm), ek convex lens.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek myopic person ka far point eye ke saamne 80 cm par hai. Problem ko correct karne ke liye required lens ki nature aur power kya hai?

  1. Lens ka kaam kya hai Person ko distant objects clearly dikhne chahiye. Isliye corrective lens ko ek very distant object ka image person ke apne far point par banana hoga, jo eye ke saamne 80 cm par hai.
  2. Required lens ki focal length Infinity par object ke liye image lens ke focal point par banti hai, isliye focal length 80 cm honi chahiye: f = -80 cm = -0.8 m. Minus sign isliye use hota hai kyunki ye image lens ke usi side par hai jis side object hai.
  3. Lens ki power P = 1/f = 1/(-0.8) = -1.25 D.
  4. Lens ki nature Negative power ka matlab concave lens hota hai, aur myopia ko exactly yahi chahiye.

Uttar (Answer) Power -1.25 D wala concave lens.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek diagram banao jo dikhaye ki hypermetropia kaise correct hota hai. Ek hypermetropic eye ka near point 1 m hai. Is defect ko correct karne ke liye required lens ki power kya hai? Assume karo ki normal eye ka near point 25 cm hai.

  1. Lens ko kya karna hai Person ko normal near point 25 cm par padhne ke liye able hona chahiye. Isliye 25 cm par rakha gaya object lens ke same side par person ke apne near point, 1 m, par image produce kare.
  2. Object distance u = -25 cm = -0.25 m. Object lens ke near side par hai, isliye iski distance (doori) negative li jati hai.
  3. Image distance v = -1 m. Image virtual hai aur object ke same side par hai, isliye ye bhi negative hai.
  4. Lens formula apply karo 1/v - 1/u = 1/f se 1/f = 1/(-1) - 1/(-0.25) = -1 + 4 = 3 per metre milta hai.
  5. Focal length aur power f = 1/3 m = +0.33 m, aur P = 1/f = +3.0 D. Positive sign batata hai ki lens convex hai.

Uttar (Answer) Ek convex lens jiski power +3.0 D hai (focal length lagbhag +0.33 m). Ray diagram wahi hai jo upar figure mein dikhaya gaya hai: convex lens 25 cm par rakhe object se aayi rays ko retina par milata hai.

Rectangular glass slab se guzarne wala light usi direction ke parallel bahar nikalta hai jis direction mein woh andar aaya tha, bas thoda sideways displaced hota hai, kyunki slab ki dono refracting surfaces parallel hoti hain.

Ek triangular glass prism alag hota hai. Iske do triangular bases aur teen rectangular lateral surfaces hote hain, aur ye surfaces ek doosre ke inclined hote hain. Iske do lateral faces ke beech ke angle ko angle of the prism kehte hain.

Jab light ki ek ray PE prism ke ek refracting face par girti hai, toh glass mein enter karte waqt woh normal ki taraf bend hoti hai aur andar refracted ray EF ke roop mein travel karti hai. Doosre face par woh glass se air mein jaati hai, isliye normal se door bend hoti hai aur emergent ray FS ke roop mein bahar nikal jaati hai.

Prism ki peculiar shape emergent ray ko incident ray ki direction ke saath ek angle par bend kar deti hai. Is angle ko angle of deviation kehte hain.

Ek triangular glass prism mein, uske do lateral faces ke beech ke angle ko kya kehte hain?

Kyon?

Ek triangular prism ke do triangular bases aur teen rectangular lateral surfaces hote hain. Uske do lateral faces ke beech ka angle prism ka angle hota hai, jo ray diagram mein A se marked hai.

Jab Sun se aayi white light ki ek narrow beam ko glass prism par girne diya jata hai aur nikalne wali light ko paas ke screen par roka jata hai, to colours ka ek sundar band dikhta hai. Prism ne white light ko colours ke band mein split kar diya hai.

Jo colours aap dekhte hain, order mein, violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange aur red hain. VIBGYOR acronym aapko sequence yaad rakhne mein madad karta hai.

Light beam ke coloured components ke band ko uska spectrum kaha jata hai. Light ka apne component colours mein split hone ko dispersion kaha jata hai.

Ye colours humein kyun milte hain? Light ke different colours incident ray ke respect mein different angles se bend hote hain jab wo prism se pass hote hain. Red light sabse kam bend hoti hai aur violet sabse zyada bend hoti hai. Isliye har colour ki rays different paths par emerge hoti hain aur distinct ho jati hain.

Isaac Newton pehle vyakti the jinhone sunlight ka spectrum obtain karne ke liye glass prism ka use kiya. Jab unhone colours ko another similar prism se further split karne ki koshish ki, to unhe koi aur colours nahi mile. Jab unhone second identical prism ko inverted position mein rakha, to saare colours uske through pass ho gaye aur doosri side par white light ki beam nikli. Isse Newton ko idea aaya ki sunlight saat colours se milti hai.

Jo bhi light sunlight jaisa spectrum deti hai, use aksar white light kaha jata hai.

Rainbow ek natural spectrum hai jo rain shower ke baad sky mein dikhta hai. Yeh atmosphere mein present tiny water droplets dwara sunlight ke dispersion (bikharna) ki wajah se hota hai. Rainbow hamesha Sun ke opposite direction mein form hota hai.

Water droplets chhote prisms ki tarah act karte hain. Yeh incident sunlight ko refract aur disperse karte hain, phir ise internally reflect karte hain, aur finally jab yeh raindrop se bahar aati hai to ise dobara refract karte hain. Is dispersion aur internal reflection ki wajah se different colours observer ki eye tak pahunchte hain.

Aap sunny day par rainbow tab bhi dekh sakte hain jab aap Sun ko apne peeche rakh kar waterfall ya water fountain ke through sky ko dekhte hain.

Jab white light glass prism se guzarti hai, to kaunsa colour sabse zyada bend hota hai aur kaunsa sabse kam?

Kyon?

Prism se guzarte waqt different colours different angles se bend hote hain. Red light sabse kam bend hoti hai aur violet sabse zyada, isi wajah se colours alag-alag paths par nikalte hain aur ek spectrum banate hain.

Aapne aisa dekha hoga ki jab aap fire ya radiator ke upar uth rahi hot air ke through kisi object ko dekhte hain, to woh object wavering ya flickering sa lagta hai. Fire ke thik upar ki air us air se hotter hoti hai jo thodi aur upar hai.

Hotter air lighter hoti hai, yaani ki upar ki cooler air se less dense hoti hai, aur uska refractive index cooler air ke refractive index se thoda kam hota hai. Kyunki air ki physical conditions stationary nahi hoti, hot air ke through dikhne wale object ki apparent position lagatar fluctuate karti rehti hai. Yeh hamare local environment me small scale par atmospheric refraction hai.

Stars ka twinkling isi tarah ka phenomenon hai, lekin bahut bade scale par. Starlight earth ke atmosphere me enter karte hi earth tak pahunchne se pehle continuously refraction se guzarti hai. Atmosphere starlight ko normal ki taraf bend karta hai, isliye star ki apparent position uski actual position se thodi different hoti hai.

Horizon ke paas ka star apni actual position se thoda higher dikhta hai. Uski apparent position stationary nahi hoti, balki thodi-thodi badalti rehti hai, kyunki atmosphere ki physical conditions stationary nahi hoti. Jaise-jaise light rays ka path badalta rehta hai, star ki apparent position fluctuate karti hai aur eye me enter hone wali starlight ki amount flicker karti hai; isliye star kabhi brighter aur kabhi fainter dikhta hai. Yahi twinkling effect hai.

Planets twinkle nahi karte. Ye earth ke kaafi kareeb hote hain aur extended sources ke roop mein dikhte hain. Agar hum ek planet ko bahut saare point-sized sources of light ka collection maanein, to un sabhi sources se hamari aankh mein aane wali light ki total variation average out ho kar zero ho jaati hai, jisse twinkling effect khatam ho jaata hai.

Atmospheric refraction ki wajah se, Sun humein actual sunrise se lagbhag 2 minute pehle aur actual sunset ke lagbhag 2 minute baad dikhta hai. Actual sunrise ka matlab hai Sun dwara horizon ko actual cross karna.

Sunrise aur sunset ke samay Sun ke disc ka apparent flattening bhi isi phenomenon ki wajah se hota hai.

Stars twinkle kyun karte hain?

Kyon?

Atmosphere starlight ko normal ki taraf bend karta hai, isliye star ki apparent position uski actual position se alag hoti hai. Kyunki atmosphere ki physical conditions stationary nahi rehti hain, apparent position lagatar fluctuate karti rehti hai, aankh mein entering starlight ki amount flicker hoti hai, aur star twinkle karta hua dikhta hai.

Planets twinkle kyun nahi karte?

Kyon?

Ek planet star ki tulna mein earth ke kaafi zyada paas hota hai aur extended source ke roop mein dikhta hai. Ise point-sized sources ka ek bada collection maanenge to un sabse aane wali light ka total variation average hokar zero ho jaata hai, jisse twinkling effect nullify ho jaata hai.

True solution (asli solution) se guzarte hue light beam ka path visible nahi hota. Ye visible ho jata hai jab wo colloidal solution se guzarta hai, jahan particles ka size relatively larger hota hai.

Earth ka atmosphere minute particles ka heterogeneous mixture hai: smoke, tiny water droplets, suspended dust particles aur air ke molecules. Jab light ki beam aise fine particles par padti hai, to beam ka path visible ho jata hai, kyunki light in particles se diffusely reflected hone ke baad ham tak pahunchti hai. Colloidal particles dwara light ke is scattering se Tyndall effect hota hai.

Tyndall effect tab dikhta hai jab sunlight ki fine beam ek small hole se smoke-filled room mein enter karti hai, aur tab bhi jab sunlight dense forest ke canopy se guzarti hai, jahan mist mein tiny water droplets light ko scatter karte hain.

Scattered light ka colour scattering particles ke size par depend karta hai. Very fine particles mainly blue light scatter karte hain, jabki larger size ke particles longer wavelengths ki light scatter karte hain. Agar particles kaafi large hon, to scattered light white bhi appear kar sakti hai.

Atmosphere mein air ke molecules aur doosre fine particles visible light ke wavelength se chhote hote hain. Ye blue end par shorter wavelengths ki light ko scatter karne mein red end par longer wavelengths ki light se zyada effective hote hain.

Red light ka wavelength blue light se lagbhag 1.8 guna zyada hota hai. Isliye jab sunlight atmosphere se guzarta hai, toh air ke fine particles blue colour ko red se zyada strongly scatter karte hain, aur scattered blue light hamari aankhon mein pravesh karti hai.

Agar earth ka atmosphere nahi hota toh koi scattering nahi hoti, aur sky dark dikhta. Isliye sky un passengers ko dark dikhta hai jo bahut high altitudes par udte hain, jahan scattering prominent nahi hoti.

Aapne notice kiya hoga ki danger signal lights red hoti hain. Red colour fog ya smoke se sabse kam scatter hota hai, isliye ek red light distance (doori) se same colour mein dikh sakti hai.

Ek astronaut ko aasman blue ke bajaye dark kyun dikhta hai?

Kyon?

Aasman ka blue colour atmosphere mein fine particles dwara sunlight ke scattering se aata hai. Atmosphere na hone par scattering nahi hoti, isliye aasman dark dikhta hai. Isi karan se bahut high altitudes par udne wale passengers ko bhi aasman dark dikhta hai.

Hamari aankhen hamari maut ke baad bhi zinda reh sakti hain. Marne ke baad apni aankhen donate karke hum ek blind person ki life mein light la sakte hain.

Developing world mein lagbhag 35 million log blind hain, aur unmein se zyadatar cure ho sakte hain. Corneal blindness wale lagbhag 4.5 million log donated eyes ki corneal transplantation ke through cure ho sakte hain, aur unmein se 60% bachche 12 saal se kam umar ke hain.

Eye donors kisi bhi age group ya sex ke ho sakte hain. Jo log spectacles use karte hain, ya jinka cataract ke liye operation ho chuka hai, wo bhi apni aankhen donate kar sakte hain.

Death ke baad 4-6 hours ke andar aankhen nikalni hoti hain, aur nearest eye bank ko turant inform karna chahiye. Eye removal mein sirf 10-15 minutes lagte hain aur isse koi disfigurement nahi hota. Aankhon ka ek pair up to four corneal blind people ko vision de sakta hai.

Donate ki gayi eyes ka ek pair kitne corneal blind logon ko vision de sakta hai?

Kyon?

Eyes ka ek pair up to char corneal blind logon ko vision de sakta hai. Death ke baad 4-6 hours ke andar eyes nikalni hoti hain, aur jo log spectacles use karte hain ya cataract ke liye operated hue hain, woh bhi apni eyes donate kar sakte hain.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

Cornea
Woh patli transparent membrane jo eyeball ke front par bulge banati hai; aankh mein enter hone wali light ka zyada refraction iski outer surface par hota hai.
Iris
Cornea ke peeche dark muscular diaphragm jo pupil ka size control karti hai.
Pupil
Iris ke centre par opening; yeh aankh mein enter hone wali light ki amount ko regulate aur control karta hai.
Retina
Aankh ke back par light-sensitive screen jis par eye lens inverted real image banata hai.
Accommodation
Eye lens ki apni focal length adjust karne ki ability, jo ciliary muscles se iski curvature badal kar hoti hai.
Near point
Least distance of distinct vision: sabse kareeb ki distance jis par object bina strain ke sabse distinctly dekh sakte hain, normal young adult ke liye about 25 cm.
Far point
Sabse door ki distance jahan tak aankh objects ko clearly dekh sakti hai; normal eye ke liye infinity.
Myopia
Near-sightedness: nearby objects clear hote hain lekin distant nahi, kyunki distant object ka image retina ke aage banata hai; concave lens se corrected.
Hypermetropia
Far-sightedness: distant objects clear hote hain lekin nearby nahi, kyunki nearby object ka image retina ke peeche banata hai; convex lens se corrected.
Presbyopia
Woh defect jisme ageing ke saath near point door hota jaata hai kyunki ciliary muscles weak hote hain aur eye lens flexibility kho deta hai.
Dispersion
White light ka prism dwara apne seven component colours mein split hona.
Spectrum
Light beam ke coloured components ka band, order mein violet, indigo, blue, green, yellow, orange, red.
Angle of deviation
Woh angle jis par prism emergent ray ko incident ray ki direction se bend karta hai.
Tyndall effect
Colloidal particles dwara light ka scattering, jisse beam ka path visible ho jaata hai.
