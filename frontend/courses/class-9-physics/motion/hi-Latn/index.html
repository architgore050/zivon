Motion

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap force (bal) ko uske magnitude, direction aur unit se describe kar sakte hain, aur spring balance se force measure kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap net force nikal sakte hain jab kisi object par kai forces act karti hain, aur bata sakte hain ki forces balanced hain ya nahi.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap Newton ke teen laws of motion state aur apply kar sakte hain, including F = ma aur newton ki definition.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap do connected objects ki acceleration (tvaran) nikal sakte hain jab unhe ek single system maana jaye.

Zameen par padi ek ball motion (gati) mein aa jati hai jab aap use kick karte hain. Aapki taraf aa rahi cricket ball ki direction badal jati hai jab bat usse hit karti hai. Nimbu ka shape badal jata hai jab aapki ungliyan use dabati hain. Ye teenon changes force (bal) ki wajah se hote hain.

To force (bal) kisi object ko rest se motion mein la sakta hai, kisi moving object ki speed (chaal) aur direction of motion badal sakta hai, aur kisi object ka shape badal sakta hai.

Dhyan dijiye ki in examples mein har force ki ek direction hoti hai. Friction motion ke opposite act karta hai, magnets kisi particular line ke along push ya pull karte hain, Earth kisi object ko apni taraf pull karti hai, aur ek liquid floating object ko buoyant force se upwards push karta hai.

Isliye force ek physical quantity hai jiske liye humein magnitude aur unit ke saath-saath direction bhi batani hoti hai. Force ka SI unit newton hai, words mein chhote 'n' ke saath likha jata hai, aur iska symbol N hai. Kisi force ka magnitude uski strength batata hai. Agar magnitude, direction, ya dono badal jayein, to force ka effect bhi badal jata hai.

Hum kaise measure karte hain ki ek force (bal) kitna bada hai? Ek spring balance yeh kaam karta hai. Aapne iska istemal kisi object ka weight measure karne ke liye kiya hai, aur kisi object ka weight woh gravitational force hai jisse Earth use kheenchti hai.

Ek spring balance weight measure karne se zyada karta hai. Agar aap iske free end ko kheenchte hain, toh yeh us force ko measure karta hai jisse aap balance ke andar spring par kheenchte hain. Toh wahi instrument generally ek force ka magnitude measure karta hai.

Ek halka touch millinewtons ke order ka force hota hai, yaani 10⁻³ N, jo lagbhag sabse chhota force hai jo ek insaan direct feel kar sakta hai. Specialised experiments mein, scientists yoctonewtons jitne chhote forces measure kar sakte hain, yaani 10⁻²⁴ N.

Force (bal) ke baare mein kaun sa statement sahi hai?

Kyon?

Force (bal) ek physical quantity hai jisme magnitude aur direction dono hote hain, aur iska SI unit newton (N) hai. Agar magnitude, direction, ya dono badal jayein, to force (bal) ka effect badal jata hai.

Asli zindagi mein, aksar ek hi samay par kisi object par ek se zyada force (bal) act karte hain. Jab aap floor par box ko dhakelte hain, to aapka push ek force hai aur friction ka force doosra, jo opposite direction mein act karta hai. Jab ek ball paani par tairti hai, to Earth ka gravitational force use neeche kheenchta hai aur liquid ka buoyant force use upar dhakelta hai.

Tug of war sochiye. Agar dono teams equal force se kheenchti hain, to rope nahi move karti. Do forces jo magnitude mein equal hain lekin direction mein opposite hain, unhe balanced forces kehte hain. Agar ek team zyada zor se kheenchti hai, to forces balanced nahi rehte aur rope larger force ki direction mein move karti hai.

Jab kisi object par forces balanced nahi hote, to us par non-zero net force act karta hai. Agar do opposite forces unequal hain, to net force unke magnitudes ka difference hota hai aur larger force ki direction mein point karta hai. Agar do forces same direction mein act karte hain, to net force unke magnitudes ka sum hota hai aur usi same direction mein point karta hai.

Key idea yaad rakhiye: kisi object par kai forces act kar sakte hain, lekin uski motion (gati) sirf net force par depend karti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek block par, jo table par rakha hai, 10 N aur 6 N ke do force (bal) act kar rahe hain. Har case mein block par act karne wale net force ka magnitude aur direction kya hai? (a) dono force right ki taraf act karte hain; (b) 10 N force right ki taraf act karta hai aur 6 N force left ki taraf act karta hai; (c) 10 N force left ki taraf act karta hai aur 6 N force right ki taraf act karta hai.

  1. (a) Same direction mein forces Dono force right ki taraf act karte hain, isliye unhe add karein: net force = 10 N + 6 N = 16 N, jo right side ki taraf act karta hai.
  2. (b) Opposite forces, larger force right ki taraf Forces ek doosre ke opposite hain, isliye subtract karein: net force = 10 N − 6 N = 4 N, jo right side ki taraf act karta hai, yani larger force ke along.
  3. (c) Opposite forces, larger force left ki taraf Phir subtract karein: net force = 10 N − 6 N = 4 N, lekin ab yeh left side ki taraf act karta hai, 10 N force ke along.

Uttar (Answer) (a) 16 N right ki taraf; (b) 4 N right ki taraf; (c) 4 N left ki taraf.

Ek weightlifter barbell ko utha kar apne sir ke upar steady rakhti hai. Barbell par kaun se do force (bal) act kar rahe hain, aur kya woh balanced hain?

Kyon?

Barbell steady hai, isliye iski velocity nahin badalti. Iske liye zero net force chahiye, isliye weightlifter ka upward force downward weight ko balance karna chahiye.

Do players R aur S ek arm-wrestling match mein hain. Us instant par jab arms front direction (page ke bahar, aapki taraf) tilt karte hain, kya players dwara lagaye gaye forces balanced hain? Agar nahi, to kis player ne larger force lagaya?

Kyon?

Jab do opposite forces unequal hoti hain, net force larger force ki direction mein point karta hai, aur arms us direction mein move karte hain. Ek side ki taraf tilt dikhata hai ki kiski force larger hai.

Kisi object ko floor par rest mein rakho aur use halke se push karo. Aksar woh move nahi karta; aapko zyada zor se push karna padta hai. Iska reason object ki bottom surface aur floor ke beech ka friction (gharshana) force (bal) hai, jo aapke push ke opposite direction mein act karta hai.

Box sirf tab move karna shuru karta hai jab aapka force friction ke force se larger ho, taaki box par motion (gati) ki direction mein net force act kare.

Pushed box par do aur forces act karte hain. Gravitational force, yani uska weight, downwards act karta hai. Jis surface par box rests karta hai, us surface dwara lagaya gaya force normal force kehlata hai, aur woh upwards, surface ke perpendicular act karta hai. Level floor par box ke liye ye dono forces balanced hain. Air bhi moving box par friction force lagata hai, lekin usually yeh itna small hota hai ki ise neglect kiya ja sakta hai.

Ab pushing band kar do. Box slow down hota hai aur rest mein aa jata hai. Jis bicycle ko aap pedalling band karte ho, woh bhi kuch distance (doori) ke baad rest mein aa jati hai. Iska matlab yeh nahi ki motion continue karne ke liye force chahiye; iska matlab friction motion ke against act karta hai. Object ko moving rakhte rehne ke liye aapko ek force lagate rehna padta hai jo friction ko counter karta hai.

Do simple experiments dikhate hain ki friction different surfaces par kaise behave karta hai. Pehle experiment mein, chaar sikkon ka stack ek stretched rubber band se peeche khincha jata hai aur level surfaces par chhod diya jata hai: wooden table top, laminated table top, aur polished marble ya tiled floor. Rubber band ko har baar same amount tak stretch kiya jata hai.

Release se pehle, coins stationary hain, isliye un par forces (bal) balanced hain. Release par, rubber band coins ko forward push karta hai ek force ke saath jo friction se larger hai, isliye net force act karta hai aur coins accelerate karte hain. Jis moment coins rubber band se contact kho dete hain, un par sirf friction act karta hai, unki motion (gati) ke opposite, aur dheere-dheere unhe rest par le aata hai.

Coins different surfaces par different distances (doori) travel karte hain, isliye friction different hai. Ek spring balance jise horizontally kheencha jata hai aur jisme wooden block attached hai, friction ka approximate measure deta hai: jis reading par block just move karna shuru karta hai, woh force of friction ka magnitude dikhati hai.

Conclusion simple hai. Jahan friction smaller hai, velocity (veg) dheere-dheere decrease hoti hai aur object stopping se pehle larger distance travel karta hai. Jahan friction larger hai, object jaldi stop ho jata hai.

Ek thought experiment: agar friction zero hota to kya hota?

Friction ko zero set karo

Ek object aur ek horizontal floor imagine karo jo itna smooth ho ki unke beech friction force (bal) zero ho. Thought experiment tab use hota hai jab experiment ke liye zaroori conditions real world mein recreate karna mushkil ho.

Object ko release karo

Is floor par rubber band experiment repeat karo. Jab object rubber band se contact kho deta hai, to koi force use aage nahi dhakelta aur koi friction uske against act nahi karta.

Koi cheez velocity (veg) ko kam nahi kar sakti

Jab moving object par koi net force act nahi karta, to uski velocity change nahi ho sakti. Ye bilkul slow down nahi hota, isliye ye kabhi rest mein nahi aata; ye hamesha move karta rehta hai.

Historical step

Galileo Galilei ne 17th century mein, kai thought experiments ke through, argue kiya ki agar ek body horizontal plane par move karti hai aur uske motion (gati) ke saare impediments hata diye jayein, to wo indefinitely move karti rahegi.

Is tendency ka naam

Isaac Newton ne inertia word ka use kiya to describe kiya objects ki tendency ko ki wo apne state of rest ya uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein change ko resist karein, aur is idea ka use karke apna first law of motion frame kiya.

Ek horizontal force (bal) F ka use karke, ek table ko floor par constant velocity (veg) se move kiya jata hai. Floor dwara table par lagaya gaya frictional force kitna hai?

Kyon?

Constant velocity (veg) ka matlab hai ki velocity change nahi ho rahi, isliye acceleration (tvaran) aur net force zero hain. Isliye friction force ko applied force F ko exactly balance karna chahiye.

Newton's first law of motion (gati) kehta hai: ek object rest mein rest rehta hai, aur ek object motion mein constant velocity (veg) ke saath move karta rehta hai, jab tak us object par net force act nahi karta.

Dusre shabdon mein, agar kisi object par act karne wala net force zero hai, to object move karna shuru nahi kar sakta aur apni velocity change nahi kar sakta. Iska acceleration (tvaran) zero hai.

Ek object rest mein zero velocity rakhta hai. Constant velocity ka matlab hai ki velocity ke magnitude ya direction mein koi change nahi hota. Agar wo constant velocity non-zero hai, to object same direction mein same speed (chaal) ke saath straight line mein move karta hai.

Ye law ek common doubt ko bhi settle karta hai. Ek moving box ko forward push karo with a force (bal) exactly equal to the friction force: dono forces balance ho jate hain, net force zero ho jata hai, aur box rest mein aane ke bajaye constant velocity ke saath move karta rehta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek person ek moving box par forward direction mein force (bal) laga raha hai jo box ke bottom surface aur floor ke beech lagne wale friction force ke equal hai. Kya box move karta rahega ya kuch time baad rest mein aa jayega?

  1. Dono forces ko identify karein Person ka force forward act karta hai. Friction ka force backward, motion (gati) ke opposite, same magnitude ke saath act karta hai.
  2. Net force nikaalein Dono forces magnitude mein equal aur direction mein opposite hain, isliye wo ek doosre ko balance karte hain. Box par net force zero hai.
  3. Newton's first law apply karein Zero net force ke saath box ki velocity (veg) change nahi ho sakti, isliye box apni existing velocity ko maintain karta hai.

Uttar (Answer) Box constant velocity ke saath move karta rahega; wo rest mein nahi aayega.

Graphs jab net force zero hota hai

Us object ke position–time aur velocity–time graphs dikhao jis par koi net force act nahi karta. Zero speed (chaal) par object rest mein rehta hai; kisi bhi doosri speed par wo constant velocity (veg) se move karta hai.

Ek object constant velocity (veg) se move kar raha hai. Kya us par koi net force act kar raha hai?

Kyon?

Constant velocity ka matlab hai ki velocity ka magnitude aur direction dono change nahi ho rahe, isliye acceleration zero hai; Newton's first law ke according net force zero hona chahiye.

Maan lijiye kisi object par koi net force act nahi kar raha hai. Nimnlikhit situations mein se kaunsi possible NAHI hai?

Kyon?

Zero net force hone par acceleration (tvaran) zero hota hai, isliye velocity (veg) bilkul change nahi ho sakti. Constant non-zero acceleration ke liye net force chahiye hoga.

Ek ball smooth frictionless surface par move karti hai. Woh option chuniye jisse teenon statements physically correct ho. (i) Agar ball par koi net force apply nahi hota, to ball ki velocity (veg) ___. (ii) Agar uski motion (gati) ki direction mein net force apply hota hai, to velocity ka magnitude ___. (iii) Agar uski motion ke opposite net force apply hota hai, to velocity ka magnitude ___.

Kyon?

Net force na hone se velocity unchanged rehti hai. Motion ki direction mein force ball ko accelerate karta hai, isliye uski speed badh jaati hai. Motion ke opposite force usko opposite direction mein accelerate karta hai, isliye uski speed ghat jaati hai.

Ek ball ko halke se push kijiye to woh dheere move karna shuru karti hai. Zor se push kijiye to woh tezi se move karna shuru karti hai. Is rozmarra ke anubhav se aap ek hypothesis bana sakte hain: same object ke liye, larger force (bal) larger acceleration produce karta hai.

Ise test karne ke liye, same surface par same cart par different magnitudes ke weights ko pulley ke upar thread se latka kar different magnitudes ke forces lagaye jaate hain, aur fixed distance (doori) cover karne mein laga time measure kiya jaata hai. Same mass ke liye jab force badhaya jaata hai, acceleration (tvaran) badh jaata hai. Isliye fixed mass wale object ka acceleration badhta hai jaise-jaise us par net force badhta hai.

Rozmarra ka anubhav second hypothesis deta hai: same force ke saath, lighter object ko motion (gati) mein set karna heavier object se aasan hota hai. Same pulling force lekin heavier cart ke saath experiment repeat karne se pata chalta hai ki mass badhne par acceleration kam ho jaata hai. Isliye given force ke liye, acceleration object ke mass ke inversely related hota hai.

Newton's second law of motion states: jab kisi object par net force act karta hai, to object net force ki direction mein accelerate karta hai. Acceleration ka magnitude net force ke magnitude ke proportional hota hai aur object ke mass ke inversely proportional hota hai.

Second law se F = ma tak, aur newton ki definition

Proportionality likhiye

Law kehta hai ki acceleration (tvaran) net force ke proportional hai aur mass ke inversely proportional. Dono statements ko combine karne se a = F/m milta hai.

Ise rearrange kijiye

Dono sides ko m se multiply karne par F = ma milta hai, jahan F net force hai, m mass hai aur a acceleration (tvaran) hai. Acceleration ki direction net force ki direction ke same hoti hai.

Unit fix kijiye

Mass kg mein aur acceleration m s⁻² mein measure kiya jata hai. Agar m = 1 kg aur a = 1 m s⁻², to F = (1 kg) × (1 m s⁻²) = 1 kg m s⁻² = 1 N.

Definition bataiye

Ek newton force woh force hai jo 1 kg mass wale object par 1 m s⁻² ka acceleration (tvaran) produce karta hai.

Weight likhiye

Earth ke gravitational force ke under, girte hue object ka acceleration g hota hai. F = ma mein a = g rakhne se mass m wale object par gravitational force F = mg milta hai. g ki value 9.8 m s⁻² hai, Earth ki surface ke paas lagbhag constant; quick estimation ke liye aap g = 10 m s⁻² le sakte hain. Dhyan dijiye ki g object ke mass par depend nahi karta.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek weightlifter bar ke dono sides par 10 kg mass fixed wala barbell hold kar rahi hai. Bar ka khud ka mass 10 kg hai. Barbell ko steady rakhne ke liye woh kitna force (bal) apply kar rahi hai? (g = 9.8 m s⁻² lein.)

  1. Barbell ka total mass Ek side par 10 kg + doosri side par 10 kg + bar ke liye 10 kg = 30 kg.
  2. Barbell ka weight F = mg = 30 kg × 9.8 m s⁻² = 294 N, jo neeche ki direction mein lagta hai.
  3. Weightlifter dwara apply kiya gaya force Barbell ko steady rakhne ke liye uski velocity change nahi honi chahiye, isliye net force zero hai. Use opposite direction mein, yaani upwards, 294 N ke barabar force lagana hoga.

Uttar (Answer) Woh upward direction mein 294 N apply karti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek student horizontal floor par 25 kg ke stationary block ko push karne ki koshish kar raha hai. Is motion (gati) ko oppose karne wala maximum force (bal) of friction 50 N hai. Block ka displacement (visthapan) 2 seconds mein determine karein agar student use forward direction mein constant force (i) 50 N aur (ii) 55 N se push karta hai.

  1. (i) Applied force friction ke equal 50 N ka applied force 50 N ke opposing force of friction ke equal hai. Dono forces balanced hain aur block par net force zero hai, isliye block stationary rehta hai aur uska displacement 0 m hai.
  2. (ii) Net force Block par net force forward direction mein 55 N − 50 N = 5 N hai.
  3. Acceleration (tvaran) Newton's second law use karke, a = F/m = 5 N / 25 kg = 0.2 m s⁻², forward direction mein.
  4. 2 s mein displacement Block rest se start hota hai, isliye u = 0 m s⁻¹. s = ut + ½at² use karke, s = 0 × 2 s + ½ × 0.2 m s⁻² × (2 s)² = 0.4 m.

Uttar (Answer) (i) 0 m — block stationary rehta hai; (ii) forward direction mein 0.4 m.

Solved example (Worked example)

1500 kg mass ki ek sports car east ki taraf moving hai. Iska velocity–time graph t = 0 s par 0 m s⁻¹ se shuru hokar t = 5 s par 10 m s⁻¹ tak straight line ke roop mein badhta hai, 5 s se 10 s tak 10 m s⁻¹ par time axis ke parallel straight line rehta hai, aur phir t = 10 s par 10 m s⁻¹ se t = 15 s par 0 m s⁻¹ tak straight line ke roop mein girta hai. Car par lagne wala force (bal) calculate karein during (i) 0 s se 5 s, (ii) 5 s se 10 s, aur (iii) 10 s se 15 s.

  1. (i) 0 s se 5 s Graph time axis ki taraf inclined straight line hai, isliye acceleration (tvaran) constant hai, jahan u = 0 m s⁻¹, v = 10 m s⁻¹ aur t = 5 s. v = u + at se: 10 = 0 + a × 5, isliye a = 2 m s⁻². Phir F = ma = 1500 kg × 2 m s⁻² = 3000 N, east ki taraf act karta hai.
  2. (ii) 5 s se 10 s Graph time axis ke parallel hai, isliye velocity (veg) constant hai aur acceleration zero hai. Is interval mein car par koi force act nahin karta.
  3. (iii) 10 s se 15 s Yahan u = 10 m s⁻¹, v = 0 m s⁻¹ aur t = 5 s. v = u + at se: 0 = 10 + a × 5, isliye a = −2 m s⁻². Phir F = ma = 1500 kg × (−2 m s⁻²) = −3000 N. Negative sign dikhata hai ki force motion (gati) ki direction ke opposite act karta hai, yani west ki taraf.

Uttar (Answer) (i) 3000 N east ki taraf; (ii) zero force; (iii) 3000 N west ki taraf.

100 g mass ki ek toy car 0.5 m s⁻¹ ki constant velocity (veg) se move kar rahi hai. Toy car par lagne wala net force kya hai?

Kyon?

Constant velocity ka matlab hai acceleration (tvaran) zero hai. F = ma use karne par net force zero hota hai, car ka mass jo bhi ho.

Alag-alag masses ke do children ek jaise swings par baithe hain. Same initial acceleration (tvaran) dene ke liye, aapko kis child par larger force (bal) lagana hoga?

Kyon?

Same acceleration a ke liye, needed force F = ma hai, jo mass ke saath badhta hai. Isliye heavier child ko larger force chahiye.

Transportation ke liye glass items ko bubble wrap ya hay se pack karke damage se kaise protect kiya jata hai?

Kyon?

Soft packing same change of velocity ke liye stopping time ko lamba karti hai, jiska matlab smaller acceleration; F = ma se glass par smaller force milta hai.

Kisi force (bal) ke kaam aane ke liye at least do objects ka interact karna zaroori hai. Jab aap apne foot se ball ko push karte hain, to aapko ball dwara aapke foot par lagaya gaya force feel hota hai.

Newton's third law of motion (gati) kehta hai: jab bhi ek object doosre object par force exert karta hai, usi samay doosra object pehle object par equal aur opposite force exert karta hai.

Wheels wali chair par baithein aur ek heavy table ko apne se door push karein: aapki chair backwards move karti hai. Table ko apni taraf pull karein: aapki chair table ki taraf move karti hai. Har baar table aap par aapke push ya pull ki opposite direction mein force lagata hai. Do identical spring balances ko hook karke apart pull karne par har baar same value read hoti hai, jo dikhata hai ki wo ek doosre par equal magnitude ke forces lagate hain.

Forces hamesha pairs mein aate hain, lekin wo do different objects par act karte hain, isliye wo ek doosre ko cancel nahi karte. Same object par act karne wale do equal aur opposite forces ek doosre ko balance karte hain. Ye law contact aur non-contact forces dono ke liye valid hai: do magnets ke beech magnetic forces, do charged balloons ke beech electrostatic forces, aur Earth aur ek fruit ke beech gravitational forces—sab equal aur opposite pairs mein aate hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Earth aur ek fruit ek doosre par equal aur opposite gravitational forces (bal) lagate hain. Phir fruit Earth ki taraf kyun move karta hai, jabki Earth fruit ki taraf move hota hua nahi dikhta?

  1. Forces equal hain Newton's third law ke anusar, Earth dwara fruit par lagaya gaya force aur fruit dwara Earth par lagaya gaya force magnitude mein equal aur direction mein opposite hain.
  2. Lekin accelerations equal nahi hain a = F/m ka use karke, har body ko milne wala acceleration (tvaran) uske apne mass par depend karta hai. Earth ka mass fruit ki tulna mein itna zyada hai ki is force se Earth ka acceleration bahut hi small hota hai.
  3. Hum kya notice karte hain Fruit ka acceleration dekhne ke liye kaafi large hai, jabki Earth par effect itna small hai ki notice nahi hota.

Uttar (Answer) Dono forces magnitude mein equal hain, lekin Earth ka enormous mass uske acceleration ko extremely small bana deta hai, isliye uska motion (gati) notice nahi hota.

Solved example (Worked example)

Jab 0.1 kg ki bullet ko 5 kg ke gun se 2 N ke force (bal) se fire kiya jata hai, to gun recoil karta hai. Bullet aur gun ke initial accelerations (tvaran) ke magnitudes kya hain?

  1. Recoil force Newton's third law ke anusar, gun par 2 N ka recoil force lagta hai, jo bullet par lage force ke magnitude ke barabar hai.
  2. Gun ka acceleration a = F/m = 2 N / 5 kg = 0.4 m s⁻².
  3. Bullet ka acceleration a = F/m = 2 N / 0.1 kg = 20 m s⁻².

Uttar (Answer) Gun ka initial acceleration 0.4 m s⁻² hai aur bullet ka 20 m s⁻². Forces ka pair magnitude mein equal hai, lekin accelerations equal nahi hain kyunki masses different hain.

Ek fireperson kabhi-kabhi paani nikaal rahe pipe ko hold karte waqt struggle kyun karta hai?

Kyon?

Pipe paani ko forward push karta hai; Newton ke third law ke according paani pipe ko equal force ke saath backwards push karta hai. Pipe ko steady rakhne ke liye fireperson ko opposite force dena padta hai.

Ek spacecraft space ke aise region mein move kar raha hai jahan us par lagne wala gravitational force negligible hai. Suggest kijiye ki woh apni velocity kaise change kar sakta hai.

Kyon?

Velocity sirf tab change hoti hai jab koi force act karti hai. Negligible gravity ke saath, spacecraft gas ko expel karke apni khud ki force banata hai; gas equal aur opposite force ke saath spacecraft par push back karti hai.

Ek sailor chhoti boat se shore par jump karta hai. Jab sailor aage (forward) jump karta hai, to kya boat move karegi? Agar haan, to kis direction mein aur kyun?

Kyon?

Jump ek third-law pair hai: sailor boat ko backwards push karta hai, aur boat sailor ko forwards push karti hai. Backward force boat par act karta hai, isliye boat backwards move karti hai.

Teen laws ek single object ke liye use kiye gaye the, lekin ye do ya usse zyada connected objects par bhi lagti hain.

Do boxes lijiye jinke masses m₁ aur m₂ hain, ek frictionless horizontal surface par, jo ek string se connected hain, aur ek force (bal) F Box 1 ko right side pull kar raha hai. Box 1 string ke through Box 2 ko pull karta hai, aur Newton's third law ke according, Box 2 Box 1 ko equal aur opposite force se back pull karta hai. String mein is force ko tension T kehte hain.

Aap har box par net force alag-alag nikaal sakte hain. Ek simpler tarika hai do boxes aur string ko ek hi system maanna. System ke andar ke forces, jaise tension, internal forces hote hain aur unhe consider karne ki zaroorat nahi; sirf bahar se lagne wale forces, jaise F, matter karte hain.

Tab system ek single object jiska mass m₁ + m₂ hai, ki tarah behave karta hai, isliye iska acceleration (tvaran) a = F/(m₁ + m₂) hai. F ke alawa, external forces gravitational force (m₁ + m₂)g hain jo downwards act karta hai, aur ise ground se lagne wala normal force (N₁ + N₂) balance karta hai.

Do connected boxes ka acceleration (tvaran) nikalna

Sirf bahari forces (bal) ko count karo

Do boxes aur string ko ek system maano. Tension dono boxes par system ke andar act karta hai, isliye ye internal force hai; pulling force F hi sirf horizontal external force hai.

Poore system par second law use karo

System ka mass m₁ + m₂ hai, isliye Newton's second law se F = (m₁ + m₂)a milta hai.

Acceleration ke liye solve karo

Dono sides ko (m₁ + m₂) se divide karne par a = F/(m₁ + m₂) milta hai. System ek single object ki tarah accelerate karta hai jiska total mass utna hi hai.

Vertical forces ko check karo

Neeche ki gravitational force (m₁ + m₂)g aur upar ki normal force (N₁ + N₂) barabar aur opposite hain, isliye ye horizontal acceleration ko affect nahi karte.

Do boxes jinke masses m₁ aur m₂ hain, ek smooth horizontal surface par ek string se jude hue hain aur pehle box par lagaye gaye horizontal force (bal) F se kheenche ja rahe hain. System ka acceleration (tvaran) kya hai?

Kyon?

Boxes aur string ko ek system maano. Tension internal hai aur cancel ho jata hai, sirf external horizontal force F hai, aur total mass m₁ + m₂ hai, isliye a = F/(m₁ + m₂).

Snake boat race ki practice karte samay, 100 oarsmen ek boat ko mil kar row karte hain. Unme se 95 boat ko aage badhane ke liye peeche ki taraf row karte hain, lekin galti se 5 oarsmen ulti direction mein row karte hain. Agar har oarsman 200 N ka horizontal force (bal) lagata hai, to snake boat par net force kitna hai, newtons mein? (Drag forces aur air friction ko ignore karein.) Sirf number dijiye.

Kyon?

95 oarsmen aage ki taraf push karte hain aur 5 peeche ki taraf, isliye effective number 95 − 5 = 90 hai. Net force 90 × 200 N = 18000 N hai, forward direction mein.

Solved example (Worked example)

50 g mass ki ek bullet 100 m s⁻¹ speed (chaal) se chalte hue ek heavy stationary wooden block mein enters karti hai aur 50 cm distance penetrate karne ke baad ruk jaati hai. Maan lijiye ki bullet block ke andar constant acceleration (tvaran) se chalti hai. Bullet par lagne wale stopping force (bal) ka estimate kijiye.

  1. Jo aapko pata hai use SI units mein likhiye Mass 50 g hai, yani 0.05 kg. Penetration distance 50 cm hai, yani 0.5 m. Saath hi u = 100 m s⁻¹ aur v = 0 m s⁻¹ hai. Maan lijiye block ke andar constant acceleration a hai aur laga time t hai.
  2. Velocity relation ka use kijiye v = u + at se: 0 = 100 + a t, isliye t = −100/a.
  3. Displacement (visthapan) relation mein substitute kijiye s = ut + ½at² se: 0.5 = 100t + ½at². t = −100/a rakhne par 0.5 = −10000/a + 5000/a = −5000/a milta hai, isliye a = −10000 m s⁻².
  4. Stopping force nikaliye Force ka magnitude F = ma = 0.05 kg × 10000 m s⁻² = 500 N hai, jo bullet ki motion ke opposite act karta hai.

Uttar (Answer) Stopping force lagbhag 500 N hai, jo bullet ki motion ki direction ke opposite hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

2 kg mass ka ek object constant velocity (veg) 10 m s⁻¹ se chal raha hai; usse ek rough patch milta hai jahan object par friction force (bal) 7 N hai. Usi samay, motion (gati) ke opposing ek additional constant force 3 N object par lagaya jata hai. Rough patch mein enter karne ke baad, object rest mein aane se pehle kitni distance (doori) travel karta hai?

  1. Kul opposing force Dono forces motion ka oppose karte hain, isliye add hote hain: 7 N + 3 N = 10 N peeche ki taraf.
  2. Acceleration (tvaran) a = F/m = 10 N / 2 kg = 5 m s⁻², motion ke opposite directed. Motion ko positive maante hue, a = −5 m s⁻².
  3. Rukne mein laga time v = u + at se: 0 = 10 + (−5)t, isliye t = 2 s.
  4. Distance travelled (doori) s = ut + ½at² se: s = 10 × 2 + ½ × (−5) × (2)² = 20 − 10 = 10 m.

Uttar (Answer) Object rest mein aane se pehle 10 m travel karta hai.

Ek force (bal), do loads: accelerations (tvaran) ko ek formula mein combine karna

Sirf harrow

Tractor mass m₁ ko net force F se kheenchta hai aur acceleration a₁ paida karta hai, isliye F = m₁a₁ aur isliye m₁ = F/a₁.

Sirf trolley

Wahi force F mass m₂ par acceleration a₂ paida karta hai, isliye F = m₂a₂ aur isliye m₂ = F/a₂.

Trolley par harrow

Ab tractor usi force F se trolley ko kheenchta hai jis par harrow rakha hai. Inhe ek hi system maankar total mass m₁ + m₂ hai aur acceleration a = F/(m₁ + m₂) hai.

Substitute karke simplify karna

m₁ = F/a₁ aur m₂ = F/a₂ ko expression mein rakho: a = F / (F/a₁ + F/a₂). F cancel karo: a = 1 / (1/a₁ + 1/a₂) = a₁a₂ / (a₁ + a₂).

Ek footballer 0.4 kg mass ki football ko kick karta hai aur usse 108 km h⁻¹ ki speed (chaal) deta hai. Estimated force (bal) 800 N tha. Foot aur ball ke beech contact time seconds mein calculate karo. Sirf number do.

Kyon?

Pehle speed convert karo: 108 km h⁻¹ = 108 × 1000 m / 3600 s = 30 m s⁻¹. Phir a = F/m = 800 N / 0.4 kg = 2000 m s⁻². Ball rest se start hoti hai, isliye v = u + at se: 30 = 0 + 2000t, jisse t = 30/2000 = 0.015 s.

Jab ek bar magnet ka pole magnetic compass ke paas laaya jaata hai, to bar magnet aur compass needle ek doosre par equal aur opposite magnetic forces lagate hain. Phir bhi compass needle move karti hai aur bar magnet move nahi karta. Kyun?

Kyon?

Do different masses par equal forces different accelerations dete hain, kyunki a = F/m. Needle ka small mass use visible acceleration deta hai jabki magnet ki acceleration negligible hai.

High jump event mein, athlete ke girne ke liye landing mat ya sand bed rakha jaata hai. Yeh injury ka risk kyun kam karta hai?

Kyon?

Same change of velocity (veg) ke liye, lamba stopping time chhota acceleration deta hai; phir F = ma se athlete par chhota force milta hai, jaise vehicle mein airbag karta hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

force
Force (bal) ek aisa push ya pull hai jo kisi object ko move karna shuru kar sakta hai, uski speed ya direction badal sakta hai, ya uski shape badal sakta hai; iska magnitude, direction aur unit newton (N) hota hai.
balanced forces
Do forces jo magnitude mein equal aur direction mein opposite hain, taaki object move na kare.
net force
Saari forces add karne ke baad bachi single force: agar same direction mein hain toh sum, agar opposite hain toh difference. Motion sirf net force decide karti hai.
normal force
Kisi surface par rest kar rahe object par us surface ka upward push, surface ke perpendicular.
inertia
Kisi object ki tendency jo uske rest ya uniform motion ki state mein kisi bhi change ko resist karti hai.
tension
Do objects ko jodne wali string dwara carry ki gayi force; ye har object ko opposite direction mein pull karti hai.
newton
Force ka SI unit: woh force jo 1 kg object ko 1 m s⁻² ka acceleration deti hai.
