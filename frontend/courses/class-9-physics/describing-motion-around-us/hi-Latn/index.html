Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is paath ke baad aap ek seedhi line mein chalne wale object ki position, distance (doori) aur displacement (visthapan) describe kar sakte hain.
  • Is paath ke baad aap average speed (chaal), average velocity aur average acceleration calculate kar sakte hain aur unhe graphs se find kar sakte hain.
  • Is paath ke baad aap constant acceleration ke liye kinematic equations use kar sakte hain aur uniform circular motion ko explain kar sakte hain.

Hamare aas-paas sab kuch motion (gati) mein hai, butterflies aur horses se lekar clouds aur tides tak. Is variety ko simply study karne ke liye, scientists pehle idealised forms jaise linear motion ka study karte hain.

Linear motion seedhi line mein motion hai. Yah sabse simple kind ka motion hai. Examples hain: vertically girta hua ball, seedhi highway par car, aur seedhi track par train.

Position ko describe karne ke liye hum pehle ek reference point fix karte hain. Us point se distance (doori) aur direction, kisi bhi instant par, position ko describe karte hain.

Agar position time ke saath badalti hai, to object motion (gati) mein hai. Agar position time ke saath nahi badalti, to object rest par hai. Ek straight line par, O ke right positive hai aur O ke left negative hai.

Ek object ko motion (gati) mein kaha jata hai jab uska position reference point ke respect mein time ke saath change hota hai. Aapke aage chal rahe truck ke liye iska kya matlab hai?

Kyon?

Motion ka judgement position ke change se hota hai reference point ke respect mein, sirf speed (chaal) se nahi. Distance aur direction milke position ko describe karte hain.

Ek athlete O se start karta hai, B tak pahunchta hai, phir A tak, phir B tak wapas daudta hai. Total distance (doori) travelled OA + AB = 100 m + 60 m = 160 m hai.

Start aur stop ke beech displacement (visthapan) OB = 40 m positive direction mein hai. Jab object wapas palat jata hai, tab total distance aur magnitude of displacement barabar nahi hote.

Displacement ko direction aur magnitude dono chahiye. Total distance ko sirf ek number chahiye. Dono ka SI unit metre hai.

Ek ball ko O se vertically upwards throw kiya jata hai, wo B tak jaati hai aur wapas O par gir jaati hai. Displacement (visthapan) ke baare mein kaun sa statement true hai?

Kyon?

Jab ball wapas O par aati hai, displacement (visthapan) zero hota hai jabki total distance zero nahi hota. Displacement ka magnitude kabhi bhi total distance se zyada nahi ho sakta, aur yeh total distance ke equal tabhi hota hai jab motion sirf ek direction mein ho.

Average speed total distance (doori) travelled divided by time interval hoti hai. Yeh batati hai ki object kitni speed se move karta hai aur iski koi direction nahi hoti.

Equal distances in equal intervals ka matlab hai uniform motion (eksamaan gati) at constant speed. Unequal distances ka matlab hai non-uniform motion with increasing ya decreasing speed.

Solved example (Worked example)

Do postmen 210 yojanas ki distance (doori) se ek dusre ki taraf chalna shuru karte hain. Ek 9 yojanas per day chalta hai aur dusra 5 yojanas per day cover karta hai. Ve kitne days mein milenge?

  1. Ek din mein saath mein cover ki gayi distance 9 yojanas + 5 yojanas = 14 yojanas per day.
  2. 210 yojanas cover karne ka time 210 / 14 = 15 days.
  3. Distances check karo Pehla postman 9 x 15 = 135 yojanas. Dusra postman 5 x 15 = 75 yojanas. Total 210 yojanas.

Uttar (Answer) Ve 15 days ke baad milenge.

Average velocity displacement (visthapan) ko time interval se divide karke milti hai. Ise v_av = s/t ke roop mein likha jata hai.

Iski direction displacement ke same hoti hai aur plus ya minus sign se dikhayi jati hai. Iski SI unit m s-1 hai, aur ise km h-1 mein bhi measure kiya jata hai. Yeh time ke saath position ke change ka average rate hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Sarang ko swimming pool mein ek end se doosre end tak swim karke wapas aane mein 50 seconds lagte hain. 50 s mein uska average speed (ausat chaal) aur average velocity (ausat veg) nikaalo.

  1. Total distance (kul doori) Tay ki gayi total distance = 50 m.
  2. Displacement (visthapan) Wah start point par wapas aa jata hai, isliye displacement = 0 m.
  3. Average speed (ausat chaal) 50 m / 50 s = 1 m s-1.
  4. Average velocity (ausat veg) 0 m / 50 s = 0 m s-1.

Uttar (Answer) Average speed 1 m s-1, average velocity 0 m s-1.

Aap teen ghante mein 200 km north drive karte hain, phir do ghante mein 200 km south. Poore trip ke liye average speed kya hai?

Kyon?

Total distance (doori) = 400 km. Total time = 5 h. Average speed (ausat chaal) = 400 / 5 = 80 km h-1. Displacement (visthapan) zero hai, isliye average velocity (ausat veg) zero hai.

Average acceleration velocity (veg) ke change ko time interval se divide karke nikaala jaata hai. Yeh (v - u) / (t2 - t1) hai aur iska SI unit m s-2 hai.

Agar velocity ka magnitude badhta hai, to acceleration (tvaran) velocity ki direction mein hota hai. Agar magnitude ghatta hai, to acceleration velocity ke opposite hota hai.

Acceleration magnitude mein change, direction mein change, ya dono se aa sakta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek bus velocity (veg) 36 km h-1 se chalti hai. Driver 10 s tak accelerator dabata hai aur velocity badhkar 54 km h-1 ho jaati hai. Phir brakes dabane par bus 5 s mein ruk jaati hai. Dono intervals mein average acceleration nikaalo.

  1. Velocities ko convert karo 36 km h-1 = 10 m s-1. 54 km h-1 = 15 m s-1.
  2. Accelerator interval a = (15 - 10) / 10 = 0.5 m s-2 velocity ki direction mein.
  3. Brake interval a = (0 - 15) / 5 = -3 m s-2. Minus sign ka matlab velocity ke opposite.

Uttar (Answer) (i) 0.5 m s-2 velocity ki direction mein. (ii) -3 m s-2 velocity ke opposite.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek object ko height se drop kiya jaata hai; uski velocities 0 s par 0 m s-1, 1 s par 9.8 m s-1, 2 s par 19.6 m s-1, 3 s par 29.4 m s-1 aur 4 s par 39.2 m s-1 hain. Har 1 s interval mein average acceleration (ausat tvaran) nikaalo.

  1. 0 s se 1 s (9.8 - 0) / 1 = 9.8 m s-2.
  2. 1 s se 2 s, 2 s se 3 s, 3 s se 4 s Har interval se (19.6-9.8)/1, (29.4-19.6)/1 aur (39.2-29.4)/1 = 9.8 m s-2 milta hai.
  3. Nishkarsh Acceleration motion ki direction mein constant 9.8 m s-2 hai. Yah g hai, acceleration due to gravity.

Uttar (Answer) Motion ki direction mein constant average acceleration 9.8 m s-2.

Ek scooter ka speedometer reading constant hai. Kya scooter phir bhi accelerating ho sakta hai?

Kyon?

Acceleration (tvaran) tab hota hai jab velocity (veg) ka magnitude ya direction ya dono badalte hain. Circular motion mein speed constant hoti hai lekin direction badalti hai, isliye acceleration non-zero hota hai.

Graphs dikhate hain ki position (sthan), velocity (veg) ya acceleration (tvaran) time ke saath kaise badalte hain. Ye motions ko compare karne aur quantities nikalne mein madad karte hain.

Plot karne ke liye, origin O se X aur Y axes draw karein, X-axis par time aur Y-axis par position chunein, ek suitable scale chunein, points mark karein aur unhe join karein.

Position-time graph plot karna

Axes draw karo

Graph paper par perpendicular lines OX aur OY draw karo jo origin O par milti hain.

Scale chuno

Table 4.3 ke liye X-axis par 5 divisions = 1 s aur Y-axis par 5 divisions = 20 m use karo.

Points mark karo

0 s par position 0 m origin hai. 1 s par position 20 m, 2 s par 40 m, aur isi tarah 6 s par 120 m tak.

Points join karo

Points ko join karne se constant velocity (veg) data ke liye straight line milti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek vehicle ke liye jo rest se start karke speed up ho raha hai, positions 0 m at 0 s, 1 m at 2 s, 4 m at 4 s, 9 m at 6 s, 16 m at 8 s, 25 m at 10 s aur 36 m at 12 s hain. Position-time graph plot karein.

  1. Scale chunein X-axis: 5 divisions = 2 s, Y-axis: 5 divisions = 5 m.
  2. Points mark karein Table se har time-position pair ko mark karein.
  3. Points ko join karein Points ek straight line par nahi aate. Curve se join karein.

Uttar (Answer) Graph ek curve hai, jo dikhata hai ki velocity (veg) badh rahi hai.

Position-time graph aur velocity

Constant velocity ke liye position = velocity x time equation se position-time graph banaya jata hai. Velocity badalne se line ki steepness badalti hai.

Ek seedhi line wala position-time graph constant velocity (veg) ko darshata hai. Ek curved graph ka matlab hai ki velocity constant nahi hai.

Time axis ke parallel ek line ka matlab hai ki position nahi badal rahi, isliye object rest par hai. Ek steeper line ka matlab hai higher velocity.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek position-time graph dikhata hai ki ek vehicle origin se 40 m par hai aur uska position time ke saath change nahi ho raha. Yah kya darshata hai?

  1. Position padho Position har time par 40 m par hi rehti hai.
  2. Line interpret karo Line time axis ke parallel hai.

Uttar (Answer) Vehicle origin se 40 m par rest (viram) mein hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Do objects A aur B ke position-time graphs diye gaye hain. Kis object ki average velocity ka magnitude zyada hai?

  1. Same time mein displacement (visthapan) compare karo Same time interval ke liye axes ke parallel lines draw karo.
  2. Slope compare karo Same time mein B ka displacement A se zyada hai, isliye B ka slope steeper hai.

Uttar (Answer) B ki velocity A ki velocity se zyada hai.

Position-time slope (dhaal) se velocity (veg)

Graph ka part AB lo

A aur B se axes ke parallel lines kheencho taaki triangle ABC bane.

Sides identify karo

BC position mein change s2 - s1 hai. CA time mein change t2 - t1 hai.

Divide karo

Velocity = BC / CA = (s2 - s1)/(t2 - t1). Example (80 - 40)/(4 - 2) = 20 m s-1.

Velocity-time graph samay ke saath velocity (veg) mein change dikhata hai. Constant velocity X-axis ke parallel ek line deti hai aur zero acceleration (tvaran) deti hai.

Straight line ke saath velocity badhne par velocity ki direction mein constant acceleration milta hai. Velocity ghatne par velocity ke opposite constant acceleration milta hai.

Velocity-time graph ka slope (dhaal) aur area

Velocity-time graph initial velocity (veg) aur constant acceleration (tvaran) se compute hota hai. Slope acceleration deta hai aur line ke neeche area displacement (visthapan) deta hai.

Velocity (veg)-time graph se slope (dhaal) aur area

Acceleration (tvaran) ke liye slope

Acceleration = velocity mein change / time mein change. 10 s aur 20 s ke beech, (10 - 5)/10 = 0.5 m s-2. Decreasing graph ke liye ye -0.5 m s-2 hota hai.

Displacement (visthapan) ke liye area, constant velocity

Rectangle ka area = velocity x time. 20 m s-1 x 6 s = 120 m.

Displacement ke liye area, changing velocity

10 s aur 20 s ke beech area = rectangle + triangle = 50 m + 25 m = 75 m.

Kinematic equations constant acceleration (tvaran) ke saath motion (gati) ko describe karte hain. Har instant par acceleration average acceleration ke barabar hota hai.

Paanch quantities hain: displacement (visthapan) s, time (samay) t, initial velocity (prarambhik veg) u, final velocity (antim veg) v aur acceleration a. Known quantities ke liye fit hone wala equation choose karein.

Kinematic equations derive karna

Velocity (veg) equation

a = (v - u)/t se hume at = v - u milta hai, isliye v = u + at.

Displacement (visthapan) equation

Displacement velocity-time graph ke neeche area hota hai: rectangle + triangle se s = ut + 1/2 at^2 milta hai.

Velocity squared equation

Dono equations ke beech t eliminate karke v^2 = u^2 + 2as milta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek car acceleration (tvaran) -4 m s-2 ke saath brake karti hai. Rukne se pehle kitni distance (doori) tay karegi agar woh (i) 54 km h-1 aur (ii) 108 km h-1 se chal rahi thi?

  1. Velocities ko convert karo (i) 54 km h-1 = 15 m s-1. (ii) 108 km h-1 = 30 m s-1. Final velocity (antim veg) v = 0.
  2. v^2 = u^2 + 2as use karo 0 = u^2 + 2 x (-4) x s, isliye s = u^2 / 8.
  3. Calculate karo (i) 225/8 = 28.1 m. (ii) 900/8 = 112.5 m.

Uttar (Answer) (i) 28.1 m (ii) 112.5 m. Zyada initial speed (chaal) ke liye kaafi zyada stopping distance (doori) chahiye.

Kaun sa pair remaining two kinematic equations ko v = u + at aur s = ut + 1/2 at^2 se derive karta hai?

Kyon?

Dusra equation velocity-time graph ke neeche trapezium ke area se aata hai, jo s = (u + v)/2 t deta hai. Pehla substitution se follow hota hai.

Motion (gati) in a plane do dimensions mein motion hai. Iske examples hain: ek kicked ball, ek satellite, aur ek vehicle jo dusre ko overtake kar raha hai.

Uniform circular motion ek circular path mein constant speed (chaal) ke saath motion hai. Ek revolution mein distance (doori) circumference hota hai, displacement (visthapan) zero hota hai, average speed 2 pi R / T hoti hai aur average velocity zero hoti hai.

Uniform circular motion

Ek point circle par constant speed (chaal) se move karta hai. Uska velocity (veg) arrow hamesha tangent ke along hota hai aur direction continuously badalta rehta hai.

Kisi point par velocity (veg) circle ke tangent ke along hoti hai, motion ki direction mein. Ek tangent circle se sirf ek hi point par milta hai.

Jab ek ring ke andar moving marble ko release kiya jata hai, toh woh release ke instant par ki direction mein straight line mein move karta hai.

Uniform circular motion ek idealised model hai. Yeh Sun ke around planets aur circular turns lene wale vehicles ke liye useful hai.

Ek aadmi ghar se 250 m door ek dukaan tak jaata hai, ghar laut aata hai, phir dukaan jaata hai aur ghar laut aata hai. Ghar se total distance (doori) aur displacement (visthapan) kya hai?

Kyon?

250 m ki chaar yatraein total distance 1000 m deti hain. Woh ghar par hi khatam hota hai, isliye position mein net change 0 hai.

Ek car rest se start hoti hai aur uski velocity 6 s mein 24 m s-1 tak pahunchti hai. Average acceleration aur 6 s mein tay ki gayi distance (doori) nikaalo.

Kyon?

a = (24 - 0)/6 = 4 m s-2. s = ut + 1/2 at^2 = 0 + 0.5 x 4 x 36 = 72 m.

Ek motorbike initial velocity (veg) 28 m s-1 aur constant acceleration (tvaran) ke saath move kar rahi hai aur 98 m travel karne ke baad ruk jaati hai. Acceleration aur rukne ka time nikaalo.

Kyon?

0 = 28^2 + 2 x a x 98 se a = -4 m s-2 milta hai. Time = (0 - 28)/(-4) = 7 s.

Ek truck 54 km h-1 se 36 km h-1 tak 36 s mein constant acceleration (tvaran) ke saath slow hota hai. Yeh kitna distance (doori) travel karta hai?

Kyon?

15 m s-1 aur 10 m s-1 mein convert karo. Average velocity = 12.5 m s-1. Distance (doori) = 12.5 x 36 = 450 m.

Ek car rest se uniformly accelerate karke 5 s mein 20 m s-1 tak pahunchti hai, phir 10 s tak 20 m s-1 se travel karti hai, uske baad uniformly brake karke 6 s mein stop hoti hai. Total distance (doori) nikaalo.

Kyon?

Phase 1: a = 4 m s-2, s = 50 m. Phase 2: s = 200 m. Phase 3: a = -3.33 m s-2, s = 60 m. Total distance = 310 m.

Velocity (veg)-time graph par, graph aur time axis ke beech ka area kya represent karta hai?

Kyon?

Area = velocity x time constant velocity ke liye, aur badalti velocity ke liye shape ka area. Dono us time interval mein displacement dete hain.

Earth Sun ke around move karti hai. Kya Earth par rakha koi object rest mein mana ja sakta hai?

Kyon?

Rest aur motion (gati) reference point par depend karte hain. Earth ke respect mein object rest mein hai, chahe Earth Sun ke around move karti hai.

Do objects ke position-time graphs par 10 s mein initial aur final positions same hain. 10 s ke over unki average velocity (ausat veg) ke baare mein aap kya keh sakte hain?

Kyon?

Average velocity = displacement / time. Same initial aur final positions same displacement dete hain, aur time bhi same hai, isliye average velocities equal hain. Average speed total distance par depend karti hai, jo alag ho sakta hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

displacement
Do instants ke beech position mein net change, jisme magnitude aur direction dono hote hain.
average velocity
Displacement ko time interval se divide karke nikalte hain.
average acceleration
Velocity ke change ko time interval se divide karke nikalte hain.
slope
Graph line ki steepness, jo rate of change deti hai.
uniform circular motion
Circle mein constant speed ke saath motion, jisme velocity ki direction change hoti rehti hai.
tangent
Ek line jo circle ko sirf ek point par touch karti hai.
