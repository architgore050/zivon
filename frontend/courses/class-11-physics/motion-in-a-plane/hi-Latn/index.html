Motion in a Plane

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap bata sakte hain ki koi quantity scalar hai ya vector, aur vectors ko graphically aur components se add kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap plane mein position, velocity aur acceleration ko components se aur differentiation se nikaal sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap is chapter ke equations use karke constant-acceleration, projectile aur uniform circular motion ke problems solve kar sakte hain.

Aap ek ball phenkte hain aur wo ek hi samay mein sideways aur up dono taraf move karti hai. Ek plus ya minus sign us sideways-plus-up direction ko describe nahi kar sakta. Aapko vectors (sadish) chahiye, aisi quantities jo magnitude aur direction dono carry karti hain.

Is lesson mein aap vectors ki language seekhenge, phir ise plane mein position, velocity (veg) aur acceleration (tvaran) ke liye use karenge. Uske baad aap constant-acceleration motion (gati), projectile motion aur uniform circular motion solve karenge.

Scalar (adish) mein sirf magnitude hota hai aur ise unit ke saath ek number se vyakt kiya jata hai. Distance (doori), mass, temperature aur time scalars hain. Vector (sadish) mein magnitude aur direction dono hote hain aur ye triangle ya parallelogram addition follow karta hai. Displacement (visthapan), velocity (veg), acceleration (tvaran) aur force (bal) vectors hain.

Origin O choose karein. Position vector OP equals r, jo O se P tak points karta hai. Agar object P se P prime tak move karta hai, to displacement PP prime start aur finish ko jodne wali straight line hai. Ye actual path par depend nahi karta, aur iska magnitude path length se less than ya equal hota hai.

Kaunsi list mein sirf vector (sadish) quantities hain?

Kyon?

Displacement (visthapan), velocity (veg) aur acceleration (tvaran) mein magnitude aur direction dono hote hain aur ye vector addition follow karte hain. Baaki lists scalar (adish) hain.

A ko positive lambda se multiply karne par same direction mein magnitude lambda times |A| milta hai. -lambda se multiply karne par direction reverse ho jaati hai. Velocity (veg) ko time se multiply karne par displacement (visthapan) milta hai.

Do vector (sadish) tabhi equal hote hain jab unka magnitude aur direction match kare. Kisi free vector ko khud ke parallel shift karne se woh unchanged rehta hai. Null vector ka magnitude zero hota hai aur koi direction nahi hoti, aur A plus 0 equals A.

Head-to-tail method: B ka tail A ke head par rakho, A ke tail ko B ke head se jodo taaki R mile. Parallelogram method: dono tails ko common origin par lao aur parallelogram complete karo; R diagonal hai. Dono se same R milta hai.

Addition commutative aur associative hai. Subtraction A minus B equals A plus (-B). A aur -A ko add karne se null vector milta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Rain (barish) vertically gir rahi hai jiski speed (chaal) 35 m s-1 hai. Wind 12 m s-1 ki speed se east to west direction mein chalna shuru karti hai. Boy apna umbrella kis direction mein rakhe?

  1. Velocities ko represent karo Maan lo vr rain ki velocity (veg) vertically neeche hai aur vw wind ki velocity east se west direction mein hai. Resultant R unka vector (sadish) sum hai.
  2. Magnitude R = sqrt(vr squared + vw squared) = sqrt(35 squared + 12 squared) = 37 m s-1.
  3. Direction tan theta = vw / vr = 12 / 35 = 0.343, isliye theta = tan inverse 0.343 = 19 degrees vertical se.

Uttar (Answer) Umbrella ko vertical plane mein vertical se lagbhag 19 degrees east ki taraf rakho, jaise source mein diya hai.

Scalar aur vector quantities ke saath kaunsa algebraic operation aam taur par meaningful hota hai?

Kyon?

Vector ko scalar se multiply karne par scaled magnitude wala vector milta hai. Vector mein scalar add karna, ya different units wale scalars ko add karna, meaningful nahi hai.

Plane mein ek vector (sadish) A ko do non-parallel directions ke along resolve kiya ja sakta hai. Rectangular axes use karte hue, unit vectors i, j, k ka magnitude 1 hota hai aur ye x, y, z axes ke along point karte hain.

A equals Ax i plus Ay j likhiye. Ax aur Ay scalar (adish) hain; Ax i aur Ay j vectors hain. Teen dimensions mein Az k add kijiye. Position vector r equals x i plus y j plus z k hota hai.

Magnitude-angle se components tak aur wapas

A aur theta diye hue hon

Ax = A cos theta and Ay = A sin theta, component diagram par trigonometry se.

Ax aur Ay diye hue hon

Pythagoras se A = sqrt(Ax squared + Ay squared), aur tan theta = Ay / Ax.

Signs

Ek component theta ke according positive, negative ya zero ho sakta hai, kyunki cos aur sin quadrant ke hisaab se sign badalte hain.

Analytical addition: A equals Ax i plus Ay j aur B equals Bx i plus By j likhein. Phir R equals (Ax plus Bx) i plus (Ay plus By) j. Har resultant component corresponding components ka sum hota hai.

Teen dimensions mein z components ko usi tarah add karein. Yeh kisi bhi number ke vectors (sadish) tak extend hota hai, aur subtraction negative ke addition ke roop mein hota hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Do vector (sadish) A aur B ke resultant ka magnitude aur direction unke magnitudes aur unke beech ke angle theta ke terms mein nikaliye.

  1. Parallelogram geometry Maan lijiye OS resultant R hai. ON = A + B cos theta aur SN = B sin theta, aur OS squared = ON squared + SN squared.
  2. Magnitude R squared = (A + B cos theta) squared + (B sin theta) squared = A squared + B squared + 2AB cos theta.
  3. Direction R sin alpha = B sin theta, isliye sin alpha = B sin theta / R, jahan alpha R aur A ke beech ka angle hai.

Uttar (Answer) R squared = A squared + B squared + 2AB cos theta; direction sin alpha = B sin theta / R se milti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek motorboat north ki taraf 25 km/h ki speed (chaal) se ja rahi hai aur water current 10 km/h hai, south se 60 degrees east ki direction mein. Boat ka resultant velocity (veg) nikaliye.

  1. Vectors (sadish) draw kijiye vb 25 km/h north hai, vc 10 km/h hai south se 60 degrees east par. Parallelogram mein unke beech ka angle 120 degrees hai.
  2. Law of cosines se magnitude R squared = 25 squared + 10 squared + 2 x 25 x 10 x cos 120 degrees, jisse R approx 21.8 km/h milta hai.
  3. Law of sines se direction sin f = vc sin 120 degrees / R = 10 x sin 120 degrees / 21.8 = 0.397, isliye f approx 23.4 degrees.

Uttar (Answer) Resultant speed (chaal) lagbhag 21.8 km/h hai, direction source figure mein f = 23.4 degrees se di gayi hai.

Vector (sadish) inequalities mein |a + b| = |a| + |b| equality ke roop mein kab hold karta hai?

Kyon?

Geometrically triangle inequality equality tabhi hoti hai jab vectors same direction mein collinear hote hain. Agar a+b+c+d = 0 to |a+c| = |b+d| kyunki a+c = -(b+d).

Position (sthiti) r equals x i plus y j hota hai. Displacement (visthapan) delta r equals r prime minus r equals delta x i plus delta y j hota hai. Average velocity (ausat veg) delta r over delta t hoti hai.

Instantaneous velocity (taatkalik veg) v equals dr over dt hoti hai, jo path ke tangent hoti hai. Acceleration (tvaran) a equals dv over dt hota hai. Components (ghatak) vx equals dx over dt, vy equals dy over dt, ax equals dvx over dt, ay equals dvy over dt hote hain.

Velocity aur acceleration limits ke roop mein

Average velocity

v average = delta r / delta t; iska direction delta r ke direction mein hota hai, jo path ka chord hai.

Instantaneous velocity

Jab delta t zero ki taraf tend karta hai, chord tangent ban jata hai; v = limit delta r / delta t = dr/dt.

Acceleration (tvaran)

a average = delta v / delta t; jab delta t zero ki taraf tend karta hai, a = dv/dt. 2D mein, a, v ke saath 0 to 180 degrees tak koi bhi angle bana sakta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek particle ki position coefficients 3.0, 2.0 aur 5.0 ke saath di gayi hai, t seconds mein aur r metres mein. v(t) aur a(t) nikalo, aur t = 1.0 s par v ka magnitude aur direction bhi nikalo.

  1. Differentiate karo Source result mein v = dr/dt = 3.0 i + 4.0 t j m/s; a = dv/dt = 4.0 j m/s squared.
  2. t = 1.0 s par v = 3.0 i + 4.0 j m/s.
  3. Magnitude aur direction Magnitude = sqrt(3 squared + 4 squared) = 5.0 m/s; theta = tan inverse (4/3) = 53 degrees, x-axis ke saath.

Uttar (Answer) a = 4.0 m s-2 y-direction ke along hai; 1.0 s par speed (chaal) 5.0 m/s hai, x-axis ke saath 53 degrees par.

Constant acceleration (tvaran) ke saath, average acceleration us constant ke barabar hota hai. t=0 par v0 se lekar t par v tak, v equals v0 plus a t hota hai. Position r equals r0 plus v0 t plus one half a t squared hoti hai.

x aur y ko alag-alag likho: vx equals vox plus ax t, x equals x0 plus vox t plus one half ax t squared, aur y ke liye bhi isi tarah. Do perpendicular motions (gatiyan) ko independently treat karo.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek particle origin se t = 0 par velocity (veg) 5.0 i m/s ke saath shuru hota hai aur x-y plane mein constant acceleration (tvaran) (3.0 i + 2.0 j) m/s squared ke under move karta hai. Jab x 84 m hai tab y kya hai, aur us samay speed (chaal) kya hai?

  1. Position x = 5.0 t + 1.5 t squared, y = 1.0 t squared, r = v0 t + one half a t squared se.
  2. t nikaalo 5.0 t + 1.5 t squared = 84 se t = 6 s milta hai. Phir y = 1.0 x 36 = 36.0 m.
  3. Velocity v = 5.0 i + 3.0 t i + 2.0 t j; 6 s par, vx = 23.0, vy = 12.0 m/s, speed = sqrt(23 squared + 12 squared) approx 26 m/s.

Uttar (Answer) t = 6 s par y = 36.0 m; speed approx 26 m/s.

Space mein arbitrary motion (gati) ke liye, kaun sa relation hamesha true hota hai?

Kyon?

Average velocity (average veg) ko time interval ke dauran displacement (visthapan) ke roop mein define kiya jata hai. Baaki forms sirf constant acceleration ke liye valid hain, aur a average = [v(t2)-v(t1)]/(t2-t1) acceleration ki definition hai.

Ek projectile throw hone ke baad flight mein hota hai, jaise ball. Iski motion (gati) do simultaneous components se banti hai: horizontal without acceleration (tvaran) aur vertical constant g downward ke saath. Air resistance ko neglect kiya gaya hai.

x ko horizontal aur y ko vertical up choose karo. Tab ax equals 0 aur ay equals -g. Initial components vox equals v0 cos theta0 aur voy equals v0 sin theta0 hain.

Projectile trajectory

Origin se launch kiya gaya projectile speed (chaal) v0 aur angle theta0 ke saath. Curve ko x = v0 cos theta0 t aur y = v0 sin theta0 t - 0.5 g t squared se compute kiya jata hai.

Projectile equations aur key results

Position aur velocity (veg)

x = v0 cos theta0 t, y = v0 sin theta0 t - 0.5 g t squared; vx = v0 cos theta0, vy = v0 sin theta0 - g t.

Path

t ko eliminate karo: y = x tan theta0 - g x squared / (2 v0 squared cos squared theta0), ek parabola.

Top aur flight

vy = 0 se tm = v0 sin theta0 / g milta hai; y=0 se Tf = 2 tm milta hai; tm substitute karne se hm = v0 squared sin squared theta0 / (2g) milta hai.

Range

R = v0 cos theta0 x Tf = v0 squared sin 2theta0 / g, maximum v0 squared / g 45 degrees par.

Solved example (Worked example)

Galileo ne kaha tha ki jin elevations ke liye 45 degrees se equal amount upar ya neeche jaate hain, unki ranges equal hoti hain. Is statement ko prove karein.

  1. Range formula Launch speed v0 ke liye R = v0 squared sin 2theta0 / g.
  2. Do angles 45 degrees + alpha aur 45 degrees - alpha ke liye, 2theta0 90 degrees + 2alpha aur 90 degrees - 2alpha hota hai.
  3. Sines equal sin(90 degrees + 2alpha) = sin(90 degrees - 2alpha) = cos 2alpha, isliye dono ke liye R same hai.

Uttar (Answer) Elevations 45 degrees plus or minus alpha ke liye ranges equal hoti hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek hiker zameen se 490 m upar cliff ke kinare par khada hai aur ek stone ko horizontally initial speed (chaal) 15 m s-1 se phenkta hai. Air resistance neglect karein, g = 9.8 m s-2 lein. Zameen tak time aur zameen par hit hone ke waqt speed nikaalein.

  1. Axes set karein Origin cliff edge par, x initial velocity (veg) ke along, y up ki taraf. vox = 15 m/s, voy = 0, ay = -9.8 m/s squared.
  2. Time Ground par jab y = -490 m: -490 = -0.5 x 9.8 x t squared, isse t = 10 s milta hai.
  3. Speed vx = 15 m/s, vy = -9.8 x 10 = -98 m/s, speed = sqrt(15 squared + 98 squared) approx 99 m/s.

Uttar (Answer) Time 10 s; zameen par hit hone par speed lagbhag 99 m/s.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek cricket ball ko horizontal ke upar 30 degrees ki direction mein 28 m s-1 speed se phenka jaata hai. Maximum height, same level par wapas aane ka time, aur us point tak distance (doori) calculate karein.

  1. Maximum height (adhiktam unchai) hm = v0 squared sin squared 30 degrees / (2g) = 28 squared x 0.25 / (2 x 9.8) = 10.0 m.
  2. Time of flight (udan samay) Tf = 2 v0 sin 30 degrees / g = 2 x 28 x 0.5 / 9.8 = 2.9 s.
  3. Range (doori) R = v0 squared sin 60 degrees / g = 28 squared x sin 60 degrees / 9.8 approx 69 m.

Uttar (Answer) Maximum height 10.0 m; time 2.9 s; distance (doori) 69 m.

Ek cricketer ball ko maximum horizontal distance 100 m tak throw kar sakta hai. Wahi ball ground se kitni height tak throw kar sakta hai?

Kyon?

Rmax = v0 squared / g = 100 m, isliye v0 squared = 100 g. Same v0 ke liye maximum height v0 squared / (2g) = 50 m hai. Ceiling height ko isi tarah limit karti hai kyunki woh vertical component ko limit karti hai.

Uniform circular motion ka matlab hai circle par constant speed (chaal) se chalna. Velocity (veg) ki direction continuously badalti hai, isliye acceleration (tvaran) hota hai aur centre ki taraf point karta hai.

Magnitude ac equals v squared over R hai. Direction har point par badalti hai, isliye acceleration constant vector (sadish) nahi hota, even though magnitude constant hai.

Angular speed (koniy chaal) aur centripetal acceleration

Angular speed

omega = delta theta / delta t, angular displacement (koniy visthapan) ke change ki rate.

Linear speed

Distance (doori) delta s = R delta theta, isliye v = delta s / delta t = R omega.

Acceleration ke forms

ac = v squared / R = omega squared R. Period T aur frequency n = 1/T ke saath, v = 2 pi R / T = 2 pi R n, omega = 2 pi n, ac = 4 pi squared n squared R.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek insect 12 cm radius ki circular groove mein phansa hua hai, steady move karta hai aur 100 s mein 7 revolutions complete karta hai. Angular speed, linear speed, aur acceleration (tvaran) ka magnitude find karo. Kya acceleration ek constant vector (sadish) hai?

  1. Angular speed omega = 2 pi x 7 / 100 = 0.44 rad/s.
  2. Linear speed v = omega R = 0.44 x 12 cm = 5.3 cm/s, circle ke tangent.
  3. Acceleration a = omega squared R = 0.44 squared x 12 = 2.3 cm/s squared centre ki taraf; direction badalta hai, isliye constant vector nahi hai.

Uttar (Answer) omega = 0.44 rad/s, v = 5.3 cm/s, a = 2.3 cm/s squared centre ki taraf, constant vector nahi hai.

Ek stone jo 80 cm lambi string se bandha hai, constant speed (chaal) se horizontal circle mein ghumaya jaata hai aur 25 s mein 14 revolutions karta hai. Acceleration (tvaran) ka magnitude aur direction kya hai?

Kyon?

omega = 2 pi x 14 / 25 = 3.52 rad/s; ac = omega squared R = 12.4 x 0.80 approx 9.9 m/s squared, centre ki taraf directed.

Ek aircraft 1.00 km radius ka horizontal loop constant speed (chaal) 900 km/h se karta hai. Iske centripetal acceleration ki g se tulna kijiye.

Kyon?

900 km/h = 250 m/s; ac = v squared / R = 250 squared / 1000 = 62.5 m/s squared; 9.8 se divide karne par lagbhag 6.4 g milta hai.

Path length actual path par depend karta hai; displacement (visthapan) sirf end points par depend karta hai. Ye dono tabhi equal hote hain jab direction nahi badalti. Isliye average speed (chaal) average velocity (veg) ke magnitude se greater than ya equal hoti hai.

Constant acceleration ke liye vector (sadish) equations mein axes choose karna zaruri nahi hota, lekin aap unhe kisi bhi do independent axes ke along resolve kar sakte hain. Trajectory initial position aur velocity (veg) ke saath-saath acceleration (tvaran) par bhi depend karti hai. Resultant velocity v1 plus v2 hoti hai, jabki relative velocity v12 common frame ke respect mein v1 minus v2 hoti hai.

Teen ladkiyan 200 m radius wale circular ground par P se shuru karti hain aur alag-alag paths se diametrically opposite Q tak pahunchti hain. Har ek ke liye displacement (visthapan) ka magnitude kya hai?

Kyon?

Displacement straight line PQ hai, yaani diameter 400 m, sabke liye same. Path length displacement ke barabar sirf us ladki ke liye hoti hai jo diameter ke along seedha jaati hai.

Ek hotel straight road par 10 km door hai. Ek cab 23 km ka ghumavdar path leti hai aur 28 min mein pahunchti hai. Average speed aur average velocity ka magnitude nikaalo.

Kyon?

Average speed = 23 km / (28/60 h) approx 49 km/h. Average velocity ka magnitude = 10 km / (28/60 h) approx 21 km/h. Path length displacement (visthapan) se alag hota hai, isliye ye alag hote hain.

i + j aur i - j ka magnitude aur direction kya hai?

Kyon?

i+j ke components 1,1 hain, magnitude sqrt(2), direction tan inverse 1 = 45 degrees. i-j ke components 1,-1 hain, magnitude sqrt(2), direction -45 degrees. In directions ke along components ko projection se graphically nikaal sakte hain.

Scalar (adish) quantity ke baare mein kaunsa statement galat hai?

Kyon?

Scalar (adish) sirf magnitude se define hota hai; source yeh require nahi karta ki wo conserved, dimensionless, non-negative ya space mein constant ho. Temperature difference aur doosre scalars dikhate hain ki definition sirf magnitude hai.

Position r = 3.0 t squared i - 2.0 t squared j + 4.0 t squared k metres hai, t seconds mein. t = 2.0 s par velocity (veg) ka magnitude kya hai?

Kyon?

v = dr/dt = 6.0 t i - 4.0 t j + 8.0 t k; 2.0 s par v = 12 i - 8 j + 16 k, magnitude sqrt(144+64+256)=sqrt(464) approx 21.5 m/s. Acceleration (tvaran) 6 i -4 j +8 k m/s squared hai.

Ek aircraft 3400 m height par ud raha hai. Ground point par 10.0 s apart positions ke dwara subtend kiya gaya angle 30 degrees hai. Speed (chaal) estimate karo, assuming ground point dono positions ke midpoint ke neeche hai.

Kyon?

Half distance (doori) = 3400 x tan 15 degrees approx 911 m, total distance approx 1822 m in 10.0 s, speed approx 182 m/s. Yeh midpoint assumption use karta hai jo diya gaya hai kyunki source mein figure geometry poori tarah readable nahi hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

scalar
Sirf magnitude wali quantity, jaise mass ya time.
vector
Magnitude aur direction wali quantity, jaise velocity.
displacement
Start se finish tak position ka straight-line change, jo path par depend nahi karta.
unit vector
Magnitude 1 wala vector jo sirf direction specify karne ke liye use hota hai.
projectile
Throw hone ke baad flight mein object, jisme horizontal aur vertical motion independent hoti hai.
centripetal acceleration
Circle ke centre ki taraf acceleration, magnitude v squared over R.
