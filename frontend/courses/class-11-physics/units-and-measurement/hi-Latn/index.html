Units and Measurement

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap samjha sakte hain ki unit, base quantity aur derived unit kya hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap calculations mein significant figures, rounding aur uncertainty ke rules use kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap dimensional formulae likh sakte hain aur equations check karne aur relations deduce karne ke liye dimensional analysis use kar sakte hain.

Aap length ko scale se aur mass ko balance se measure karte hain. Measurement ka matlab standard se comparison hota hai. Us standard ko unit kaha jata hai.

Measurement ka result ek number hota hai jo unit ke saath hota hai. For example, length ko metres mein ek number ke roop mein likha jata hai.

Base quantities ke units base units hote hain. Doosri quantities ke units derived units hote hain. Ye sab milkar units ka ek system banate hain.

Measurement ka result kya hota hai?

Kyon?

Measurement kisi quantity ko unit se compare karta hai, isliye result ek number with unit hota hai.

Pehle ke systems CGS, FPS aur MKS the. CGS mein centimetre, gram aur second use hote the. FPS mein foot, pound aur second use hote the. MKS mein metre, kilogram aur second use hote the.

SI ab internationally accepted hai. Isse BIPM ne 1971 mein develop kiya aur November 2018 mein revise kiya gaya. Yeh book SI units ko follow karti hai.

SI mein saat base units hain. Plane angle ka unit radian, rad hai. Solid angle ka unit steradian, sr hai. Dono dimensionless hain. SI decimal system use karta hai.

Kaunsa set length, mass aur time ke SI base units deta hai?

Kyon?

MKS aur SI length, mass aur time ke liye metre, kilogram aur second use karte hain.

Significant figures reliable digits plus pehla uncertain digit hote hain. 1.62 s mein, 1 aur 6 certain hain aur 2 uncertain hai, isliye teen significant figures hain. 287.5 cm mein chaar hain.

Sabhi non-zero digits significant hote hain. Do non-zero digits ke beech ke zeros significant hote hain. 1 se kam number mein pehle non-zero digit se pehle ke zeros significant nahi hote.

Decimal point ke bina trailing zeros significant nahi hote. Decimal point ke saath trailing zeros significant hote hain. Units badalne se significant figures nahi badalte. Precision instrument ke least count par depend karti hai.

Scientific notation aur order of magnitude

a × 10b likhein

Number ko a × 10b ke roop mein likhein, jahan a 1 aur 10 ke beech hai aur b koi bhi positive ya negative exponent hai.

Order of magnitude nikaalein

a ko 1 tak round karein jab a ≤ 5 ho, aur 10 tak jab 5 < a ≤ 10 ho. Phir number lagbhag 10b ho jaata hai aur b hi order of magnitude hai.

Examples use karein

Earth ka diameter 1.28 × 107 m order 107 m ka hai, order 7 ke saath. Hydrogen atom ka diameter 1.06 × 10-10 m order 10-10 m ka hai, order -10 ke saath.

Trailing zeros

Scientific notation mein a ke saare zeros significant hote hain, isliye 4.700 m = 4.700 × 102 cm mein chaar significant figures hain.

0.2370 g cm-3 mein kitne significant figures hain?

Kyon?

Leading zeros significant nahi hote aur decimal point ke saath trailing zero significant hota hai, isliye 2, 3, 7 aur 0 milakar chaar significant figures dete hain. Isi tarah 0.007 m2 mein 1, 2.64 × 1024 kg mein 3, 6.320 J mein 4, 6.032 N m-2 mein 4 aur 0.0006032 m2 mein 4 significant figures hain.

Multiplication ya division mein, utne hi significant figures rakho jitne least significant figures wale number mein hain. Density 4.237 g ko 2.51 cm3 se divide karne par 1.69 g cm-3 report hoti hai, na ki bahut zyada decimal places tak.

Addition ya subtraction mein, utne hi decimal places rakho jitne least decimal places wale number mein hain. 436.32 g, 227.2 g aur 0.301 g ka sum 663.8 g hai.

Difference 0.307 m - 0.304 m = 0.003 m hai. Addition aur subtraction ke liye multiplication rule ka use mat karo.

Solved example (Worked example)

5.74 g kisi substance ka volume 1.2 cm3 hai. Significant figures ko dhyan mein rakhte hue iski density express karo.

  1. Significant figures count karo Mass 5.74 g mein 3 significant figures hain. Volume 1.2 cm3 mein 2 significant figures hain.
  2. Divide karo Density = 5.74 / 1.2 = 4.783... g cm-3.
  3. Least tak round karo Data mein sabse kam 2 significant figures hain, isliye 2 significant figures tak round karo.

Uttar (Answer) 4.8 g cm-3

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek cube ki har side 7.203 m measured hai. Appropriate significant figures mein cube ka total surface area aur volume kya hai?

  1. Significant figures count karo Length 7.203 m mein 4 significant figures hain, isliye area aur volume ke liye 4 significant figures chahiye.
  2. Surface area Area = 6 × (7.203)2 = 311.299254 m2, 311.3 m2 tak rounded.
  3. Volume Volume = (7.203)3 = 373.714754 m3, 373.7 m3 tak rounded.

Uttar (Answer) Surface area 311.3 m2, volume 373.7 m3

Anishchit digits ko round off karna

Dropped digit 5 se zyada hai

Preceding digit ko 1 se badha do. Example: 2.746 ko three significant figures tak round karne par 2.75 milta hai.

Dropped digit 5 se kam hai

Preceding digit ko unchanged chhod do. Example: 1.743 ko three significant figures tak round karne par 1.74 milta hai.

Dropped digit exactly 5 hai

Agar preceding digit even hai, to 5 ko drop kar do. Agar odd hai, to use 1 se badha do. Isliye 2.745 2.74 ban jata hai aur 2.735 2.74 ban jata hai.

Intermediate steps

Intermediate steps mein ek extra digit rakho aur rounding errors build hone se bachne ke liye end mein round karo.

436.32 g + 227.2 g + 0.301 g = ? g correct decimal places tak

Kyon?

Arithmetic sum 663.821 g hai, lekin 227.2 g sirf ek decimal place tak correct hai, isliye round karke 663.8 g karo.

Product mein uncertainty combine hoti hai. 16.2 cm by 10.1 cm ki sheet ke liye, har measurement ± 0.1 cm hai, isliye area 163.62 cm2 mein lagbhag 1.6% error hai aur ise 164 ± 3 cm2 likha jata hai.

Subtraction significant figures kam kar sakta hai. 12.9 g - 7.06 g ka result 5.8 g hai, 5.84 g nahin, kyunki decimal places matter karti hain.

Relative error number par hi depend karta hai. Multi-step work ke intermediate steps mein ek extra digit rakhein.

1.02 g aur 9.89 g dono ± 0.01 g tak accurate hain. Kis mein relative error chhota hai?

Kyon?

1.02 g mein relative error (±0.01/1.02) × 100% = ±1% hai. 9.89 g mein ye (±0.01/9.89) × 100% = ±0.1% hai, isliye 9.89 g ka relative error chhota hai.

Dimension kisi quantity ki nature batate hain. Length [L] hai, mass [M] hai, time [T] hai, electric current [A] hai, temperature [K] hai, luminous intensity [cd] hai aur amount of substance [mol] hai.

Volume teen lengths ka hota hai, isliye dimensions [L3] hain. Iska mass aur time mein zero dimension hai.

Force (bal) mass times acceleration (tvaran) hota hai, isliye dimensions [M L T-2] hain. Magnitudes consider nahi kiye jaate, isliye speed (chaal) aur velocity (veg) dono ka dimension [L T-1] hota hai.

Ek dimensional formula dikhata hai ki base quantities kisi quantity ko kaise represent karti hain. Volume [M0 L3 T0] hai. Speed (chaal) [M0 L T-1] hai. Acceleration (tvaran) [M0 L T-2] hai. Mass density [M L-3 T0] hai.

Ek dimensional equation kisi quantity ko uske formula ke barabar rakhta hai. [V] = [M0 L3 T0], [v] = [M0 L T-1], [F] = [M L T-2] aur [ρ] = [M L-3 T0].

Aap quantities ke beech ke relations se dimensional equations paate hain, har quantity ko uske dimensions se replace karke.

x = x0 + v0 t + (1/2) a t2 ko check karna

Left side (baaya paksh)

[x] = [L].

Right side ka pehla term

[x0] = [L].

Right side ka doosra term

[v0 t] = [L T-1][T] = [L].

Right side ka teesra term

[(1/2) a t2] = [L T-2][T2] = [L].

Conclusion (nishkarsh)

Har term ka dimension length hai, left side ke same, isliye equation dimensionally correct hai. Pass hona ise sahi prove nahi karta.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek equation 1/2 m v2 = m g h consider karte hain, jahan m body ka mass hai, v uska velocity (veg) hai, g acceleration due to gravity (tvaran) hai aur h height hai. Check kijiye ki ye equation dimensionally correct hai ya nahi.

  1. Baayi taraf [M][L T-1]2 = [M][L2 T-2] = [M L2 T-2].
  2. Daayi taraf [M][L T-2][L] = [M][L2 T-2] = [M L2 T-2].
  3. Compare karein LHS aur RHS ke dimensions same hain, isliye equation dimensionally correct hai.

Uttar (Answer) Dimensionally correct

Solved example (Worked example)

Energy ka SI unit J = kg m2 s-2 hai; speed (chaal) v ka unit m s-1 hai aur acceleration (tvaran) a ka unit m s-2 hai. Kinetic energy (K) ke liye di gayi formulae mein se kaun-si formulae ko dimensional arguments se rule out kar sakte hain (m ka matlab mass hai): (a) K = m2 v3 (b) K = (1/2) m v2 (c) K = m a (d) K = (3/16) m v2 (e) K = (1/2) m v2 + m a

  1. K ke dimensions K ke dimensions [M L2 T-2] hain, jo J = kg m2 s-2 se aate hain.
  2. Har formula check kijiye (a) [M2 L3 T-3] deta hai, (b) aur (d) [M L2 T-2] dete hain, (c) [M L T-2] deta hai, aur (e) different dimensions ki quantities ko add karta hai, isliye iske proper dimensions nahi hain.
  3. Rule out kijiye (a), (c) aur (e) rule out ho jate hain. Dimensions (b) aur (d) mein distinguish nahi kar sakte. Correct formula (b) hai.

Uttar (Answer) (a), (c) aur (e) ruled out; correct (b) K = (1/2) m v2 hai

Dimensions se pendulum period deduce karna

Product form maan lijiye

Maan lijiye period T length l, mass m aur gravity g par depend karta hai: T = k lx gy mz, jahan k dimensionless hai aur x, y, z exponents hain.

Dimensions match karna

[T] = [L]x [L T-2]y [M]z se Lx+y T-2y Mz milta hai, isliye x + y = 0, -2y = 1 aur z = 0.

Solve kijiye

x = 1/2, y = -1/2, z = 0, isliye T = k l1/2 g-1/2 = k sqrt(l/g).

Constant

k ko dimensions se nahi nikal sakte. Asal mein k = 2π, isliye T = 2π sqrt(l/g).

Pendulum period aur length

Simple pendulum ke liye length badalne par period T = 2π sqrt(l/g) dikhata hai.

Principle of homogeneity ke according kaunsa addition allowed hai?

Kyon?

Sirf same dimensions wali quantities ko add ya subtract kiya ja sakta hai. Length plus length mein dono sides par same dimension [L] hota hai.

Base quantities hain length, mass, time, electric current, temperature, amount of substance aur luminous intensity. Inke units hain metre, kilogram, second, ampere, kelvin, mole aur candela.

Derived unit (vyutpann unit) ke special names ho sakte hain jaise joule, newton aur watt. Calculations mein units ko algebraic quantities ki tarah treat kiya jata hai.

Sirf proper significant figures hi retain karne chahiye. Dimensions equations ko check karte hain, lekin dimensionally correct equation exact hona zaroori nahi hai aur dimensionally wrong equation zaroor wrong hogi.

Solved example (Worked example)

Khaali sthaan bharo: (a) 1 cm side wale cube ka volume m3 mein (b) speed (chaal) 18 km h-1 se chalne wala vehicle 1 s mein m cover karta hai (c) 1 kg m2 s-2 = g cm2 s-2 (d) 3.0 m s-2 = km h-2 (e) 1 m = ly (f) G = 6.67 × 10-11 N m2 (kg)-2 = (cm)3 s-2 g-1

  1. Cube aur vehicle 1 cm = 0.01 m, isliye volume = (0.01)3 = 1 × 10-6 m3. 18 km h-1 = 5 m s-1, isliye 1 s mein ye 5 m cover karta hai.
  2. Energy unit 1 kg = 1000 g aur 1 m = 100 cm, isliye 1 kg m2 s-2 = 1000 × 10000 = 1 × 107 g cm2 s-2.
  3. Acceleration (tvaran) 3.0 m s-2 = 0.003 km per (1/3600 h)2 = 38880 km h-2.
  4. Light year aur G Source example ke anusaar light year 9.47 × 1015 m hai, isliye 1 m = 1.06 × 10-16 ly. G ke liye, 1 N = 105 g cm s-2, jisse 6.67 × 10-8 (cm)3 s-2 g-1 milta hai.

Uttar (Answer) 1 × 10-6 m3; 5 m; 1 × 107 g cm2 s-2; 38880 km h-2; 1.06 × 10-16 ly; 6.67 × 10-8 (cm)3 s-2 g-1

Calorie naye units mein

Calorie se shuru karein

Ek calorie lagbhag 4.2 J ke barabar hai aur 1 J = 1 kg m2 s-2.

Naye units

Mass ka naya unit α kg ke barabar hai, length β m ke barabar hai aur time γ s ke barabar hai. Units ko algebraic quantities ki tarah treat karein.

Convert karein

1 kg = 1/α naye mass units, 1 m = 1/β naye length units, 1 s = 1/γ naye time units, isliye 1 kg m2 s-2 = α-1 β-2 γ2 naye units.

Result

Naye units mein calorie ka magnitude 4.2 α-1 β-2 γ2 hai.

Kaun sa statement meaningful hai bina comparison ke liye standard specify kiye?

Kyon?

Large ya small ka koi matlab nahi hai bina standard ke. Proton ko electron se compare karne se standard specify hota hai, yaani electron, jabki baaki statements mein standard specify nahi kiya gaya.

Length ki ek nayi unit aise chuni jaati hai ki vacuum mein speed of light unity ho. Light Sun se Earth tak 8 min aur 20 s leta hai. Is nayi unit mein distance (doori) kya hai?

Kyon?

Time 8 × 60 + 20 = 500 s hai. Nayi unit mein speed = 1 hone par, distance = speed × time = 500 new units.

Kaunsa device length measure karne ke liye sabse precise hai: (a) vernier callipers jisme sliding scale par 20 divisions hain (b) screw gauge jiska pitch 1 mm hai aur circular scale par 100 divisions hain (c) optical instrument jo length ko light ke wavelength ke andar measure kar sakta hai?

Kyon?

Precision least count par depend karti hai. Optical instrument light ke wavelength ke andar measure karta hai, jo is list mein sabse chhota scale hai, isliye yahi sabse precise hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek student human hair ki thickness measure karta hai microscope se jiska magnification 100 hai. Woh 20 observations karta hai aur paata hai ki microscope ke field of view mein hair ki average width 3.5 mm hai. Hair ki thickness ka estimate kya hai?

  1. Magnification ka use karein Actual thickness = observed width / magnification = 3.5 mm / 100.
  2. Calculate karein 3.5 / 100 = 0.035 mm.
  3. Significant figures 3.5 mm mein 2 significant figures hain, isliye answer 0.035 mm = 3.5 × 10-5 m hai.

Uttar (Answer) 0.035 mm ya 3.5 × 10-5 m

Ek thin brass rod ke diameter ki 100 measurements ka set sirf 5 measurements ke set se zyada reliable estimate kyun dega?

Kyon?

Zyada observations average estimate ko better banate hain. Yahi principle metre scale se thread diameter estimate karne mein aur general repeated measurements mein bhi lagta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek ghar ki photograph 35 mm slide par 1.75 cm2 area occupy karti hai. Slide ko screen par project kiya jata hai, aur screen par ghar ka area 1.55 m2 hai. Projector-screen arrangement ka linear magnification kya hai?

  1. Slide area convert karo 1.75 cm2 = 1.75 × 10-4 m2.
  2. Area ratio Area ratio = 1.55 / (1.75 × 10-4) = 8857.
  3. Linear magnification Linear magnification = sqrt(area ratio) = sqrt(8857) = 94.1 (teen significant figures tak).

Uttar (Answer) 94.1

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek rectangular metal sheet ki length, breadth aur thickness kramashah 4.234 m, 1.005 m, aur 2.01 cm hain. Sheet ka area aur volume correct significant figures tak dijiye.

  1. Area Area = 4.234 × 1.005 = 4.25517 m2. Dono mein 4 significant figures hain, isliye area = 4.255 m2.
  2. Thickness metres mein 2.01 cm = 0.0201 m, 3 significant figures ke saath.
  3. Volume Volume = 4.255 × 0.0201 = 0.0855 m3, 3 significant figures tak.

Uttar (Answer) Area 4.255 m2, volume 0.0855 m3

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek box ka mass, jo grocer's balance se measure kiya gaya hai, 2.30 kg hai. Box mein 20.15 g aur 20.17 g mass ke do gold pieces add kiye jaate hain. Correct significant figures tak (a) box ka total mass kya hai, (b) pieces ke masses ka difference kya hai?

  1. Total mass 2.30 kg = 2300 g, 10 g place tak correct hai. Total = 2300 + 20.15 + 20.17 = 2340.32 g, decimal places ke hisaab se round karke 2340 g = 2.34 kg.
  2. Difference Difference = 20.17 - 20.15 = 0.02 g, two decimal places tak correct.

Uttar (Answer) (a) 2.34 kg (b) 0.02 g

Ek ladka m/m0 = (1 - v2)-1/2 likhta hai aur bhool jaata hai ki c kahan rakhna hai. Dimensional correctness ke liye c kahan hona chahiye?

Kyon?

v aur c dono ke dimensions [L T-1] hain, isliye v/c dimensionless hai. Function ka argument dimensionless hona chahiye, aur similar quantities ka ratio dimensionless hota hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Atomic scale par convenient length ka unit angstrom kehlata hai aur ise Å se denote karte hain: 1 Å = 10-10 m. Hydrogen atom ka size lagbhag 0.5 Å hai. Hydrogen atoms ke ek mole ka total atomic volume m3 mein kya hai?

  1. Har atom ka volume Size = 0.5 Å = 0.5 × 10-10 m. Har atom ka volume = (0.5 × 10-10)3 = 1.25 × 10-31 m3.
  2. Har mole mein number Mole ki definition ke anusar ek mole mein 6.02214076 × 1023 elementary entities hoti hain.
  3. Total volume Total = 1.25 × 10-31 × 6.022 × 1023 = 7.5 × 10-8 m3.

Uttar (Answer) 7.5 × 10-8 m3

Standard temperature aur pressure par ek mole ideal gas 22.4 L occupy karta hai. Hydrogen molecule ka size lagbhag 1 Å lete hue, ek mole hydrogen ke molar volume aur atomic volume ka ratio kya hai?

Kyon?

Molar volume = 22.4 L = 2.24 × 10-2 m3. Atomic volume = (1 × 10-10)3 × 6.022 × 1023 = 6.0 × 10-7 m3. Ratio = 2.24 × 10-2 / 6.0 × 10-7 = 3.7 × 104. Ratio bada hai kyunki gas mein atoms door-door hote hain aur unke beech zyada tar volume khaali space hota hai.

Agar aap ek fast moving train se bahar dekhein, to paas ke trees train ke opposite direction mein tezi se move karte hain, lekin door ke objects stationary lagte hain. Kyun?

Kyon?

Apparent motion (gati) is par depend karta hai ki moving observer ke liye object ki direction kitni jaldi badalti hai. Paas ke objects ki direction jaldi badalti hai, door ke objects ki direction dheere badalti hai, isliye wo aapke saath move karte hue ya stationary lagte hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Sun ek hot plasma hai jiska inner core temperature 107 K se zyada hai aur outer surface lagbhag 6000 K hai. Kya aap expect karte hain ki Sun ki mass density solids aur liquids ke range mein hogi ya gases ke? Check karein: mass of the Sun = 2.0 × 1030 kg, radius of the Sun = 7.0 × 108 m.

  1. Sun ka volume Volume = (4/3) π r3 = (4/3) π (7.0 × 108)3 = 1.44 × 1027 m3.
  2. Density Density = mass / volume = 2.0 × 1030 / 1.44 × 1027 = 1.4 × 103 kg m-3.
  3. Range compare karein 1.4 × 103 kg m-3 solids aur liquids ke range mein hai, gases ke nahi, isliye solids aur liquids range ke liye guess sahi hai.

Uttar (Answer) 1.4 × 103 kg m-3, solids aur liquids ke range mein

Dimensional analysis humein ek equation ke baare mein kya batata hai?

Kyon?

Agar ek equation test mein fail ho jaata hai to woh galat sabit ho jaata hai. Agar pass ho jaata hai, to woh sahi sabit nahi hota. Constants obtain nahi kiye ja sakte aur same dimensions wali quantities ko distinguish nahi kiya ja sakta.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

unit
Ek standard reference jisse physical quantity compare ki jaati hai.
base quantity
System of units ke liye choose ki gayi fundamental quantity, jaise length, mass aur time.
derived unit
Derived quantity ke liye base units ke combination se bana unit.
significant figures
Measurement mein reliable digits plus pehla uncertain digit.
dimension
Base quantities ki powers jo kisi physical quantity ko represent karti hain, square brackets mein likhi jaati hain.
dimensional formula
Expression jo dikhata hai ki kaunsi base quantities kisi quantity ke dimensions ko represent karti hain.
order of magnitude
Kisi number ke approximate estimate ke liye a × 10b mein exponent b.
principle of homogeneity
Sirf same dimensions wali quantities ko add ya subtract kiya ja sakta hai.
