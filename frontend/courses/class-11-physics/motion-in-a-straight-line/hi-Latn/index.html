Motion in a Straight Line

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is paath ke baad aap position, velocity aur acceleration ka use karke seedhi rekha ke saath motion describe kar sakte hain.
  • Is paath ke baad aap position-time aur velocity-time graphs se velocity aur acceleration padh sakte hain.
  • Is paath ke baad aap uniformly accelerated motion aur free fall ke liye kinematic equations ka use kar sakte hain.
  • Is paath ke baad aap average speed ko average velocity ke magnitude se distinguish kar sakte hain aur frame of reference ke saath relative motion explain kar sakte hain.

Aap apne chaaro taraf motion (gati) dekhte hain. Aap chalte hain, daudte hain, aur cycle chalate hain. Pedon se patte girte hain aur paani dam se neeche bahta hai.

Motion (gati) ka matlab hai kisi object ki position ka samay ke saath badalna. Isko describe karne ke liye, aap pehle ek origin chunte hain. Origin ke right side ki position ko positive aur left side ko negative maana jata hai.

Is lesson mein hum seedhi rekha ke saath motion (gati) ka adhyayan karte hain. Ise rectilinear motion kehte hain. Hum objects ko point objects maante hain jab unka size unke dwara tay ki gayi distance (doori) se bahut chhota ho.

Kinematics motion ko describe karne ke tarikon ka adhyayan hai. Yeh motion ke karanon mein nahi jaata. Hum velocity (veg) aur acceleration (tvaran) ko develop karte hain taaki position samay ke saath kaise badalti hai, iska varnan kar sakein.

Ek railway carriage do stations ke beech bina jerks ke move karta hai, apni khud ki length se kaafi zyada door tak. Kya ise point object maana ja sakta hai?

Kyon?

Ek object ko point object tab maana ja sakta hai jab uska size reasonable time mein move kiye gaye distance (doori) se bahut chhota ho.

Average velocity (veg) batata hai ki koi object poore interval mein kitni tezi se move hua. Yeh nahi batata ki kisi ek instant par woh kitni tezi se move hua.

Instantaneous velocity average velocity ki limit hai jab time interval infinitesimally small ho jata hai. Symbols mein, v = dx/dt.

Position-time graph par, kisi instant par velocity us point par tangent ka slope (dhaal) hoti hai. Chhote intervals joining line ko tangent ke kareeb le jaate hain.

x = 0.08 t^3 ke liye, t = 4.0 s par centred average velocity 3.84 m s-1 ke kareeb pahunchti hai jab interval ghatta hai. Woh limiting value hi t = 4.0 s par velocity hai.

Position-time graph aur tangent slope

Yeh curve x = 0.08 t^3 aur chune gaye time par tangent dikhata hai. Tangent ka slope (dhaal) instantaneous velocity (veg) hota hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek object jo x-axis ke along move kar raha hai, uski position x = a + b t^2 se di gayi hai jahan a = 8.5 m, b = 2.5 m s-2 hai aur t seconds mein measure kiya jata hai. t = 0 s aur t = 2.0 s par iski velocity (veg) kya hai? t = 2.0 s aur t = 4.0 s ke beech average velocity kya hai?

  1. Position ko differentiate karo v = dx/dt = 2 b t = 5.0 t m s-1.
  2. t = 0 s par velocity v = 5.0 x 0 = 0 m s-1.
  3. t = 2.0 s par velocity v = 5.0 x 2.0 = 10 m s-1.
  4. 2.0 s aur 4.0 s ke beech average velocity 2.0 s par x = 8.5 + 2.5 x 4 = 18.5 m. 4.0 s par x = 8.5 + 2.5 x 16 = 48.5 m. Average velocity = (48.5 - 18.5)/(4.0 - 2.0) = 15 m s-1.

Uttar (Answer) t = 0 s par velocity 0 m s-1 hai, t = 2.0 s par 10 m s-1 hai, aur t = 2.0 s aur 4.0 s ke beech average velocity 15 m s-1 hai.

Speed (chaal) velocity (veg) ka magnitude hoti hai. +24.0 m s-1 velocity aur -24.0 m s-1 velocity, dono ki speed 24.0 m s-1 hoti hai.

Finite interval par Average speed, average velocity ke magnitude se greater than ya equal hoti hai. Lekin kisi instant par instantaneous speed us instant ki instantaneous velocity ke magnitude ke equal hoti hai.

Motion (gati) ke dauraan velocity (veg) badalti hai. Hum is badlav ko samay ke saath velocity ke change ki rate ke roop mein describe karte hain. Yeh rate acceleration (tvaran) hai.

Average acceleration a = (v2 - v1)/(t2 - t1) hai, jiska SI unit m s-2 hai. Instantaneous acceleration a = dv/dt hai, jo velocity-time curve ke tangent ka slope (dhaal) hai.

Acceleration positive, negative ya zero ho sakta hai. Yeh speed (chaal) mein badlav, direction mein badlav, ya dono se aa sakta hai.

Position-time graphs positive acceleration ke liye upward curve karte hain, negative acceleration ke liye downward, aur zero acceleration ke liye straight lines hote hain.

Velocity (veg)-time graph aur area as displacement (visthapan)

Constant acceleration (tvaran) ke liye velocity-time line dikhata hai aur uske neeche ka area shade karta hai. Shaded area displacement (visthapan) ke barabar hai.

Agar koi particle speeding up hai, to acceleration (tvaran) velocity (veg) ki direction mein hota hai. Agar uski speed (chaal) kam ho rahi hai, to acceleration velocity ki opposite direction mein hota hai.

Yeh baat har origin aur positive direction ke liye sach hai jo aap choose karein. Sirf acceleration ka sign yeh nahi batata ki speed badh rahi hai ya kam ho rahi hai.

Constant acceleration (tvaran) ke liye, average acceleration interval ke dauraan constant value ke barabar hota hai. Agar t = 0 par velocity (veg) v0 hai aur time t par v hai, to v = v0 + a t.

Velocity-time graph ke neeche ka area ek triangle plus ek rectangle hota hai. Yeh area displacement (visthapan) ke barabar hota hai.

Isse x = ((v0 + v)/2) t milta hai, isliye average velocity initial aur final velocities ka arithmetic average hota hai.

t ko eliminate karne se uniformly accelerated motion (gati) ke liye teen kinematic equations milti hain.

velocity (veg)-time area se kinematic equations derive karna

v = v0 + a t se shuru karein

Yeh a = (v - v0)/t se aata hai constant acceleration (tvaran) ke liye.

v-t graph ke neeche area nikaalein

Area = triangle ka area + rectangle ka area = (1/2)(v - v0) t + v0 t = ((v0 + v)/2) t.

Area displacement (visthapan) ke barabar hai

To x = ((v0 + v)/2) t.

t ko hataayein

x = ((v0 + v)/2) t mein t = (v - v0)/a use karein to v^2 = v0^2 + 2 a x milta hai. x = ((v0 + v)/2) t mein v - v0 = a t use karein to x = v0 t + (1/2) a t^2 milta hai.

Calculus se equations of motion (gati ke equations) derive karna

Acceleration (tvaran) ko integrate karna

a = dv/dt, isliye dv = a dt. Integrate karne se v - v0 = a t milta hai, isliye v = v0 + a t.

Velocity (veg) ko integrate karna

v = dx/dt, isliye dx = v dt. v = v0 + a t ke saath integrate karne se x - x0 = v0 t + (1/2) a t^2 milta hai.

Time ko eliminate karna

a = dv/dt aur v = dx/dt se, a = v dv/dx, isliye v dv = a dx. Integrate karne se (1/2)(v^2 - v0^2) = a (x - x0) milta hai, isliye v^2 = v0^2 + 2 a (x - x0).

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek ball ko multistorey building ke top se vertically upwards 20 m s-1 ki velocity (veg) se throw kiya jata hai. Jis point se ball throw kiya gaya hai, uski height ground se 25.0 m hai. (a) Ball kitni high tak rise karegi? aur (b) ball ground hit karne se pehle kitna time lagega? g = 10 m s-2 lein.

  1. Upward ko positive maanein v0 = +20 m s-1, a = -g = -10 m s-2, aur top par v = 0 m s-1.
  2. Rise ki height v^2 = v0^2 + 2 a (y - y0) use karein: 0 = (20)^2 + 2(-10)(y - y0), isliye y - y0 = 20 m. Ground se height 25.0 + 20 = 45 m hai.
  3. Upar jaane ka time v = v0 + a t use karein: 0 = 20 - 10 t1, isliye t1 = 2 s.
  4. Neeche girne ka time Top se, v0 = 0, a = -10 m s-2, y0 = 45 m, y = 0: 0 = 45 + (1/2)(-10) t2^2, isliye t2 = 3 s. Total time = 2 + 3 = 5 s.

Uttar (Answer) (a) Ball launch point se 20 m upar rise karti hai, ground se 45 m. (b) Yeh ground ko 5 s baad hit karti hai.

Free fall (mukta patan) gravity ke under motion (gati) hai, jisme air resistance neglected hai. Earth ki surface ke paas, jab height earth ke radius ke tulna mein chhoti ho, to g constant 9.8 m s-2 hota hai.

Upward direction ko positive maanein. Phir gravity ke karan acceleration (tvaran) a = -g = -9.8 m s-2 hota hai.

Ek object jo rest se y = 0 par release kiya gaya hai, uske liye kinematic equations se v = -9.8 t, y = -4.9 t^2 aur v^2 = -19.6 y milta hai.

Acceleration constant hai, velocity (veg) time ke saath linearly vary karti hai, aur distance (doori) t^2 ke hisaab se vary karti hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Free fall (mukta patan) ke under ek object ki motion (gati) discuss karo. Air resistance ko neglect karo. g = 9.8 m s-2 lo, upward positive, aur object ko rest se y = 0 par release kiya gaya hai.

  1. Acceleration (tvaran) likho a = -g = -9.8 m s-2, v0 = 0.
  2. Velocity (veg) time ke saath v = v0 + a t = 0 - 9.8 t = -9.8 t m s-1.
  3. Time ke saath position y = 0 + (1/2) a t^2 = -4.9 t^2 m.
  4. Position ke saath velocity v^2 = 0 + 2 a y = -19.6 y m^2 s-2.

Uttar (Answer) v = -9.8 t m s-1, y = -4.9 t^2 m, v^2 = -19.6 y m^2 s-2.

Agar particle speed up ho raha hai, to acceleration velocity ki direction mein hota hai. Agar speed decrease ho rahi hai, to acceleration velocity ke opposite hota hai. Yeh origin aur axis ke choice se independent hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

Position
Kisi object ki location chosen origin ke respect mein; seedhi rekha ke motion mein right positive aur left negative hota hai.
Velocity
Kisi instant par time ke saath position ke change ki rate, v = dx/dt; iska magnitude aur direction hoti hai.
Acceleration
Time ke saath velocity ke change ki rate, a = dv/dt; iska magnitude aur direction hoti hai aur iska unit m s-2 hai.
Displacement
Time interval mein position ka change; yeh positive, negative ya zero ho sakta hai aur velocity-time graph ke neeche ke area ke barabar hota hai.
Average speed
Total path length ko time interval se divide kiya gaya; yeh average velocity ke magnitude se greater ya equal hoti hai.
Frame of reference
Woh fixed point ya points ka group jiske respect mein motion measure kiya jaata hai.
