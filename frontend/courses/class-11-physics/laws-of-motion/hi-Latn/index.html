Laws of Motion

Is lesson ke baad aap seekhenge:

  • Is lesson ke baad aap samjha sakte hain ki Aristotle ka yeh vichar kyun galat hai ki kisi body ko moving rakhne ke liye force chahiye, aur Galileo ka law of inertia bata sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap Newton ke teen laws of motion bata aur apply kar sakte hain, jisme momentum, impulse aur conservation of momentum shamil hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap equilibrium, level aur inclined surfaces par friction, aur level aur banked roads par car ki circular motion ka analyse kar sakte hain.
  • Is lesson ke baad aap free-body diagram bana sakte hain aur laws of motion se multi-body problems solve kar sakte hain.

Socho ek football zameen par sthir pada hai. Use move karne ke liye, kisi ko use kick karna padega. Ek patthar ko upar bhejne ke liye, kisi ko use upar dhakelna padega. Ek halki hawa ped ki tehniyon ko jhulati hai, aur tez hawa bhaari vastuon ko bhi move kar sakti hai. In sabhi maamlon mein koi bahari agency force (bal) supply karti hai.

Kabhi kabhi agency body ko touch karti hai, jaise tumhara haath ball ko touch karta hai ya ek behti dhara boat ko dhakelti hai. Yeh hamesha zaroori nahi hai. Ek building ke top se chhoda gaya patthar neeche ki taraf speed up hota hai kyunki earth ka gravitational pull hota hai, aur ek bar magnet iron nail ko distance (doori) se attract kar sakta hai. Toh ek external agency contact mein ya distance se act kar sakti hai.

Force motion (gati) ko rokne ke liye bhi zaroori hai: tum ek inclined plane par rolling ball ko uski motion ki direction ke against dhakel kar rok sakte ho.

Ab ek tez sawaal. Maan lo ek body pehle se hi uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein move kar rahi hai - ek skater horizontal ice slab par constant speed (chaal) ke saath seedha move kar raha hai. Kya use uniform motion mein rakhne ke liye external force ki zaroorat hai? Yeh sawaal simple lagta hai, lekin iska jawab dene mein ages lag gaye.

Aristotle (384 B.C. - 322 B.C.) ka maanna tha ki agar koi body move kar rahi hai, to use moving rakhne ke liye kuch external chahiye hota hai. Is view ke hisaab se dhanush se chhoda gaya arrow udta rehta hai kyunki uske peeche ki hawa use dhakelti rehti hai. Ise law ki tarah kaho: body ko motion (gati) mein rakhne ke liye external force (bal) zaroori hai.

Daily experience se ye natural lagta hai. Ek chhota bachcha toy-car kheenchta hua jaanta hai ki string ko har waqt kheenchna padta hai; chhod do aur car rest par aa jaati hai. Agar unhein unke haal par chhod diya jaye, to earth par sabhi bodies rukti hui lagti hain.

Flaw friction hai. Moving toy-car rukti hai kyunki floor us par friction ka force lagata hai, jo uski motion ke opposite hai. Car ko chalte rakhne ke liye, bachche ko forward equal force se kheenchna padega, taaki car par net external force zero ho. Agar friction na hota, to car ko uniformly moving rakhne ke liye koi force bilkul zaroori nahi hota.

Solids ke beech friction, aur fluids mein viscous forces jaise opposing forces, natural world mein hamesha present hote hain. Isliye normally humein cheezon ko push karna padta hai taaki wo moving rahein. Nature ka true law dhoondhne ke liye, Galileo ne ek aisi duniya imagine ki jisme bilkul friction nahi tha.

Galileo ne inclined planes par objects ki motion (gati) study ki. Ek object jo inclined plane par neeche move karta hai, accelerate karta hai; jo upar move karta hai, retard karta hai. Horizontal plane par motion in-between case hai. Galileo ne conclude kiya ki frictionless horizontal plane par move karta hua object na accelerate karega na retard karega: wo constant velocity (veg) se move karta hai.

Uska second experiment double inclined plane use karta tha. Ek ball jo ek plane par rest se release hoti hai, neeche roll karti hai aur doosre par climb karti hai. Agar planes smooth hain, to ball lagbhag utni hi height tak pahunchti hai jitni se start hui thi - thodi kam, kabhi zyada nahi. Friction absent hone par, final height starting height ke barabar hoti.

Doosre plane ko aur flat banao aur repeat karo: ball phir bhi same height tak climb karti hai, isliye use zyada door travel karna padta hai. Limiting case mein doosra plane horizontal ho jata hai aur ball infinite distance (doori) travel karti hai - uski motion kabhi khatam nahi hoti.

Practice mein ball finite distance ke baad ruk jaati hai, kyunki friction ko kabhi bhi poori tarah remove nahi kiya ja sakta. Galileo ka insight yeh tha ki rest ki state aur uniform linear motion ki state equivalent hain: dono cases mein body par koi net force (bal) nahi hota. Ek body mein jo property hoti hai ki wo apni state of rest ya uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein change ko resist karti hai, use inertia kehte hain. Inertia ka matlab hai 'resistance to change'.

Ek bachcha floor par string se toy-car kheencha hai. Car straight line mein constant speed (chaal) se move karti hai. Kaunsa statement correct hai?

Kyon?

Constant velocity (veg) ka matlab zero acceleration (tvaran) hai, isliye first law ke anusar net external force zero hona chahiye. Child ka pull aur friction equal aur opposite hain. Agar friction na ho, to pull ki zaroorat hi nahi hoti — yahi exactly Aristotle galat the.

Galileo ke double inclined plane experiment mein planes smooth hain. Jab second plane ka slope (dhaal) aur flat hota jata hai to kya hota hai?

Kyon?

Ball hamesha lagbhag apni starting height tak wapas chadh jati hai. Flatter plane ka matlab hai us height tak pahunchne ke liye zyada distance chahiye, aur horizontal plane par required distance infinite hota hai - idealised motion kabhi rukta nahi.

Newton ne Galileo ke ideas par aage kaam kiya aur mechanics ki foundation three laws of motion (gati ke teen niyam) mein rakhi. Galileo ka law of inertia hi first law ban gaya: har body apne state of rest ya straight line mein uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein rehti hai jab tak koi external force (bahari bal) use otherwise act karne ke liye compel na kare.

Rest aur straight line mein uniform motion dono ka matlab zero acceleration hai. Isliye first law ko ek line mein kaha ja sakta hai: agar kisi body par net external force zero hai, to uska acceleration (tvaran) zero hai. Acceleration non-zero sirf tab ho sakta hai jab body par net external force ho.

Do tarah ki situations aati hain. Kabhi hum jaante hain ki net force zero hai - jaise ek spaceship jo baaki sab objects se door hai aur uske saare rockets off hain; tab uska acceleration zero hona chahiye, aur agar wo move kar raha hai to wo uniform velocity (veg) maintain karta hai. Zyada tar hum saari forces nahi jaante, lekin hum dekh sakte hain ki object rest mein hai ya uniform motion mein; tab first law batata hai ki us par net external force zero hona chahiye.

Ek table par rest mein rakhi book lo. Us par do forces act karti hain: uska weight W downward aur table ka normal force R upward. Sahi reasoning ye nahi hai ki 'kyunki W = R, forces cancel ho jaate hain aur book rest mein rehti hai'. Sahi reasoning ye hai: book rest mein observed hoti hai, isliye first law se us par net external force zero hona chahiye, aur is wajah se R ko W ke equal aur opposite hona chahiye. Gravity har jagah hai aur friction common hai, isliye earth par rest mein ya uniform motion mein kisi object par lagne wali forces ka sum zero hota hai - aisa nahi ki koi force hi na ho.

Bus mein aap first law feel karte hain. Jab bus suddenly start hoti hai to aap peeche dhakel diye jaate hain: aapke feet friction ki wajah se floor ke saath move karte hain, jabki aapke body ka baaki hissa wahi rehna chahta hai, inertia ki wajah se. Jab bus suddenly rukti hai to aapke feet ruk jaate hain aur body ka baaki hissa aage move karta rehta hai, isliye aap aage dhakel diye jaate hain.

Ek baarish ki boond constant speed (chaal) se vertically neeche gir rahi hai. Boond par net external force (bal) kya hai?

Kyon?

Straight line mein constant speed (chaal) ka matlab zero acceleration (tvaran) hai, isliye first law ke anusar net external force (bal) zero hona chahiye. Weight ko hawa ka upward drag balance karta hai. Forces alag-alag zero nahi hain - woh cancel karti hain.

Second law kisi body par net external force (bal) ko uske acceleration (tvaran) se jodta hai, aur ise momentum (samveg) ke terms mein statement kiya jata hai. Kisi body ka momentum uske mass m aur velocity (veg) v ke product ke roop mein define hota hai, likha jata hai p = m v. Momentum ek vector (sadish) quantity hai; yeh velocity ke along point karta hai.

Yeh product kyun? Same time mein same speed tak pahunchne ke liye loaded truck ko small car se kaafi zyada force chahiye hota hai, aur same speed par same time mein rukne ke liye zyada opposing force chahiye hota hai. Same height se giraye gaye halke stone ki tulna mein heavy stone ko catch karna zyada mushkil hota hai. High speed se fired bullet tissue ko pierce kar sakti hai, jabki wahi bullet moderate speed par little damage karti hai. Sirf mass, ya sirf speed, enough nahi hai - product m v hi quantity hai jo matter karti hai.

Agar ek fixed force kisi certain time interval tak different masses ki do bodies par, jo initially rest par hain, act karta hai, to lighter body greater speed pick up karti hai; lekin us time ke end mein har body ne same momentum acquire kar liya hota hai. Same force same time ke liye different bodies par same change in momentum cause karta hai.

Direction bhi matter karti hai. Constant speed par horizontal circle mein whirled ek stone ka momentum fixed magnitude ka hota hai jiska direction constantly badalta rehta hai, aur us change ko produce karne ke liye force chahiye hoti hai. Agar aap stone ko faster whirl karte hain ya smaller circle mein whirl karte hain to aapke hand ko harder pull karna padta hai. Isliye momentum vector ke change ki rate jitni greater hogi, force utni greater hogi.

Momentum (samveg) se F = m a tak

Step 1: momentum (samveg)

Mass m wali body jab velocity (veg) v se move karti hai, to momentum p = m v hota hai.

Step 2: law shabdon mein

Kisi body ke momentum ke change ki rate applied force (bal) ke directly proportional hoti hai aur yeh us direction mein hoti hai jisme force act karta hai.

Step 3: ise symbols mein likho

Agar force F kisi time interval Δt ke liye act karta hai aur velocity v se v + Δv ho jati hai, to momentum Δp = m Δv se change hota hai. Law kehta hai F ∝ Δp/Δt, yani F = k Δp/Δt jahan k proportionality ka constant hai.

Step 4: limit lo

Maan lo Δt bahut chhota ho jata hai. Tab Δp/Δt derivative dp/dt ban jata hai, isliye F = k dp/dt.

Step 5: fixed mass

Fixed mass wali body ke liye, dp/dt = d(m v)/dt = m dv/dt = m a. Isliye F = k m a.

Step 6: k = 1 chuno

Force ki unit abhi tak define nahi hui hai, isliye hum k = 1 choose karne ke liye free hain. Tab F = dp/dt = m a. SI units mein jo force mass 1 kg ko acceleration (tvaran) 1 m s-2 deta hai woh one newton hai, isliye 1 N = 1 kg m s-2.

Second law ke baare mein chaar baatein. Pehla, F = 0 rakhne se a = 0 milta hai, isliye second law first law se match karta hai. Doosra, yeh ek vector (sadish) law hai: yeh teen equations hain, har component ke liye ek (F_x = dp_x/dt = m a_x, aur isi tarah y aur z ke liye). Ek force (bal) jo velocity (veg) ke parallel nahi hai, sirf velocity ke us component ko badalta hai jo force ke along hai; force ke perpendicular component wala velocity component unchanged rehta hai - isliye vertical gravity ke under projectile apna horizontal velocity component banaye rakhta hai.

Teesra, yeh law ek single point particle par lagta hai, aur usi form mein rigid body ya particles ke system par bhi; tab F system par total external force hai aur a system ke acceleration (tvaran) hai as a whole, internal forces ko chhod kar. Chautha, yeh law ek local relation hai: ek point par ek instant par force us point par us instant par acceleration determine karta hai. Ek accelerating train se dropped stone ke paas, release ke turant baad, koi horizontal force nahi hai aur koi horizontal acceleration nahi hai - usme train ke acceleration ki koi memory nahi hoti.

Yeh bhi yaad rakhein ki F ka matlab kya hai. F = m a mein, F body ke external sabhi material agencies ke kaaran net force hai, aur a us force ka effect hai. Product m a ek aur force nahi hai jo list mein add kiya jaaye.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek astronaut accidentally apne chhote spaceship se alag ho jata hai jo interstellar space mein constant rate 100 m s-2 se accelerate kar raha hai. Spaceship ke bahar nikalne ke turant baad astronaut ka acceleration (tvaran) kya hai? (Maan lijiye ki paas mein koi stars nahi hain jo us par gravitational force exert karein.)

  1. Forces ki list banao Paas mein koi stars nahi hain jo use pull karein, aur chhota spaceship us par negligible gravitational attraction exert karta hai.
  2. Net force Isliye astronaut par net external force, spaceship se bahar nikalne ke baad, zero hai.
  3. First law apply karo Agar kisi body par net external force zero hai, to uska acceleration zero hota hai.

Uttar (Answer) Astronaut ka acceleration zero hai. Woh spaceship ka 100 m s-2 acceleration nahi rakhta, kyunki force (bal) kisi body ke earlier motion se 'carried' nahi hota.

Solved example (Worked example)

0.04 kg mass ki ek bullet 90 m s-1 ki speed (chaal) se chalte hue ek heavy wooden block mein ghusati hai aur 60 cm distance (doori) ke baad ruk jati hai. Block dwara bullet par lagaya gaya average resistive force (bal) kya hai?

  1. Retardation nikalna Maan lijiye ki retardation a constant hai. Speed u = 90 m s-1 aur stopping distance s = 60 cm = 0.6 m se, a = u²/(2s) = (90 × 90)/(2 × 0.6) m s-2 = 6750 m s-2.
  2. Second law lagayein Retarding force F = m a = 0.04 kg × 6750 m s-2 hai.

Uttar (Answer) F = 270 N. Actual resistive force, aur isliye retardation, uniform nahi ho sakta, isliye yeh answer sirf average resistive force darshata hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Mass m ke ek particle ki motion (gati) y = u t + ½ g t² dwara describe ki jaati hai. Particle par lagne wala force (bal) nikaalein.

  1. Velocity (veg) Position ko time ke respect mein differentiate karein: v = dy/dt = u + g t.
  2. Acceleration (tvaran) Phir se differentiate karein: a = dv/dt = g.
  3. Force (bal) Second law apply karein: F = m a = m g.

Uttar (Answer) F = m g. Ye equation gravity ke karan acceleration ke under move karte particle ko describe karti hai, jahan y position coordinate hai jo g ki direction mein measured hai.

Kuch force (bal) bahut kam samay ke liye act karte hain lekin itne large hote hain ki momentum (samveg) ko kaafi badal dete hain - jaise ball ka wall se takra kar bounce back hona. Ball par force sirf tab act karta hai jab ball aur wall contact mein hote hain, aur force aur contact time ko alag-alag measure karna mushkil hota hai. Unka product mushkil nahi hai: yah momentum mein change hai.

Us product ko impulse kaha jata hai: impulse = force × time duration = change in momentum. Ek large force jo short time ke liye act karke momentum mein finite change produce karta hai, use impulsive force kehte hain. Newtonian mechanics mein impulsive force alag kind ka force nahi hai - yah simply large aur short-lived hota hai.

Yeh ek familiar skill ko explain karta hai. Ek seasoned cricketer catch karte waqt apne hands backward kheenchta hai, isliye ball ko stop hone mein zyada time lagta hai. Momentum ka same change agar zyada time tak spread ho to smaller force chahiye, isliye uske hands hurt nahi hote. Ek novice jo apne hands fixed rakhta hai, use ball ko almost instantly stop karna padta hai, usse bahut larger force chahiye, aur usse hurt hota hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek batsman ball ko seedha bowler ki direction mein wapas hit karta hai bina uski initial speed (chaal) 12 m s-1 badle. Agar ball ka mass 0.15 kg hai, to ball par diya gaya impulse determine karein. (Ball ki linear motion (gati) assume karein.)

  1. Ek positive direction chunein Bowler se batsman ki direction ko positive maanein. Initial momentum (samveg) = 0.15 × 12 = 1.8 kg m s-1.
  2. Final momentum Hit ke baad ball same speed se wapas move karti hai, isliye uska final momentum −1.8 kg m s-1 hai.
  3. Momentum mein change Momentum mein change = 0.15 × 12 − (−0.15 × 12) = 3.6 N s, aur impulse is change ke barabar hai.

Uttar (Answer) Impulse = 3.6 N s, batsman se bowler ki direction mein. Ball par force (bal) aur contact time yahan pata karna mushkil hai, lekin impulse calculate karna aasan hai.

20 kg mass ke ek body par 50 N ka constant retarding force (bal) lagaya jaata hai; body initially 15 m s-1 speed (chaal) se move kar rahi hai. Body ko stop hone mein kitna time lagega? Answer seconds mein dijiye.

Kyon?

Retardation a = F/m = 50/20 = 2.5 m s-2 hai. Iska matlab body har second 2.5 m s-1 speed (chaal) kho deti hai, isliye uska 15 m s-1 speed 15/2.5 = 6 s mein use ho jaata hai.

Doosra law batata hai ki external force (bahari bal) kya karta hai; teesra law batata hai ki aisa force kahan se aata hai. Newtonian mechanics mein kisi body par external force hamesha kisi doosri body se hi aata hai. Ek coiled spring ko dabaiye, to spring aapke haath par wapas push karti hai. Earth ek stone ko neeche kheenchti hai, aur stone earth ko barabar aur opposite force se upar kheenchta hai, jise hum notice nahi karte kyunki earth bahut massive hai.

Toh force kabhi akela nahi hota: force do bodies ke beech mutual interaction hai, aur forces hamesha pairs mein hote hain. Newton ne ise aise kaha: har action ke liye hamesha ek equal aur opposite reaction hota hai. Symbols mein, B dwara A par lagne wala force minus B par A dwara lagne wala force ke barabar hai: F_AB = −F_BA.

Teen warnings. Action aur reaction shabd 'force' se zyada kuch mean nahi karte. Inme koi cause-and-effect order nahi hota - dono forces same instant par act karte hain, aur dono mein se kisi ko bhi action kaha ja sakta hai. Aur dono forces different bodies par act karte hain, isliye agar aap ek body ke motion (gati) ka study kar rahe hain, to pair mein se sirf ek relevant hai; unhe add karke yeh claim karna ki net force zero hai, ek error hai. Sirf jab dono bodies ko ek system maana jaye tab hi yeh pair internal forces ban jaate hain aur cancel ho jaate hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

Do identical billiard balls ek rigid wall se same speed (chaal) par lekin different angles par takrate hain, aur speed mein koi change ke bina reflect ho jaati hain. Case (a) mein ek ball wall par normally takrati hai aur seedha wapas bounce karti hai. Case (b) mein ek ball wall par apni velocity (veg) ke saath normal se 30° par takrati hai aur doosri side par normal se 30° par reflect hoti hai. (i) har ball ke karan wall par force (bal) ki direction kya hai, aur (ii) wall dwara balls ko diye gaye Impulse ke magnitudes ka ratio kya hai?

  1. Axes set karein Maan lijiye collision se pehle aur baad mein har ball ki speed u hai aur uski mass m hai. x-axis ko wall ke normal ke along lijiye, wall se away direction negative, aur y-axis ko wall ke along lijiye.
  2. Case (a): normal hit Initial p_x = m u, p_y = 0; final p_x = −m u, p_y = 0. Isliye impulse ka x-component −2 m u hai aur y-component 0 hai.
  3. Case (b): normal se 30° par hit Initial p_x = m u cos 30°, p_y = −m u sin 30°; final p_x = −m u cos 30°, p_y = −m u sin 30°. Sirf p_x ka sign badalta hai, isliye impulse ka x-component −2 m u cos 30° hai aur y-component 0 hai.
  4. Teesra law use karein Impulse aur force same direction mein hote hain. Dono cases mein ball par impulse wall ke normal hai; teesre law ke according ball ke karan wall par force wall ke normal hai, dono cases mein.

Uttar (Answer) Wall par force dono cases mein wall ke normal hai. Impulse magnitudes ka ratio 2 m u /(2 m u cos 30°) = 2/√3 hai, lagbhag 1.2. Force ka magnitude nahi nikal sakte, kyunki chhota contact time nahi diya gaya hai.

Ek book table par rest karti hai. Newton ke third law ke sense mein inmein se kaunsa action-reaction pair hai?

Kyon?

Action-reaction pair same do bodies ke beech ke do mutual forces hote hain — yahan book aur earth. Book ka weight aur us par lagne wala normal force ek hi body par do forces hain; book rest par hone ki wajah se ye equal aur opposite hain, lekin ye third-law pair nahin hain.

Doosra aur teesra law milkar momentum (samveg) ke conservation ka law dete hain. Ek gun se bullet fire karo. Bullet par gun dwara lagaya gaya force (bal) F hai; teesre law ke anusar gun par bullet dwara lagaya gaya force −F hai; dono same time interval ke liye act karte hain. Isliye bullet ke momentum mein change F times woh interval hai, aur gun ka change ulta hai. Dono rest se start karte hain, isliye unke final momenta equal aur opposite hain, aur (bullet + gun) system ka total momentum zero rehta hai.

Ek isolated system mein - jismein koi external force nahi hai - particles ke jodo ke beech mutual forces individual particles ka momentum change karte hain, lekin har jode ke liye changes equal aur opposite hote hain aur cancel ho jate hain. Isliye total momentum unchanged rehta hai. Momentum ke conservation ka law kehta hai: interacting particles ke isolated system ka total momentum conserved rehta hai.

Collisions standard example hain. Do bodies A aur B jinke initial momenta p_A aur p_B hain, collide karte hain aur p′_A aur p′_B momenta ke saath separate hote hain. Second law ke anusar, contact ke common time ka use karte hue, F_AB times woh time = p′_A − p_A aur F_BA times woh time = p′_B − p_B. Kyunki F_AB = −F_BA, humein milta hai p′_A + p′_B = p_A + p_B: final total momentum initial total momentum ke barabar hai. Yeh sach hai chahe collision elastic ho ya inelastic.

Solved example (Worked example)

0.020 kg mass ka ek shell 100 kg mass ki gun se fire kiya jata hai. Agar shell ki muzzle speed (chaal) 80 m s-1 hai, to gun ki recoil speed kya hai?

  1. Firing se pehle Shell aur gun dono rest par hain, isliye system ka total momentum (samveg) zero hai.
  2. Conservation of momentum Barrel ke along koi external force (bal) nahi hai, isliye total momentum zero hi rehta hai: mass of shell × speed of shell + mass of gun × speed of gun = 0.
  3. Solve 0.020 × 80 = 100 × v_gun, isliye v_gun = 1.6/100.

Uttar (Answer) Gun 0.016 m s-1 ki speed se recoil karti hai, shell ki direction ke opposite.

Do billiard balls, har ek ka mass 0.05 kg, opposite directions mein speed 6 m s-1 se move karte hain, collide karte hain aur same speed se rebound karte hain. Ek ball ko doosri ball ke karan mila impulse ka magnitude kya hai? Answer N s mein dijiye.

Kyon?

Ek ball ki initial direction ko positive maaniye. Iska momentum 0.05 × 6 = 0.3 kg m s-1 se −0.3 kg m s-1 ho jata hai. Change 0.6 kg m s-1 hai, isliye impulse 0.6 N s hai, jo us ball ki original motion ke opposite directed hai.

Mechanics mein ek particle equilibrium mein hota hai jab us par net external force (bal) zero ho. First law ke anusar, iska matlab hai particle ya to rest mein hai ya uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein.

Do forces F1 aur F2 ke under, equilibrium ke liye F1 = −F2 chahiye: dono forces equal aur opposite hone chahiye. Teen concurrent forces F1, F2 aur F3 ke under, equilibrium ke liye F1 + F2 + F3 = 0 chahiye. Inme se kisi bhi do ka resultant, parallelogram law se nikal kar, teesre ke equal aur opposite hona chahiye; teeno forces ko ek triangle ki sides ke roop mein draw kiya ja sakta hai jisme arrows same sense mein hon.

Yeh result kisi bhi number ke forces ke liye generalise hota hai: woh ek particle ko equilibrium mein rakhte hain agar unhe ek closed n-sided polygon ki sides se represent kiya ja sake jisme arrows same sense mein hon. Components mein, x, y aur z ke har sum alag-alag zero hona chahiye.

Solved example (Worked example)

6 kg ka mass ceiling se 2 m lambi rope se suspend kiya gaya hai. Rope ke mid-point P par horizontal direction mein 50 N ka force (bal) lagaya jata hai. Equilibrium mein rope vertical ke saath kitna angle banati hai? (g = 10 m s-2 lijiye.) Rope ke mass ko neglect kijiye.

  1. Suspended weight ko dekhein Weight apne khud ke weight aur rope ke lower part mein tension T2 ke under equilibrium mein hai, isliye T2 = 6 × 10 = 60 N.
  2. Point P ko dekhein P par teen forces act karti hain: rope ke upper part ke along tension T1, lower part ke along (vertically down) tension T2 = 60 N, aur 50 N ka horizontal force (bal).
  3. P par resolve kijiye Vertical components cancel hone chahiye: T1 cos θ = T2 = 60 N. Horizontal components cancel hone chahiye: T1 sin θ = 50 N.
  4. Solve kijiye Do equations ko divide kijiye: tan θ = 50/60 = 0.83, isliye θ lagbhag 40° hai. Phir T1 = √(60² + 50²) ≈ 78 N.

Uttar (Answer) Rope vertical ke saath lagbhag 40° ka angle banati hai. Answer rope ki length (assumed massless) par depend nahi karta, aur na hi us point par jahan horizontal force (bal) lagaya jata hai.

Teen concurrent forces (bal) ek particle par act karte hain jo equilibrium mein hai. Kaun sa statement zaroor sahi hona chahiye?

Kyon?

Ek particle ka equilibrium ka matlab zero net external force (bahari bal) hai, isliye F1 + F2 + F3 = 0, aur har component ka sum alag-alag zero hota hai. Particle uniform motion (eksamaan gati) mein bhi ho sakta hai, zaroori nahi ki rest mein ho.

Kai tarah ke force (bal) mechanics mein baar-baar aate hain. Gravitational force pervasive hai: earth par har object earth ke pull ko feel karta hai, aur gravity celestial bodies ki motion (gati) ko bhi govern karta hai. Ye distance par act karta hai, bina kisi medium ke beech mein.

Baaki sab common forces contact forces hain, jo tab arise hote hain jab ek object doosre object ko touch karta hai, solid ho ya fluid. Jab do bodies contact mein hote hain to mutual contact forces hote hain jo third law satisfy karte hain. Contact force ka component jo surfaces ke normal hota hai use normal reaction kehte hain; surfaces ke parallel component ko friction kehte hain.

Contact forces solid aur fluid ke beech bhi arise hote hain: fluid mein immersed solid ko upward buoyant force feel hota hai jo displaced fluid ke weight ke equal hota hai, aur viscous force aur air resistance bhi contact forces hain. String mein tension aur spring ka force further examples hain. Spring ko amount x tak compress ya stretch karne par restoring force F = −k x exert hota hai, jahan k force constant hai; minus sign batata hai ki force unstretched state se displacement (visthapan) ke opposite hai.

Inextensible string ke liye force constant bahut high hota hai aur restoring force ko tension kehte hain. Negligible mass ki string ke liye tension T ko poore string mein same maan sakte hain. Microscopic level par ye sab contact forces bodies ke charged constituents ke beech electrical forces se aate hain, lekin unka detailed origin complex hai, isliye mechanics mein inhe separate kinds of force maana jata hai jinki properties experiment se milti hain.

Ek block ko horizontal table par rakhiye. Gravity mg ko normal reaction N cancel kar deta hai. Ab ek chhota horizontal force F apply kijiye. Ho sakta hai block bilkul move na kare - lekin agar F hi only horizontal force hota to use accelerate karna padta. Isliye ek aur horizontal force F ko oppose karna chahiye, jisse net force zero rahe. Wahi force, surface ke parallel, friction hai.

Jab tak block rest par rehta hai, friction static friction f_s hoti hai. Ye apne aap exist nahi karti: jis pal applied force aata hai, usi pal ye appear hoti hai, aur applied force ke equal aur opposite rehne ke liye grow karti hai. Static friction impending motion ko oppose karti hai - wo motion jo friction ke absent hone par hoti.

Static friction ki ek limit hoti hai. Experiment show karta hai ki limiting value (f_s)max area of contact par depend nahi karti aur normal force ke saath badalti hai as (f_s)max = µ_s N, jahan µ_s coefficient of static friction hai, ek constant jo sirf do surfaces ki nature par depend karta hai. Isliye f_s ≤ µ_s N. Jab applied force (f_s)max se exceed karta hai, block slide karna shuru karta hai, aur friction ghat kar chhoti value par aa jati hai: kinetic (sliding) friction f_k = µ_k N, phir bhi area of contact par independent aur velocity par lagbhag independent, µ_k ke saath µ_s se kam.

Slide karte waqt block ka acceleration (F − f_k)/m hota hai; constant velocity par F = f_k exactly; applied force hata dijiye to acceleration − f_k/m rehta hai jab tak block rukta hai. Dhyan dijiye ki friction relative motion ko oppose karti hai, motion ko nahi: ek accelerating train ke floor par rakha box static friction se aage accelerate hota hai, aur train ke relative rest par rehta hai.

Ek ring ya sphere jo bina slipping ke roll karta hai, principle mein bilkul friction feel nahi karega, kyunki uska single point of contact plane ke saath relative motion nahi rakhta. Practice mein kuch rolling friction hoti hai, aur same weight ke liye ye static ya sliding friction se bahut chhoti hoti hai, even 2 or 3 orders of magnitude tak - isi liye wheel itna bada milestone raha hai. Lubricants, ball bearings aur air ki thin cushion friction ko reduce karte hain, lekin friction essential bhi hai: brakes kinetic friction use karke machines aur vehicles ko stop karte hain, aur hum sirf friction ki wajah se chal paate hain.

Solved example (Worked example)

4 kg ka mass ek horizontal plane par rest karta hai. Plane ko dheere-dheere inclined kiya jata hai jab tak horizontal ke saath angle θ = 15° par mass just slide karna shuru nahi kar deta. Block aur surface ke beech coefficient of static friction kya hai?

  1. Block par lagne wali forces (bal) Iska weight mg vertically downward act karta hai, plane ka normal force N, aur static frictional force f_s jo impending motion (gati) ka opposition karti hai.
  2. Weight ko resolve karo Slope (dhaal) ke along weight component mg sin θ hai; slope ke perpendicular ye mg cos θ hai. Equilibrium mein mg sin θ = f_s aur mg cos θ = N.
  3. Sliding ke point par Jab θ badhta hai, self-adjusting f_s badhta hai jab tak θ_max par ye apna maximum value µ_s N tak nahi pahunch jata. Tab mg sin θ_max = µ_s mg cos θ_max.
  4. Incline ke liye friction law Divide karne par tan θ_max = µ_s milta hai, aur θ_max sirf µ_s par depend karta hai, block ke mass par nahi.
  5. Numbers insert karo θ_max = 15° ke liye, µ_s = tan 15° = 0.27.

Uttar (Answer) µ_s = 0.27. 15° se thoda zyada par, slope ke neeche chhota net force block ko slide karna shuru kara deta hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek box train ke floor par rakha hai. Box aur floor ke beech coefficient of static friction 0.15 hai. Train ka maximum acceleration (tvaran) determine karo jiske liye box stationary rahega. (g = 10 m s-2 lo.)

  1. Box ko kaunsi force accelerate karti hai? Agar box train ke relative stationary hai, to ye train ke saath accelerate karta hai, aur iske liye available sirf horizontal force static friction hai.
  2. Box ke liye second law m a = f_s, aur static friction µ_s N = µ_s m g se zyada nahi ho sakta.
  3. Inequality To m a ≤ µ_s m g, matlab a ≤ µ_s g.

Uttar (Answer) a_max = µ_s g = 0.15 × 10 m s-2 = 1.5 m s-2.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek 3 kg block ek string se latka hai jo smooth pulley ke upar se jaati hai aur horizontal surface par rakhi 20 kg trolley se bandhi hai. Trolley aur surface ke beech coefficient of kinetic friction 0.04 hai. System ka acceleration aur string mein tension nikalo. (g = 10 m s-2 lo. String ka mass neglect karo.)

  1. Ek hi acceleration String inextensible hai aur pulley smooth hai, isliye 3 kg block aur 20 kg trolley ka acceleration magnitude same a hai.
  2. Block ke liye equation Block ka weight 30 N hai aur woh neeche move karta hai: 30 − T = 3a.
  3. Trolley ke liye equation T − f_k = 20a, jahan f_k = µ_k N = 0.04 × (20 × 10) = 8 N. Isliye T − 8 = 20a.
  4. Add karke solve karo Dono equations add karne par: 30 − 8 = 23a, isliye a = 22/23 m s-2.
  5. Tension T = 8 + 20a = 27.1 N.

Uttar (Answer) a = 22/23 m s-2 ≈ 0.96 m s-2, aur T ≈ 27.1 N.

Aap ek rough horizontal table par rakhe block ko ek chhota horizontal force (bal) se push karte hain. Block move nahi hota. Us instant par block par friction force kya hai?

Kyon?

Static friction self-adjusting hota hai: yeh applied force ke barabar hone tak badhta hai, µ_s N limit tak. Sirf jab block slide hone wala hota hai tab f_s = µ_s N hota hai. Kinetic friction sirf sliding shuru hone ke baad act karta hai.

Ek body jo radius R ke circle mein uniform speed (chaal) v se move karti hai, uska acceleration (tvaran) v²/R hota hai jo centre ki taraf directed hota hai. Second law ke according, is acceleration ko provide karne wala force (bal) f_c = m v²/R hai, jo bhi centre ki taraf directed hota hai. Is force ko centripetal force kaha jata hai.

Centripetal force koi naya type ka force nahi hai. Yeh sirf us real force ka naam hai jo centre ki taraf point karta ho: string par whirled stone ke liye yeh string mein tension hai, sun ke around ghoomte planet ke liye yeh sun ka gravitational pull hai, aur horizontal road par turn karti car ke liye yeh tyres aur road ke beech friction hai.

Level road par car ki maximum speed (chaal)

Step 1: teen forces (bal)

Car par teen forces act karte hain: uska weight mg, Normal reaction N, aur friction f.

Step 2: koi vertical acceleration nahi

N − mg = 0, isliye N = mg.

Step 3: Centripetal force kaun supply karta hai?

Centripetal force road ke along, turn ke centre ki taraf act karna chahiye. Sirf horizontal force available friction hai, isliye f = m v²/R.

Step 4: friction ki ek limit hoti hai

Static friction f ≤ µ_s N = µ_s m g satisfy karta hai.

Step 5: combine karo

m v²/R ≤ µ_s m g se v² ≤ µ_s R g milta hai. Mass cancel ho jata hai, isliye answer car ke mass par depend nahi karta.

Level circular turn par maximum safe speed (chaal)

Level (unbanked) road par centripetal force sirf friction se milta hai, isliye maximum safe speed v_max = √(µ_s R g) hoti hai. Plot aapke chune hue coefficient of static friction ke liye v_max versus turn radius R ki yeh curve dikhata hai, aur aapke chune hue radius par value print karta hai. g = 10 m s-2 lein.

Banked road par turn ka outer edge utha hua hota hai, isliye road surface horizontal ke saath angle θ banata hai. Tab Normal reaction ka ek horizontal component centre ki taraf hota hai, aur friction ko kam kaam karna padta hai.

Vertical direction mein abhi bhi koi acceleration nahi hai, isliye vertical forces cancel honi chahiye: N cos θ = mg + f sin θ. Centripetal force N aur f ke horizontal components milakar supply hota hai: N sin θ + f cos θ = m v²/R.

Maximum permissible speed nikalne ke liye friction ko uski limit par rakho, f = µ_s N, aur in dono equations ko saath solve karo.

Banked road par maximum aur optimum speed (chaal)

Step 1: f ko uski limit par set karo

Do banked-road equations mein f = µ_s N set karo: N cos θ = mg + µ_s N sin θ, aur N sin θ + µ_s N cos θ = m v²/R.

Step 2: N nikaalo

Pehli equation se, N = mg/(cos θ − µ_s sin θ).

Step 3: substitute karo

Is N ko doosri equation mein rakhne aur simplify karne se milta hai v²_max = R g (tan θ + µ_s)/(1 − µ_s tan θ).

Step 4: level road se compare karo

Banked road par car ki maximum possible speed (chaal) flat road ke muqable zyada hoti hai.

Step 5: friction off karo

µ_s = 0 rakhne par result v_o = √(R g tan θ) ho jaata hai. Is optimum speed (chaal) par friction ki zaroorat bilkul nahi hoti, isliye is par drive karne se tyres ka wear and tear kam hota hai. v < v_o ke liye friction slope (dhaal) ke upar act karta hai, aur car sirf tab park ho sakti hai jab tan θ ≤ µ_s.

Solved example (Worked example)

Ek cyclist level road par 18 km/h ki speed (chaal) se ja raha hai aur speed kam kiye bina radius 3 m ka sharp circular turn leta hai. Tyres aur road ke beech coefficient of static friction 0.1 hai. Kya cyclist turn lete waqt slip karega? (g = 9.8 m s-2 lo.)

  1. Not slipping ki condition Unbanked road par sirf friction hi centripetal force deta hai, isliye cyclist tabhi nahi slip karega jab v² ≤ µ_s R g ho.
  2. Speed (chaal) ko convert karo 18 km/h = 5 m s-1, isliye v² = 25 m2 s-2.
  3. Limit compute karo µ_s R g = 0.1 × 3 × 9.8 = 2.94 m2 s-2.
  4. Compare karo 25 m2 s-2, 2.94 m2 s-2 se kaafi zyada hai, isliye condition follow nahi hoti.

Uttar (Answer) Cyclist slip kar jayega. Speed bahut zyada hai, ya turn bahut sharp hai, itna ki friction needed centripetal force supply nahi kar sakta.

Solved example (Worked example)

Radius 300 m ka ek circular racetrack 15° ke angle par banked hai. Agar race-car ke wheels aur road ke beech coefficient of friction 0.2 hai, to (a) tyres par wear and tear avoid karne ke liye race-car ki optimum speed (chaal) kya hai, aur (b) slipping avoid karne ke liye maximum permissible speed kya hai? (g = 9.8 m s-2 lo.)

  1. (a) Optimum speed Optimum speed par normal reaction ka horizontal component akela centripetal force provide karta hai aur friction ki zarurat nahi hoti: v_o = √(R g tan θ).
  2. Numbers insert karo v_o = √(300 × 9.8 × tan 15°) = √(300 × 9.8 × 0.268) ≈ 28.1 m s-1.
  3. (b) Maximum speed v_max = √(R g (tan θ + µ_s)/(1 − µ_s tan θ)) use karo.
  4. Numbers insert karo tan 15° = 0.268 aur µ_s = 0.2 ke saath: (0.268 + 0.2)/(1 − 0.2 × 0.268) = 0.468/0.946 ≈ 0.494. Phir v_max = √(300 × 9.8 × 0.494) ≈ 38.1 m s-1.

Uttar (Answer) (a) Optimum speed ≈ 28.1 m s-1. (b) Maximum permissible speed ≈ 38.1 m s-1.

Zyadatar real problems mein kai bodies ek doosre par push aur pull karti hain, aur unme se har ek par gravity bhi lagti hai. Working method hamesha same hota hai: assembly ke kisi bhi part ko apna system chuno aur us par laws of motion apply karo, bas shart ye hai ki remaining parts ki wajah se us part par lagne wali saari forces include karo.

Step by step: (i) assembly ka ek diagram banao, jisme links aur supports dikhayein; (ii) assembly ke ek convenient part ko system ke roop mein chuno; (iii) us system ka alag diagram banao jisme rest of the assembly aur other agencies dwara us par lagne wali saari forces dikhayein, lekin environment par woh khud jo forces lagata hai woh nahi. Is type ke diagram ko free-body diagram kehte hain - aur iska matlab ye nahi ki system par net force zero hai.

(iv) Free-body diagram par un forces ko likho jinke magnitudes aur directions diye gaye hain ya certain hain, jaise kisi tension ki direction uski length ke along; baaki ko unknowns maano jo laws of motion se nikalne hain. (v) Agar aapko second system chahiye, to procedure repeat karo aur third law use karo: agar A par B ki wajah se force A ke diagram mein F hai, to B par A ki wajah se force B ke diagram mein −F hai.

Solved example (Worked example)

2 kg mass ka ek wooden block ek soft horizontal floor par rest karta hai. Jab 25 kg mass ka iron cylinder block ke upar rakha jata hai, floor steadily yield karta hai aur block aur cylinder dono milkar 0.1 m s-2 acceleration (tvaran) se neeche jate hain. Floor par block ka action kya hai (a) floor yield karne se pehle aur (b) baad mein? g = 10 m s-2 lo. Problem mein action-reaction pairs identify karo.

  1. (a) Pehle: block ka free-body diagram Block par do forces (bal) lagti hain: gravity 2 × 10 = 20 N neeche, aur floor ka normal force R upar. Block rest par hai, isliye first law se R = 20 N.
  2. (a) Third law Floor par block ka action 20 N vertically neeche hai.
  3. (b) System block + cylinder chuno Iska mass 27 kg hai, isliye iska weight 270 N hai. Free-body diagram mein weight 270 N neeche aur floor ka normal force R′ upar dikhaya gaya hai; block aur cylinder ke beech internal forces nahi dikhaye gaye.
  4. (b) Second law System 0.1 m s-2 se neeche accelerate karta hai, isliye 270 − R′ = 27 × 0.1 = 2.7 N, jisse R′ = 267.3 N milta hai.
  5. (b) Third law aur pairs Floor par system ka action 267.3 N vertically neeche hai. Pairs ye hain: earth ka block (ya system) par pull aur block (ya system) ka earth par pull; block (ya system) ka floor par push aur floor ka block (ya system) par push; aur, floor yield karne ke baad, cylinder ka block par force aur block ka cylinder par force.

Uttar (Answer) (a) 20 N vertically neeche; (b) 267.3 N vertically neeche. Weight aur normal force action-reaction pair nahi hain: ye same body par lagte hain, aur floor yield karne ke baad ye equal bhi nahi rehte (270 N aur 267.3 N).

70 kg mass ka ek aadmi lift mein weighing scale par khada hai jo neeche ki taraf 5 m s-2 ke uniform acceleration (tvaran) se move kar rahi hai. Scale par reading kya hai? (g = 10 m s-2 lein.) Answer newtons mein dijiye.

Kyon?

Scale us normal force N ko read karti hai jo woh aadmi par exert karti hai. Downward ko positive lete hue, net downward force mg − N = m a hai, isliye N = m(g − a) = 70 × (10 − 5) = 350 N. Agar lift gravity ke under freely girti, to a barabar g hota aur reading zero hoti.

Ek patthar ko string se baandh kar horizontal circle mein ghumaya ja raha hai. Speed (chaal) ko us maximum se zyada badha diya jaata hai jo string seh sakti hai, aur string achanak toot jaati hai. Us pal ke baad patthar ka path kaunsa hoga?

Kyon?

String ka tension wahi force tha jo patthar ke momentum (samveg) ki direction badal raha tha. Jis moment string tootati hai, koi horizontal force act nahi karta (air resistance ko neglect karte hue), isliye first law ke according patthar us instant wali velocity ke saath straight line mein chalta rehta hai — jo tangent ke along hoti hai.

Ghoda empty space mein cart ko kyun pull karke daud nahi sakta?

Kyon?

Horse-and-cart system par forward force system ke bahar se aana chahiye. Ground par yeh friction hoti hai ghode ke hooves aur ground ke beech: ghoda ground ko backward push karta hai, ground use forward push karta hai. Ground ke bina aisi external force nahi hoti, aur system ke andar ke mutual forces use accelerate nahi kar sakte.

Kuch mechanics problems mein aapko forces ki list ke bajaye position vs time ka graph diya hota hai. Aap force aur impulse dono nikal sakte hain, kyunki sab kuch is baat se follow karta hai ki graph ka slope (dhaal) kya kar raha hai.

Ek particle ke liye, velocity (veg) position ke time ke saath rate of change hai, v = dy/dt (y ko position coordinate maan lijiye). Position-time graph par yeh graph ka slope hota hai. Acceleration (tvaran) velocity ke rate of change hai, a = dv/dt, yani slope khud kitni tezi se badal raha hai.

Graph ko teen steps mein padhiye. Agar graph ka koi stretch straight line hai, wahan velocity constant hai, isliye acceleration zero hai aur, second law ke according, us interval mein particle par force zero hai. Agar slope steadily badalta hai, velocity badal rahi hai, isliye acceleration aur force F = m a hai. Agar slope kisi ek instant par abruptly badalta hai - graph mein kink - to velocity almost no time mein definite amount se badalti hai, isliye momentum abruptly badalta hai: momentum ka woh change us instant par delivered impulse hai.

Mass do baar aata hai: acceleration ko force mein badalne ke liye, F = m a, aur velocity ke change ko impulse mein badalne ke liye, impulse = change in momentum = m times the change in velocity. Kisi bhi instant par force us instant ki situation se fix hota hai; particle apni earlier motion ki koi memory nahi rakhta.

Solved example (Worked example)

0.25 kg mass ka ek stone string ke end se tied hai aur horizontal plane mein 1.5 m radius ke circle mein 40 rev./min ki speed (chaal) se whirled round kiya ja raha hai. String mein tension kya hai? Agar string maximum 200 N tension seh sakti hai, to stone ko kis maximum speed se whirled around kiya ja sakta hai?

  1. Centripetal force kaun supply karta hai? Stone uniform speed se circle mein move karta hai, isliye isko centre ki taraf directed force (bal) m v^2/R chahiye. Yahan centre ki taraf act karne wala sirf string mein tension hai, isliye tension hi centripetal force hai: T = m v^2/R.
  2. Speed ko m s-1 mein convert karein Ek revolution mein stone circumference 2 pi R = 2 pi times 1.5, lagbhag 9.42 m travel karta hai. 40 revolutions per minute par yeh har second 40/60 revolution karta hai, isliye v = 9.42 times 40/60, lagbhag 6.28 m s-1.
  3. String mein tension T = m v^2/R = 0.25 times (6.28)^2 / 1.5, jo lagbhag 6.6 N hai.
  4. Maximum tension se maximum speed String maximum 200 N de sakti hai, isliye m v^2/R = 200 se v = square root of (200 times 1.5 / 0.25) = square root of 1200, lagbhag 34.6 m s-1.

Uttar (Answer) Tension lagbhag 6.6 N hai. Maximum speed jisse stone ko whirled kiya ja sakta hai lagbhag 34.6 m s-1 hai; usse zyada par string ko 200 N se zyada supply karna padega aur woh break ho jayegi.

(g = 10 m s-2 lein.)

  1. Kaunse forces act karte hain? Air resistance neglect kiya gaya hai, isliye kisi bhi instant par pebble par sirf uska weight, mg, vertically downward direction mein lagta hai. Earth use tab bhi pull kar rahi hai jab woh upar jaata hai, neeche aata hai, ya ek instant ke liye still hangs karta hai.

  3. Highest point Highest point par velocity (veg) momentarily zero hoti hai, lekin isse force zero nahi ho jaata. Force aur acceleration (tvaran) us instant par body ki location ki situation se fixed hote hain, is baat se nahi ki woh kitni fast move kar raha hai: force ab bhi uska weight mg hai aur acceleration ab bhi g hai, downward.

Lawn mower ko push karne se zyada pull karna aasan kyun hota hai? Iska jawab un do cheezon se milta hai jo aap pehle se jaante hain: normal reaction aur friction.

Jab do surfaces contact mein hoti hain, contact force ka ek component surfaces ke normal hota hai, normal reaction N, aur ek component unke parallel hota hai, friction. Friction ke laws kehte hain ki sliding friction f_k = mu_k N hota hai (aur static friction at most mu_s N tak badh sakta hai). Isliye jis friction ka aapko samna karna hai woh normal reaction ke proportional hota hai: N ko chhota banao aur friction bhi chhota ho jaata hai.

Mower ko aise handle se pull karo jo upward slant karta hai, aur aapke pull ka ek upward component hota hai. Woh upward component mower ko partly support karta hai, isliye floor ko kam push up karna padta hai: normal reaction N chhota ho jaata hai, aur uske saath woh friction bhi chhota ho jaata hai jise mower ko overcome karna hota hai. Usi mower ko downward slanting handle se push karo, aur aapke push ka ek downward component hota hai jo mower ko ground mein zyada dabata hai: N bada ho jaata hai, aur friction bhi bada ho jaata hai. Same mower, same ground - sirf handle par aapke force ki direction badli hai, aur uske saath normal reaction aur friction bhi.

4 kg mass wale particle ka position-time graph t = 0 se t = 4 s tak ek straight line hai, aur t = 4 s ke baad different slope wali ek straight line hai. t = 0 se t = 4 s ke interval mein particle par force kya hai?

Kyon?

Straight position-time graph ka matlab hai velocity change nahi ho rahi, isliye acceleration zero hai aur second law ke according net force zero hai. Kisi bhi instant par force sirf usi instant par depend karti hai, t = 4 s ke baad kya hota hai uspar nahi. t = 4 s par slope abruptly change hota hai, isliye velocity suddenly change hoti hai aur wahan momentum ke us change ke barabar ek Impulse deliver hota hai.

0.25 kg mass ka ek stone 1.5 m lambi string se bandha hai aur horizontal circle mein ghumaya ja raha hai. Agar string maximum 200 N tension seh sakti hai, toh stone ko maximum kis speed (chaal) se ghumaya ja sakta hai? Answer ko m s-1 mein do, ek decimal place tak.

Kyon?

Tension hi centripetal force hai, isliye m v^2/R = T se v = square root of (T R / m) = square root of (200 times 1.5 / 0.25) = square root of 1200, lagbhag 34.6 m s-1. Is speed se zyada hone par required centripetal force 200 N se zyada ho jayega aur string toot jayegi.

0.05 kg mass ka ek pebble vertically upwards throw kiya jata hai aur, apne path ke highest point par, momentarily rest mein hota hai. Air resistance ignore karte hue aur g = 10 m s-2 lete hue, us instant par is par lagne wale net force (bal) ka magnitude newtons mein kya hai?

Kyon?

Sirf force weight mg = 0.05 times 10 = 0.5 N hai, jo downward directed hai, aur highest point par bhi ye anywhere else jaisa hi same hai. Momentarily rest hone se velocity (veg) zero hoti hai, force nahi: wahan acceleration (tvaran) g hai, downward.

Lawn mower ko push karne ki tulna mein pull karna zyada aasan kyun hota hai?

Kyon?

Friction contact force ka woh component hai jo surfaces ke parallel hota hai, aur iska magnitude normal reaction N ke proportional hota hai. Upward-slanting pull mower ko kuch had tak support karta hai, jisse N kam hota hai aur isliye friction bhi kam hota hai; downward-slanting push mower ko ground mein dabata hai, jisse N badh jata hai aur isliye friction bhi badh jata hai.

Glossary (Shabdavali)

Inertia
Body ki apni rest state ya uniform motion mein kisi bhi change ko resist karne ki property.
Momentum
Mass times velocity, p = m v; velocity ke along point karta vector.
Impulse
Force ko uske act karne ke time se multiply karna; yeh change in momentum ke equal hota hai.
Equilibrium
Particle ki woh state jab us par net external force zero ho.
Normal reaction
Contact force ka woh part jo contact surfaces ke perpendicular hai.
Static friction
