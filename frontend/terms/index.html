Terms · Prototype

The short version.

Zivon is a prototype study aid. Use it to learn; do not treat it as an official textbook, an official exam paper, or a substitute for your teacher. These terms are written in plain language and will be replaced by a formal agreement before accounts open.

Last updated 16 November 2026

1. What Zivon is.

Zivon is a website with interactive Physics lessons for Class 9–12, built from NCERT and openly licensed sources. Reading, diagrams, simulations and checks are free during launch. Accounts, paid plans, practice sets and chapter tests are not built yet.

2. Accounts and age.

You do not need an account to read. Accounts are not open. When they arrive, they will be optional and will follow the children's-data rules on the privacy page: private by default, no ads, no sale of data, and a deletion path.

3. Content and sources.

Lessons are paraphrased from NCERT and openly licensed material. Each lesson names its source. Zivon is not affiliated with CBSE or NCERT, and a Zivon page is not an official textbook page, an official exam paper, or an official marking scheme.

Content can contain mistakes. The source line and the problem report exist so a mistake can be found and fixed. Do not rely on a single Zivon page as your only revision for an exam.

4. Acceptable use.

  • Do not scrape, bulk-download, or re-publish the lessons or their audio as your own product.
  • Do not attempt to break, overload, or bypass the site's security or rate limits.
  • Do not use the site to harass anyone or to upload unlawful material.
  • Personal study, classroom demonstration, and linking to a public lesson are fine.

5. Your data.

The privacy page is the detailed notice. In short: raw voice audio is not kept, transcripts have a 7-day server retention window, there are no third-party trackers, and the privacy page can clear local data and request server-side deletion.

6. Availability and changes.

The site is free during launch and may change, pause, or stop. There is no uptime guarantee and no paid contract. Lessons can be corrected or removed when a source or a review requires it.

These terms can change as the product changes. The date at the top is the version date.

7. Contact.

Report a bug, a content error, or a rights concern through the contact page. Do not include a child's name, phone number, school, or a screenshot with personal details.

These terms are a product notice, not legal advice. A formal policy will replace them before accounts or payments open.
