Pricing

Free during launch.

There is no paid plan, no payment integration and no waitlist form yet. This page says what is free now and what does not exist, so nobody has to guess.

Last updated 16 November 2026

What is free today.

  • Every published lesson: text, diagrams, simulations and checks.
  • All three languages on a live lesson: English, हिन्दी and Hinglish.
  • Reading without an account, on a phone or a laptop.
  • The voice tutor, within a small daily voice-credit budget. Reading, simulations, checks and typing do not use that budget.

There is no card, no trial countdown and no auto-renew. If a paid plan ever exists, the price and what it includes will be on this page before anyone can pay it.

The paid plan is not decided.

The product plan has a Pro tier and a waitlist. Neither is open. A waitlist needs accounts and a place to keep the email; accounts are a later wave, so this page does not show an email field that would go nowhere.

When the waitlist opens, it will say what is being collected, why, and how to leave it. No fake urgency, no “only 10 seats”, no price that changes when the clock runs out.

For schools.

A schools page and a schools waitlist are planned but not open. There is no teacher dashboard and no school billing. If you are a teacher who wants to use a live chapter with a class, use the contact page; do not wait for a form.

What happens when you press “Open a chapter”.

You go to the catalogue. You pick a published chapter and read it. No account, no card, no trial gate.
