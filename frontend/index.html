How it works

The answer is on the page.

Not a chat window beside a PDF. The tutor reads the block you are on, then points back at it.

  1. 01

    You speak.

    Your voice becomes text. English, Hindi, or the Hinglish you actually speak.

  2. 02

    The page is the context.

    The tutor gets the chapter, the block, and the diagram you are looking at.

  3. 03

    You hear the step.

    The answer comes back in your language, with the sentence that matters underlined.

Proof, not trust me

Every explanation can be traced.

Each lesson is written from a named NCERT chapter, and a fact review compares the page against that source pack before it publishes. The tutor can be wrong — the source pack is recorded with the lesson, and the problem report is one tap away.

Source packs: NCERT Science, Grade 9 (2025) · NCERT Physics Part I, Class XI · a source pack recorded with every lesson.

Zivon is not affiliated with CBSE or NCERT. Lessons are paraphrased from officially free NCERT material and openly licensed sources; they are not official textbook reproductions.

Still wondering

Questions a student actually asks.

Is this for Class 9 or Class 10?

As of 16 November 2026, Class 9 and Class 11 have live lessons. Class 10 and Class 12 pages exist but have no published chapter yet. The catalogue says what is open.

Do I need an account?

No. Open a chapter and start. Accounts are not built yet; when they arrive they are for saving progress across devices, not for reading.

Is it free?

Yes during launch. Reading, simulations, checks and typing are free. The voice tutor is in development; when it ships it will use a small daily voice-credit budget.

Is my voice recorded?

Voice is in development. When it ships, no raw audio will be kept: speech will be transcribed, the recording discarded, and the transcript kept for 7 days and deletable.

Will it just give me the answer?

No. It explains the step, points at the page, and asks you to check. Practice and chapter tests are next; the check is how you prove it today.

Is this CBSE?

Lessons are written from NCERT and openly licensed sources. Zivon is not affiliated with CBSE or NCERT. Exam-pattern practice, when it ships, will say clearly what it is and what it is not.
