How it works

The answer is on the page.

Not a chat window beside a PDF. The tutor reads the block you are on, then points back at it.

  1. 01

    You speak.

    Your voice becomes text. English, Hindi, or the Hinglish you actually speak.

  2. 02

    The page is the context.

    The tutor gets the chapter, the block, and the diagram you are looking at.

  3. 03

    You hear the step.

    The answer comes back in your language, with the sentence that matters underlined.

Proof, not trust me

Every explanation can be traced.

Each page names the NCERT chapter it was written from. Generated pages pass a technical review before they publish. The tutor can be wrong — the source is on the page, and the report button is one tap away.

Still wondering

Questions a student actually asks.

Is this for Class 9 or Class 10?

Class 9 is live today. Class 10 is being written; the coverage table above says what is open. Class 11 lessons are live, with practice next.

Do I need an account?

No. Open a chapter and start. An account is for saving progress across devices, not for reading.

Is it free?

Yes during launch. Voice has a small daily credit budget; reading, simulations, checks and typing stay free.

Is my voice recorded?

No raw audio is kept. The speech is transcribed, the recording is discarded, and the transcript is kept for 7 days and can be deleted.

Will it just give me the answer?

No. It explains the step, points at the page, and asks you to check. Practice is next; the check is how you prove it today.

Is this CBSE?

The lessons are written from NCERT and are not affiliated with CBSE. Board-pattern practice, when it ships, says “not an official CBSE paper” on the page.
