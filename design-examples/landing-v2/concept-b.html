Still wondering

Questions before a test.

Is this an official CBSE paper?

No. Board-pattern practice is built for practice and is “close to the paper pattern”. It is not an official CBSE paper and does not claim affiliation.

Will it show my rank or compare me to others?

No. There is no rank, no leaderboard and no parent view. The result is private to you.

Do I need an account?

No for the first test. An account is for keeping your history across devices, not for trying a test.

Is it free?

Yes during launch. The test and the review are free; voice has a small daily budget, and no test needs a microphone.

Are there negative marks?

No. The chapter tests and practice use no negative marking.

Does it work on my phone?

The test player and review are built for a 360 px screen. The page does not depend on a video or a microphone to work.

Can I see why an answer is wrong?

Yes. Every reviewed question shows your answer, the correct answer, one explanation and the next thing to practise.
