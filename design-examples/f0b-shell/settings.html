Privacy & data

What is kept, for how long, and what deletion removes.

Voice audio is transient and never stored. Transcripts and feedback are text, kept for 7 days server-side and deletable. Progress, test attempts, doubts and bookmarks are yours; no parent or cohort view exists.

Open the privacy summary

Delete account and data

This deletes your account, progress, test attempts, doubts, bookmarks and speaking transcripts. It cannot be undone. The action is server-side; a failed delete removes nothing locally and says so.

No “are you sure?” dark pattern; the next screen states exactly what goes.

You're offline

Settings are read-only from the cached copy. Saving, export and deletion are disabled with a reason; nothing is lost.

Zivon · Physics you can talk to. · Settings · Privacy Design example · no backend, no network, no microphone.

Delete everything?

This deletes your account and all of the following:

  • Profile, class, board and language settings;
  • Progress, mastery and SWOT evidence;
  • Practice sessions, test attempts and results;
  • Doubts, bookmarks and speaking transcripts.

Raw audio is not stored, so there is nothing else to delete. This cannot be undone.
