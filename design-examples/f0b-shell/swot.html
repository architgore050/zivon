How the placement works

Zivon looks at your own chapters, not a cohort. It ranks them on two axes: performance (weighted accuracy, adjusted by recent trend and pace) and coverage/effort (questions tried, hints used).

The median split gives four quadrants: high performance + high coverage is a Strength; low performance + high coverage is a Weakness; high performance + low coverage is an Opportunity; low performance + low coverage is a Threat. Exam weight raises the priority, not the quadrant.

Evidence decays with a 21-day half-life and every card shows 2–3 plain-language reasons, including pace. Fewer than three chapters with enough evidence shows “Not enough data yet”. There is no share, parent or cohort view.
