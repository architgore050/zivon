Method, pricing and trust

A plain-language page: how the tutor teaches, what it costs during launch, and what we will never do with a student's data.

How the tutor teaches

Zivon is a patient Physics tutor, not an answer machine. It asks one question at a time, waits for your attempt, and explains the step you are stuck on. The lesson page is the anchor: when you ask a doubt, the tutor can see which block you are reading.

  • Read: a full chapter written out on ruled paper, with sources.
  • Move: simulations and diagrams you can operate, not videos.
  • Ask: speak in English, Hindi, or Hinglish; type if voice is off.
  • Practise and prove: retrieval questions, a chapter test, and an explain-back.

Honest limitations: the tutor can be wrong, simulations are models, and the content is Physics only for now. Every lesson names its source and every generated page passes a technical review gate.

Pricing during launch

Everything a student needs to read, simulate, check, practise and take a chapter test is free during launch. Voice has a small daily credit budget so a live demo stays reliable; reading and typing never depend on it.

₹0 during launch · no card · no trial countdown

No fake urgency, no “best seller” badges, no parent upsell. A Pro waitlist, when it exists, will be a plain form and nothing else.

Privacy, in one screen

Voice is turned into text to answer your doubt; the audio itself is not kept. Progress is private to you: no parent account, no share links, no leaderboard, no public profile. You can delete your data from Settings, and deletion is a real server action.

The longer privacy summary and the clear-my-data flow live on the existing privacy page; this design bundle does not replace that copy.

Terms

Use Zivon to learn Physics. Do not scrape, resell, or misrepresent generated content as an official CBSE paper. Lessons are paraphrased from NCERT and openly licensed sources; citations are on every page.

Board-style practice never claims affiliation or exact reproduction. The approved copy is “close to the paper pattern” and “not an official CBSE paper”.

Contact

Found a wrong answer, a broken simulation, or a page that does not load? Use the report button on the lesson, or write to the address below. Reports never ask for a phone number, and student free text is not put in logs.

hello@zivon.example

Status

All published lessons are served statically, so reading survives an API or voice outage. When the voice backend is down, the lesson stays readable, simulations and checks keep working, and the dock falls back to typed input.

This is a design example, not a live status page. No uptime number is claimed here.
