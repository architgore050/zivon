Net force · Medium · 1 mark

A 5 kg box is pushed across a floor with a horizontal force of 20 N. Friction acts on it with a force of 5 N. What is the acceleration of the box?

You can change the answer before checking.
✓ Correct

That is the right idea. Open “Why?” for the full reasoning.

Why?

The net force is 20 N − 5 N = 15 N. Newton's second law gives a = F / m = 15 N ÷ 5 kg = 3 m/s². The common slip is to divide the applied force by the mass and ignore friction.

Hint margin

First find the net force. Then use F = ma with the mass of the box.
