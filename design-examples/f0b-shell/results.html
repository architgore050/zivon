Q7 A book stays at rest on a table. Which statement about the forces is correct? ✓ Correct · 1/1
Your answer
The forces are balanced, so the net force is zero.
Correct
The forces are balanced, so the net force is zero.

A stationary object has zero acceleration, so the net force on it is zero. Weight and the normal force are equal and opposite.

Q14 Explain why a passenger falls forward when the bus brakes. ✗ Incorrect · 0/2
Your answer
When the driver brakes, the bus slows down. The passenger keeps moving forward because of inertia, so they fall forward.
Correct
When the driver brakes, the bus slows down. The passenger's body tends to keep moving at the original speed because of inertia, so the passenger continues forward relative to the bus and falls forward.

You had the right idea but did not say “relative to the bus”, so the answer read as if the passenger was pushed. The two sentences need the bus slowing and the passenger keeping their speed.

Q17 A 5 kg box is pushed with 20 N while friction is 5 N. What is its acceleration? — Unattempted · 0/1
Your answer
Not answered
Correct
3 m/s²

Net force = 20 N − 5 N = 15 N; a = F / m = 15 ÷ 5 = 3 m/s².

Q20 Explain what happens to a passenger when a bus starts suddenly. ✗ Incorrect · 0/5
Your answer
The passenger is pushed backward by the bus.
Correct
The bus starts moving forward, while the passenger's body tends to stay at rest because of inertia. Relative to the bus, the passenger moves backward.

“Pushed backward” names a force that is not there. The bus moves forward underneath the passenger; inertia keeps the passenger at rest.
