# Gnani AI – Great Indian AI Internship Challenge  
## What They Actually Want to See

**Challenge name:** Great Indian AI Internship Challenge  
**Theme:** Build Sovereign AI for India  
**Deadline:** 10 November 2026, 11:59 PM IST  
**Organiser:** Gnani AI (Bengaluru)

### Core Requirement
Build a **working voice application** that uses Gnani’s own models:

- **Prisma v2.5** → Speech-to-Text (excellent on code-mixed + noisy telephony audio)
- **Timbre v2.5** → Text-to-Speech (natural Indian language prosody)
- **Evon v3.3** → Reasoning LLM (open-weight ~30B MoE)

You must use at least one of these models. Pure competing models or completely independent systems are not accepted.

### What “Good” Looks Like
Gnani is not looking for research papers or pure model fine-tunes. They want:

1. **Practical, working demos** that solve real problems for Indian users.
2. Strong handling of **code-mixed speech** (Hinglish, Tanglish, Benglish, etc.).
3. Robustness to **noisy, real-world telephony audio**.
4. Clear India-first use cases (farmers, kirana stores, students, rural citizens, small businesses, government schemes, etc.).
5. A polished 60-second demo video that is easy to understand.

### Submission Essentials
- Working public demo (GitHub + live link preferred)
- 60-second demo video
- Post the demo on X **and/or** LinkedIn using **both** hashtags:  
  `#GreatIndianAIInternshipChallenge` `#GnaniAI`
- Nominate the posts so Gnani can verify engagement
- Shortlisted people go through individual interviews

### Selection Reality
- Internship shortlist is driven heavily by **verified public engagement** + quality of the build.
- Awards are judged separately from the internship shortlist.
- You can win an award without getting an internship, and vice versa.

### What They Explicitly Care About
- Sovereign AI narrative (Indian models for Indian users)
- Real impact over flashy tech
- Voice-first experiences that work for people who are more comfortable speaking than typing
- Code-mixing and noisy audio as **features**, not afterthoughts

---

**Bottom line for builders:**  
Show a useful voice agent that Indians would actually want to talk to, running on Gnani’s stack, with strong code-mixed + noisy audio performance, and a clear, short demo.
